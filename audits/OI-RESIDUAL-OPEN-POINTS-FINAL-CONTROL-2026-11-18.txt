2. RL-021.1 — GENERAL H-CHARACTERIZATION

Established:
- In the unrestricted finite relational domain with |X| >= 2, the complete marginal boundary degree profile D_B does not identify Q_B.
- The joint-incidence statistic T_B = sum_x o_B(x) i_B(x) is sufficient for the binary target: Q_B = 1 iff T_B > 0.
- On G_forward, Q_B = 1 iff D_out > 0.
- On G_backward, Q_B = 1 iff D_in > 0.
- For |X| = 1, Q_B = 1 iff D_out > 0 and D_in > 0.
- C_forward is not necessary for identification from the full D_B.

Open:
A general necessary-and-sufficient structural characterization for arbitrary H remains OPEN.

Reason:
The definition of G_H alone does not fix enough comparison structure to justify an unrestricted necessity theorem. Degenerate H can make Q_B constant and therefore identifiable, even when one-sided incidence states are allowed. Any stronger theorem must explicitly fix:
(a) whether B and X are fixed or vary;
(b) the cardinalities of B and X;
(c) the permitted state set H and its realizability constraints;
(d) whether the observation is D_B alone or (R_B^int,D_B);
(e) the exact quantifiers over graphs in G_H.

Safe exact criterion:
D_B identifies Q_B on G_H iff Q_B is constant on every fiber of the observation map D_B restricted to G_H.
This is an exact fiber criterion, not yet the desired structural classification of H.

Disposition:
KEEP OPEN. Do not claim C_forward, C_backward, or their conjunction is a complete characterization for arbitrary H. A further theorem requires a separately stated nondegeneracy/comparison class and proof.

3. RL-027 — CORE MATHEMATICAL RESULTS

The later core audit OI-RL-027-CORE-MINIMALITY-STABILITY-COMPUTABILITY-AUDIT-2026-11-18 was inspected. Its mathematical conclusions are accepted within the finite-dimensional controlled-copy scope.

3.1 Pure global state domain — PASS
For a pure bipartite state, Schmidt decomposition gives:
Q_A = Tr(rho_A^2) = Tr(rho_B^2).
The recovery map is F(rho_B) = Tr(rho_B^2).
No equality of d_A and d_B is required.

3.2 Full state domain — PASS
For fixed rho_B = sigma and product states rho_AB = tau_A tensor sigma:
Q_A = Tr(tau_A^2).
For finite d_A >= 2, the attainable range is:
[1/d_A, 1].
Therefore Q_A is not identifiable from rho_B alone on the full state domain.

3.3 Essential assumptions and minimality — BOUNDED PASS
d_A >= 2 is essential for the non-identifiability claim.
Finite d_A is needed for the exact lower endpoint 1/d_A, but not for a rank-two counterexample.
Finite d_B is not essential to the product-state counterexample, provided a density operator on H_B exists.
This is an assumption-cost audit of the stated claims, not a proof of globally minimal axioms for all of TEST-I.

3.4 Stability — PASS WITH STATED NORM
For density operators rho and sigma:
|Tr(rho^2) - Tr(sigma^2)| <= 2 ||rho-sigma||_1.
By contractivity of trace norm under partial trace:
|Q_A(rho_AB) - Q_A(sigma_AB)| <= 2 ||rho_AB-sigma_AB||_1.
On the pure-state domain, F(rho_B)=Tr(rho_B^2) obeys the corresponding marginal bound.
This proves continuity/stability of the purity functional. It does not create an inverse on the full domain, where identifiability fails.

3.5 Computability — PASS WITH INPUT-REPRESENTATION LIMITS
In finite dimension, Tr(rho^2) is computable by finite matrix arithmetic for exact rational/algebraic entries and to arbitrary requested precision for effectively approximable entries.
The pure-domain recovery map is computable under those representations.
No general complexity bound or exact equality decision procedure for arbitrary computable reals is established.
No algorithm can recover Q_A from rho_B alone on the full domain when d_A >= 2, because the target is not identifiable.

3.6 Terminology correction — REQUIRED FOR FUTURE EDITING

4. THEOREM 91 — MATHEMATICAL AND RELATIONAL BOUNDARY

4.1 Algebraic theorem — PASS CONDITIONALLY
With a common map tau:L_op -> L_op for all models in the comparison class and:
Resp_(T(M))(a) = Resp_M(tau(a)),
the operational response distance is non-expansive:
D_L(T(M1),T(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2).
The proof needs a common operational domain, well-defined response maps, and the same tau for both compared models. Surjectivity of tau is not needed for non-expansion.

4.2 Quantum realization — PASS AS STANDARD-THEORY CONDITIONAL REALIZATION
A finite-dimensional CPTP channel E and its Heisenberg adjoint E* provide a common pullback on measurement effects. This realizes the response-map covariance and reproduces trace-distance contractivity.
It is a valid application in standard quantum theory, not a derivation of Hilbert space, the Born rule, CPTP dynamics, or a new OMEGA-ISOP prediction from the Frozen Core.

4.3 Full relational realization — OPEN
The response-map construction does not, by itself, complete the architectural H1 requirement.
A full relational realization must specify, without changing the Frozen Core:
(a) the concrete relational structure R in the selected realization;
(b) the state/model space on which R is defined;
(c) the precise transformation T;
(d) the exact preservation condition for R taken from the controlling source;
(e) a proof that the selected T satisfies that condition;
(f) the compatibility of this same T with the operational response construction.

Until these are supplied and checked, describe the result as the algebraic response-distance theorem plus a conditional quantum response-map realization, not as a complete realization of the entire relational architecture.

A2 — Uniform attraction:
Not derivable from R-invariance or Theorem 91 non-expansion alone. A sufficient theorem requires a specified complete metric space, a uniform strict contraction q < 1, a common fixed point for the whole family, and a bounded initial set.

A3 — Equicontinuity:
The target must be disambiguated.
- For iterates on the model-space metric quotient, A1 can imply equicontinuity conditionally if D_L is finite on the class, the quotient is well-defined, the class is invariant under T, and the same covariance hypothesis iterates.

A4 — Common anchor:
A common operational anchor response and a common dynamical fixed point are different statements. The target definition and comparison class remain to be fixed.

Disposition:
R-invariance alone establishes none of A1–A4. Existing sufficient-condition and countermodel records remain conditional. Do not promote any of these results to unconditional Frozen Core consequences.
