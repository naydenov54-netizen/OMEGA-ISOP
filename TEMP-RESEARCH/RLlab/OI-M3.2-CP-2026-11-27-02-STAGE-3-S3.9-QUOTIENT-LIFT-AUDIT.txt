3. FORMAL SETUP
============================================================

Let A=(S_A,#_A), B=(S_B,#_B), and C=(S_C,#_C) be Boolean apartness structures.

Assume:
A1: NOT(x # x).
A2: x # y IFF y # x.
A3: x # y IMPLIES (x # z) OR (z # y), for every z.

Define:
x ~_A y IFF NOT(x #_A y),
and analogously for B and C.

Under A1-A3 and classical Boolean logic, ~_A, ~_B, and ~_C are equivalence relations.

Let f:C->A and g:C->B be injective apartness embeddings:
x #_C y IFF f(x) #_A f(y),
x #_C y IFF g(x) #_B g(y).

Let:
Q_A = S_A/~_A,
Q_B = S_B/~_B,
Q_C = S_C/~_C.

The induced maps:
f_bar:Q_C->Q_A,
g_bar:Q_C->Q_B
are injective.

Let Q be the pushout of sets Q_A and Q_B over Q_C, with canonical injections:
j_A:Q_A->Q,
j_B:Q_B->Q.

Let S_P be the pushout of underlying sets S_A and S_B over S_C, with canonical injections:
i_A:S_A->S_P,
i_B:S_B->S_P.

Their images intersect exactly in the common image of C.

Define pi_P:S_P->Q by:
pi_P(i_A(a)) = j_A([a]_A),
pi_P(i_B(b)) = j_B([b]_B).

This map is well-defined because the two expressions agree on identified elements from C.

Define:
x #_P y IFF pi_P(x) != pi_P(y).

============================================================
4. S3.9.1 — P2.3 QUOTIENT-LEVEL INJECTIVITY
============================================================

CLAIM:
The induced maps f_bar and g_bar are injective.

PROOF:
Suppose f_bar([c1]_C)=f_bar([c2]_C).
Then [f(c1)]_A=[f(c2)]_A.
Therefore NOT(f(c1) #_A f(c2)).
Since f preserves and reflects apartness:
NOT(c1 #_C c2).
Thus [c1]_C=[c2]_C.
The proof for g_bar is identical.

RESULT:
ESTABLISHED UNDER THE EXPLICIT APARTNESS-EMBEDDING ASSUMPTIONS.

============================================================
5. S3.9.2 — EXACT INTERSECTION IN THE SET PUSHOUTS
============================================================

For a pushout of sets along injective maps, the canonical maps from each side are injective and their images intersect exactly in the image of the common set.

Applying this fact first to Q_A <- Q_C -> Q_B and then to S_A <- S_C -> S_B gives:

j_A(Q_A) intersect j_B(Q_B) = j_A(f_bar(Q_C)) = j_B(g_bar(Q_C)),

and:

i_A(S_A) intersect i_B(S_B) = i_A(f(S_C)) = i_B(g(S_C)).

RESULT:
ESTABLISHED AS A SET-THEORETIC PROPERTY OF THE TWO PUSHOUTS.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION:
The common part in Q is a quotient-level common part. A quotient class in that common part may have representatives in S_A or S_B that are not themselves elements of the original common substructure C.

============================================================
6. S3.9.3 — DESCENT OF SIDE RELATIONS
============================================================

Let R_A and R_B be n-ary relations on S_A and S_B, agreeing on C and preserved and reflected by f and g.

For R_A to descend to a well-defined relation on Q_A, the exact condition is coordinatewise invariance:

(x1~_A y1 AND ... AND xn~_A yn)
IMPLIES
(R_A(x1,...,xn) IFF R_A(y1,...,yn)).

The analogous condition applies to R_B and R_C.

Under this condition define:
Rbar_A([x1]_A,...,[xn]_A) IFF R_A(x1,...,xn).

This is well-defined. The same applies to B and C.

Because the original embeddings preserve and reflect R and the quotient relations are well-defined, the induced quotient maps preserve and reflect the descended relations.

RESULT:
ESTABLISHED FOR EACH SIDE, PROVIDED COORDINATEWISE INVARIANCE AND AGREEMENT ON C HOLD.

NON-IMPLICATION:
This proves only that each side relation descends. It does not prove that the free relational amalgam on S_P descends through pi_P.

============================================================
7. S3.9.4 — COUNTEREXAMPLE: QUOTIENT LIFT CREATES MIXED TUPLES
============================================================

This is the decisive obstruction.

Take:
S_C={c}
S_A={c,a}
S_B={c,b}

Let all apartness relations be empty:
#_A = #_B = #_C = empty.

Then every pair of elements in each structure is equivalent under ~.

All quotient sets are singletons:
Q_A={q_A},
Q_B={q_B},
Q_C={q_C}.

Their set pushout Q is also a singleton:
Q={q}.

The underlying set pushout is:
S_P={c,a,b}.

The map pi_P sends all three elements to q:
pi_P(c)=pi_P(a)=pi_P(b)=q.

Now use a binary relation R.

Define:
R_A = S_A x S_A,
R_B = S_B x S_B,
R_C = {(c,c)}.

Thus R_A and R_B are universal binary relations on their respective carriers and agree on C.

All three relations are coordinatewise invariant under their respective equivalence relations, because each relation is universal on its carrier.

The descended quotient relations are:
Rbar_A={(q_A,q_A)},
Rbar_B={(q_B,q_B)},
Rbar_C={(q_C,q_C)}.

The free relational amalgam on Q has:
Rbar_Q={(q,q)}.

Pulling this quotient relation back along pi_P gives:
R_P^quot = S_P x S_P.

In particular:
R_P^quot(a,b)=TRUE,
R_P^quot(b,a)=TRUE.

But the free relational amalgam on the underlying set pushout is:
R_P^free = R_A union R_B,

so:
R_P^free =
{(c,c),(c,a),(a,c),(a,a),(c,b),(b,c),(b,b)}.

Therefore:
(a,b) NOT IN R_P^free,
(b,a) NOT IN R_P^free.

Hence:
R_P^quot != R_P^free.

The quotient pullback creates mixed tuples that are absent from the free relational amalgam.

EVIDENCE_CLASS:
EXPLICIT FINITE COUNTEREXAMPLE WITH DIRECT VERIFICATION.

SCOPE:
This counterexample does not refute P2.3 as an apartness strong amalgamation construction. It refutes the inference that P2.3 automatically lifts every quotient-level relational amalgam to the free relational amalgam on S_P.

It also does not refute P2.4.1. P2.4.1 constructs the free relational expansion directly on S_P. The example shows that this free expansion need not be invariant under the equivalence relation induced by pi_P, and therefore need not be the pullback of a relation on Q.

============================================================
8. S3.9.5 — EXACT LIFT-COMPATIBILITY CRITERION
============================================================

Let R_P^free be the free union relation on S_P:
R_P^free = i_A(R_A) union i_B(R_B),
where each side relation is transported to the corresponding tuples in S_P.

Let Rbar_Q be the quotient-level relation on Q, obtained by the free union of the descended side relations.

The quotient pullback is:
R_P^pull(x1,...,xn)
IFF
Rbar_Q(pi_P(x1),...,pi_P(xn)).

The side descent assumptions guarantee that the pullback restricts to the original relations on A and B.

However:
R_P^pull = R_P^free
holds if and only if R_P^free is saturated under the fibers of pi_P.

Explicitly, the necessary-and-sufficient condition is:

For all x1,...,xn,y1,...,yn in S_P,

(pi_P(x1)=pi_P(y1) AND ... AND pi_P(xn)=pi_P(yn))
IMPLIES
(R_P^free(x1,...,xn) IFF R_P^free(y1,...,yn)).

Call this the FIBER-SATURATION CONDITION.

Proof:
If R_P^free is the pullback of a relation on Q, its truth value is constant on every coordinatewise product of pi_P-fibers. Hence fiber saturation is necessary.

Conversely, if R_P^free is fiber-saturated, define a relation on Q by declaring a tuple of quotient classes to belong exactly when one, equivalently every, tuple of representatives belongs to R_P^free. Fiber saturation makes this definition well-defined, and its pullback is exactly R_P^free.

Therefore fiber saturation is necessary and sufficient for the free union relation to descend through pi_P without changing the underlying relation.

The counterexample in Section 7 violates this condition:
pi_P(a)=pi_P(b), but
R_P^free(a,a)=TRUE,
R_P^free(a,b)=FALSE.

RESULT:
A PRECISE NECESSARY-AND-SUFFICIENT LIFT-COMPATIBILITY CRITERION HAS BEEN DERIVED FOR EACH RELATION.

LIMIT:
This criterion is relation-specific. It is not automatically guaranteed by P2.3, by sidewise coordinatewise invariance, or by exact intersection of the quotient pushouts.

============================================================
9. S3.9.6 — SUFFICIENT STRUCTURAL CONDITION AND STATUS OF S3.8-T1
============================================================

A useful sufficient condition for avoiding cross-side fiber mixing is:

Every pi_P-fiber that meets both i_A(S_A) and i_B(S_B) is contained in the common image i_A(f(S_C))=i_B(g(S_C)).

In words:
No quotient class shared by the two sides may contain a representative outside the actual common substructure on either side.

Under this condition, a tuple from one side cannot acquire a representative from the opposite non-common part by changing representatives inside its pi_P-fibers. Together with sidewise coordinatewise invariance, this is sufficient for the free union relation to be fiber-saturated.

This structural condition is SUFFICIENT, not claimed necessary. The exact necessary-and-sufficient condition remains relation-specific fiber saturation from Section 8.

S3.8-T1 STATUS:
The abstract preservation argument for a class K in a quotient relational strong amalgam remains valid under its stated assumptions on the quotient structures.

However, applying that lemma to Q does not by itself prove that the lifted structure on S_P belongs to K. The missing implication is precisely the lift-compatibility condition audited here.

Therefore:
- S3.8-T1 may be used at quotient level when its assumptions are verified.
- Its conclusion must not be transferred automatically to the underlying set pushout.
- For transfer to S_P, verify fiber saturation for every relevant relation, or use a stronger sufficient structural condition.
- P2.3 alone does not establish those additional conditions.

10. LOOP AUDIT
============================================================

TARGET C:
The quotient-based relational construction on Q, when pulled back to S_P, coincides with the free relational amalgam on S_P.

DEPENDENCIES:
1. Apartness axioms A1-A3 and classical Boolean logic.
2. Apartness embeddings f and g.
3. Set pushout constructions on quotient carriers and underlying carriers.
4. Coordinatewise invariance of each side relation.
5. Free union of the quotient-level relations.
6. Pullback along pi_P.

CONCLUSION-SMUGGLING CHECK:
No premise among 1-5 implies that the free union on S_P is saturated under pi_P-fibers. The finite counterexample in Section 7 satisfies the premises and falsifies C.

RESULT:
The unrestricted target C is FALSE.

CORRECTED TARGET:
The quotient pullback equals the free union on S_P if and only if the free union is fiber-saturated under pi_P.

This corrected statement is proved directly in Section 8 and does not assume the desired equality as an independent premise.

12. ESTABLISHED RESULTS, LIMITATIONS, AND NON-IMPLICATIONS
============================================================

ESTABLISHED WITHIN THIS AUDIT:
1. The stated P2.3 set-pushout construction satisfies the required injectivity and exact-intersection properties under its explicit assumptions.
2. Sidewise coordinatewise invariance is sufficient and necessary for each relation to descend to its side quotient.
3. Quotient pullback can create mixed tuples absent from the free relational amalgam on S_P.
4. Fiber saturation is necessary and sufficient for equality between the free union on S_P and the pullback of the quotient relation.
5. A structural condition preventing non-common representatives in shared quotient fibers is sufficient for fiber saturation.

NOT ESTABLISHED:
1. That every P2.3 instance fails relational lifting.
2. That every relation or every constraint class fails under quotient lifting.
3. That fiber saturation follows from the current Frozen Core.
4. That the original P2.3 manuscript has been independently source-compared.
5. That a particular OMEGA-ISOP relational constraint class satisfies fiber saturation.
6. That any physical, dynamical, or empirical conclusion follows.

NON-IMPLICATIONS:
P2.3 apartness strong amalgamation does not imply free relational amalgamation after quotient pullback.
Sidewise quotient descent does not imply global free-union descent.
Exact intersection of quotient pushout images does not imply that shared quotient fibers contain only representatives from the actual common substructure.
Quotient-level preservation of a hereditary relational class does not automatically imply preservation by its pullback to S_P.
