ITEM 1 — OBJECT ADMISSIBILITY Adm(C)

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, III.4:
C=(S_C,#_C), S_C subseteq U0, with #_C the restriction of the ambient apartness relation. It states |S_C| >= 2 and explicitly says exact admissibility conditions remain realization-dependent.

Volume III, Section 6:
The configuration nontriviality test requires |S_C| >= 2 and an actual apartness witness x,y in S_C with x #_C y.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
PARTIALLY SPECIFIED; COMPLETE PREDICATE EXPLICITLY LEFT REALIZATION-DEPENDENT.

SOURCE FACT:
The minimal nontriviality conditions are stated. They are not declared to be the complete admissibility predicate.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Is the Volume III nontriviality test sufficient for the object class used in D_CC, or must Adm(C) include additional realization-dependent conditions? If additional conditions apply, what is their exact list and domain?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
A single explicit predicate Adm(C), including all quantifiers, ambient dependencies, and any realization parameter. Each clause must be classified as necessary, sufficient, or both.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Show that the declared objects satisfy the predicate.
- For every selected morphism f:C -> D, prove any required admissibility invariance or closure property.
- For every proposed amalgam construction, prove its target satisfies Adm.
- If Adm depends on realization data, state whether that data is part of the object.

DO NOT INFER:
Adm(C)=Adm_min(C), or union closure of a stronger Adm_real(C), merely from the nontriviality test.

ITEM 2 — MORPHISM CLASS Hom_C_METACORE

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, III.5 defines an embedding f:C1 -> C2 as injective and satisfying:
x #1 y iff f(x) #2 f(y).

Volume II, III.4 describes C_METACORE morphisms as preserving selected structural properties.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
PARTIALLY SPECIFIED WITH A GENUINE CATEGORY-LEVEL AMBIGUITY.

SOURCE FACT:
The local embedding template is explicit. The category-level morphism class is not equated explicitly with the set of all such embeddings. The selected structural properties are not exhaustively listed.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Are all source-defined local apartness embeddings the morphisms of C_METACORE, or is Hom restricted by additional explicitly named conditions?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
A typed hom-set definition:
Hom_C_METACORE(C,D)=...
with domain, codomain, injectivity, preservation/reflection requirements, and every additional restriction stated.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Identity belongs to every endomorphism set.
- Composition of composable morphisms remains a morphism.
- Associativity and identity laws are inherited from function composition.
- If a restricted predicate P(f) is used, prove P(id_C) and composition closure.
- Prove that every selected structural property is preserved/reflected as required.

EXISTING RESULT:
S2-04C-15 proves identity and composition closure for the full class of apartness embeddings on any fixed, already specified object class. It does not prove that this is the project's exhaustive hom-class.

DO NOT INFER:
That a local embedding definition automatically settles the entire category-level morphism class.

ITEM 3 — ADDITIONAL STRUCTURE AND SIGNATURE

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, III.5 explicitly selects apartness in the embedding biconditional. Volume II, III.4 also refers to morphisms preserving selected structural properties.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
PARTIALLY SPECIFIED; THE COMPLETE SIGNATURE AND PRESERVATION POLICY ARE UNSPECIFIED.

SOURCE FACT:
Apartness is selected. The phrase "selected structural properties" leaves open whether further structure is part of the categorical objects.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Is apartness the only categorical structure at this stage, or are additional relations, operations, constants, distinguished subsets, boundary data, or realization data part of C_METACORE?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
A complete typed signature and a preservation/reflection table for every symbol:
- preserve;
- reflect;
- preserve and reflect;
- preserve only under stated conditions;
- not part of the categorical structure.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Define each symbol on each object.
- Define the action of every morphism on each symbol.
- Prove identity and composition closure for the complete signature.
- Reassess admissibility invariance under the complete morphism definition.

DO NOT INFER:
That apartness-only preservation suffices if additional selected structure is later declared.

ITEM 4 — CARRIER CONVENTION

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, III.4 represents local configuration carriers as subsets of U0 with induced apartness.
Volume II, III.5 defines embeddings as maps, not necessarily literal inclusions.
The inspected amalgamation and colimit passages do not specify the carrier type of every amalgam.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
OBJECT REPRESENTATION SPECIFIED; AMALGAM CARRIER CONVENTION SILENT.

SOURCE FACT:
Local configurations use subsets of U0. The local embedding is a function and is not defined as literal inclusion only.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Must every amalgam object also be an induced subconfiguration of the same U0, or may an amalgam have an abstract carrier, subject to an explicit realization condition?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
A declaration covering:
- ordinary objects;
- amalgam targets;
- colimit objects;
- whether all use the same U0;
- whether abstract carriers require realization maps into U0.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Fixed-carrier option: prove the proposed target is a subset of U0 and carries the required induced structure.
- Abstract-carrier option: define its structure and maps without relying on ambient element identity.
- Realization-mediated option: prove the realization map satisfies the declared admissibility and structural constraints.

DO NOT INFER:
That the local subset representation forces every colimit carrier to be a subset of U0.

ITEM 5 — COMPATIBILITY INPUT: PAIR OR SPAN

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, IV.1:
CC(C1,C2)=1 iff an admissible amalgamation of C1 and C2 exists; otherwise CC(C1,C2)=0.
The same passage says the binary form is a minimal representation and is not claimed to be the unique possible representation.

Volume II, IV.2 calls D_CC the diagram generated by selected configurations and admissible compatibility relations.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
EXPLICIT BINARY NOTATION; INPUT SEMANTICS UNDERDETERMINED.

SOURCE FACT:
The notation is binary. The source does not specify a common base object or two maps from that base.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Does CC(C1,C2) mean existence of an admissible common target for the pair, or does it abbreviate amalgamation over a designated common base and two specified maps?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
Choose and define exactly one input type for the relevant construction:
PAIR:
CC_pair(C1,C2), with a precise common-target witness;
or SPAN:
C0 --f1--> C1 and C0 --f2--> C2, with an explicit agreement equation in the target.

If a pair-based input is retained, specify how its witnesses generate the objects and arrows of D_CC.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Define the witnesses in the chosen category.
- Show that the compatibility relation is well-defined for the selected objects and morphisms.
- If span-based, verify j1 o f1 = j2 o f2.
- If pair-based, do not claim agreement over a common base unless such a base and maps are supplied.

MATHEMATICAL RELATION:
For a fixed span, a span amalgam gives a common target for its two upper objects. The converse does not follow in general: embeddings of C1 and C2 into a common target need not agree on the images of a specified C0.

DO NOT INFER:
That a binary predicate alone specifies a span or a categorical diagram.

ITEM 6 — AMALGAM ADMISSIBILITY

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, IV.1 uses the phrase "admissible amalgamation".
The inspected passage does not give a complete predicate for admissibility of the amalgam object or the full witness data.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
TERM EXPLICITLY USED; ITS COMPLETE FORMAL DEFINITION IS SILENT.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
What exact conditions make a common target an admissible amalgam, beyond mere existence of maps from C1 and C2?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
A formal witness definition specifying:
- target object M;
- proof that Adm(M) holds;
- maps j1:C1 -> M and j2:C2 -> M in the chosen hom-class;
- the span agreement equation, if applicable;
- carrier and additional-structure constraints.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Prove target admissibility.
- Prove both maps are valid morphisms.
- Prove the required agreement equation.
- If a pushout is claimed, prove the full universal property, not only existence of an amalgam.
- If union or quotient is used, prove the construction satisfies the exact target predicate.

DO NOT INFER:
Common target = admissible amalgam = pushout. These are distinct claims.

ITEM 7 — INDEXING CATEGORY J AND FUNCTOR D

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume III, Section 9 explicitly requires an indexing category J and a functor D:J -> C_METACORE, including relevant objects, morphisms, compatibility conditions, and universal property.
Volume II, IV.2 describes D_CC as a diagram generated by selected configurations and admissible compatibility relations.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
REQUIREMENT EXPLICIT; PROJECT-SPECIFIC DATA NOT PROVIDED.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
What is the intended indexing category J for D_CC: one span, a family of spans, a category generated by compatibility witnesses, or another specified diagram?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
An explicit presentation of J and D, including:
- objects of J;
- generating arrows;
- identity arrows;
- composition rules;
- object assignment;
- arrow assignment;
- proof that D preserves identities and composition.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
For every object j:
D(id_j)=id_D(j).

For every composable pair alpha:j -> k and beta:k -> l:
D(beta o alpha)=D(beta) o D(alpha).

All assigned arrows must be valid morphisms in the chosen C_METACORE.

DO NOT INFER:
That a graph of Boolean CC values is already a category or a functor.

ITEM 8 — COLIMIT COCONE AND UNIVERSAL PROPERTY

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE:
Volume II, IV.2-IV.3 makes R := colim(D_CC) conditional on a valid compatibility diagram, the relevant categorical structure, and existence of the required colimit or equivalent constraint-preserving construction.
Volume III, Sections 8-9 explicitly requires the universal property.

GAP CLASSIFICATION:
CONDITIONAL REQUIREMENT EXPLICIT; PROJECT DIAGRAM AND PROOF NOT SUPPLIED.

SMALLEST PROJECT-OWNER QUESTION:
Which exact diagram D:J -> C_METACORE and which candidate cocone are intended as the input to the project-level colimit claim?

CLOSURE EVIDENCE:
Provide a candidate object R and cocone maps:
eta_j:D(j) -> R
such that for every arrow alpha:j -> k:
eta_k o D(alpha)=eta_j.

Then prove that for every object X and every compatible cocone gamma_j:D(j) -> X, there exists exactly one morphism u:R -> X satisfying:
u o eta_j = gamma_j
for every j in J.

PROOF OBLIGATIONS:
- Quantify over all objects X in the selected category.
- Quantify over all compatible cocones in the selected hom-class.
- Prove existence of u.
- Prove uniqueness of u.
- Check R is an object under the exact Adm predicate and carrier convention.

DO NOT INFER:
A common target, a union construction, a binary pushout, or one successful realization automatically proves the required project-level colimit.

3. DEPENDENCY AND CIRCULARITY CHECK
============================================================

The eight decisions are not independent in their proof obligations. Their minimum dependency order is:

1. Object representation and exact Adm(C).
2. Complete signature and exact Hom_C_METACORE.
3. Category laws and compatibility of morphisms with object admissibility.
4. Carrier convention for objects and amalgams.
5. Pair-based or span-based compatibility input.
6. Exact amalgam witness and target admissibility.
7. Indexing category J and functor D.
8. Cocone and colimit universal property.

IMPORTANT DEPENDENCY:
The phrase "admissible amalgamation" already presupposes an object class, morphism class, and target admissibility criterion. Therefore CC cannot be treated as a fully operational primitive Boolean predicate while those ingredients remain undefined.

A pair-based common-target predicate is not independent of C_METACORE: its witnesses still require valid objects and valid morphisms in the selected category.

A span-based amalgamation predicate likewise presupposes a specified span in that category.

This is not a contradiction in the source. It is a dependency that must be made explicit in the project specification.

4. AUDIT FINDINGS AND STATUS CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

FACTS:
- Volume II III.4 fixes the local subset representation and explicitly leaves exact admissibility realization-dependent.
- Volume II III.5 defines a local embedding by injectivity and preservation/reflection of apartness.
- Volume II III.4 refers to selected structural properties for category morphisms.
- Volume II IV.1 gives binary CC notation and expressly calls it a minimal, non-unique representation.
- Volume II IV.2-IV.3 makes the colimit conditional.
- Volume III Section 9 explicitly requires J, D, objects, arrows, compatibility conditions, and the universal property.
- Volume III Section 6 supplies a nontriviality test, not a complete realization-independent Adm predicate.

HYPOTHESES / CANDIDATE DEFINITIONS:
- Adm_min and Adm_real.
- All-apartness-embeddings or a restricted Hom_P.
- Apartness-only or enriched categorical signature.
- Fixed-U0, abstract-carrier, or realization-mediated amalgams.
- Pair-based or span-based compatibility.
- Any proposed amalgam witness and any proposed J,D.

These remain candidates, not source-established project decisions.

FORMAL RESULTS:
- The all-apartness-embeddings class is closed under identities and composition on any fixed object class.
- The minimal nontriviality test is invariant under apartness embeddings.
- The union pushout theorem in P_min is valid only within its declared inclusion-only test category and minimal object class.
- The finite no-pushout result in E_F is scoped to its stated fixed-carrier all-embeddings category and specified span.

COMPUTATIONAL RESULTS:
- None introduced by this source-to-specification review.

AUDIT FINDINGS:
- Eight specification gaps remain.
- Some are explicit source-level underdeterminations; others are formal requirements whose project-specific data have not been supplied.
- No gap can be closed by choosing the category that makes a desired colimit easiest to prove.
- The binary CC notation does not eliminate the need to specify witness data.
- "Admissible amalgam" must not be conflated with "common target" or "pushout".
- A project-level colimit claim must be tested against all compatible cocones in the exact selected category.

INTERPRETATIONS:
- The source supports a local apartness-embedding template, but not an exhaustive C_METACORE hom-set.
- The source's binary CC form is intentionally minimal and non-unique, so further project specification is required rather than inferred.
- The Volume III requirement for a full diagram and universal property is explicit and cannot be replaced by a Boolean compatibility matrix.

5. EVIDENCE REQUIRED TO CLOSE THE GAPS
============================================================

A gap is closed only when the project record contains:
1. An exact, typed definition or explicit source passage.
2. The domain and quantifiers of the definition.
3. Its relation to the authoritative theory text.
4. All new assumptions clearly marked as assumptions or approved choices.
5. The mathematical proof obligations generated by that choice.
6. A review showing no contradiction with the other seven decisions.
7. Explicit project-owner approval wherever the choice is not already fixed by an authoritative source.

An audit record can document a source fact or a candidate definition. It cannot manufacture project-owner approval.

No change to Frozen Core or protected theory volumes is needed merely to close an audit question. Any later theory amendment would require its own explicit authorization and controlled procedure.
