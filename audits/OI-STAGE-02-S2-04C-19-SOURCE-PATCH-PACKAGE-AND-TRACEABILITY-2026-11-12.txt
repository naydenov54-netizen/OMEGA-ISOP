5. PATCH F-03 — EXPLICIT LEMMA UP
============================================================

Finding:
F-03 — MINOR — ACCEPTED; EXPLICIT LEMMA REQUIRED.

Proposed insertion text:

"LEMMA UP — UPWARD CLOSURE OF Adm

Let U0 be a fixed carrier. Let Adm be defined by:
(i) |C| >= 2;
(ii) there exist x,y in C such that x#y, where # is the induced relation.

If:
C in Adm and C subseteq C' subseteq U0,

then:
C' in Adm.

Proof:
Since C in Adm, |C| >= 2 and there exist x,y in C with x#y.
Since C subseteq C', we have |C'| >= |C| >= 2.
Also x,y belong to C'. The induced relation on C' retains the pair x#y.
Therefore C' satisfies both defining conditions of Adm, hence C' in Adm."

Required cross-references:

- Cite Lemma UP explicitly in FR-5 wherever upward closure is invoked.

Traceability:
S2-04C-18 / F-03.

Scope warning:
