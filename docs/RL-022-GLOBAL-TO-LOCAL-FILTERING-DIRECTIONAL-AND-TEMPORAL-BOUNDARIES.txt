CLAIMS

C1. The filtering dependency R_global →_F R_i →_F A_i does not entail the presence of A_i →_U R_global.

C2. The same dependency does not entail the absence of A_i →_U R_global.

C3. The filtering construction is conditionally extendable to S_global(t), provided that A4 holds.

GIVEN STRUCTURES

S_global = (M, Γ, Θ)

Local domain:
D_i ⊂ M

Global relational structure:
R_global

Local relational structure:
R_i ∈ Restrict(R_global, D_i) ∩ Compatible(Θ, D_i)

Local admissible dynamics:
A_i(R_i; Φ_i)

Base CE-V-06 result:
A selected global realization can conditionally filter the class of locally admissible relational structures and thereby constrain downstream organizational possibilities.

DEFINITIONS

D1. X →_F Y (filtering dependency):
Y is constrained by X through Restrict ∩ Compatible, or through the A_i(R_i; Φ_i) dependence.

D2. X →_U Y (update dependency):
In the given discrete-time model, Y(t+1) depends non-constantly on X(t), i.e. there exist admissible values x1 ≠ x2 such that:
Y(t+1 | X(t)=x1) ≠ Y(t+1 | X(t)=x2).
The dependence may concern the whole state Y or at least one explicitly defined component of Y.

D3. X ↛_U Y := ¬(X →_U Y) in the same model.

D4. P ⊬ Q:
Q is not derivable from P; this is shown by a model satisfying P and ¬Q.

ASSUMPTIONS / CONSTRAINTS

A1. Global and local structures are explicitly defined.
A2. Restriction from the global realization to a local domain is well-defined.
A3. Compatibility conditions are explicitly specified.
A4. Where temporal extension is considered, the relevant temporal structures, restriction operations, compatibility conditions and realization context are well-defined at each relevant t.
A5. The analysis does not assume that global-to-local filtering is the only possible directional dependency.
A6. No universal global dynamics is assumed.
A7. Realization is distinguished from selection.
A8. Admissibility is distinguished from realization.
A9. Local organization is not identified with global realization.

RULE

Independence of a claim Q from P is shown only by two models:
one satisfying P ∧ Q
and one satisfying P ∧ ¬Q.

DERIVATION

6.1 STATIC AND TEMPORAL FORMS

Static:
S_global = (M, Γ, Θ)
D_i ⊂ M
R_i ∈ Restrict(R_global, D_i) ∩ Compatible(Θ, D_i)
A_i = A_i(R_i; Φ_i)

Temporal (under A4):
S_global(t) = (M(t), Γ(t), Θ(t))
D_i(t) ⊂ M(t)
R_i(t) ∈ Restrict(R_global(t), D_i(t)) ∩ Compatible(Θ(t), D_i(t))
A_i(t) = A_i(R_i(t); Φ_i(t))

6.2 TEMPORAL RESULT (C3)

If restriction, compatibility and realization-context operations remain well-defined at each relevant t, the CE-V-06 filtering construction extends to:

Global(t) →_F Local(t) →_F Admissible Dynamics(t)

without requiring a static global realization.

No particular law of global evolution is derived.

6.3 DIRECTIONAL RESULT (C1, C2), MODEL-LEVEL VERIFICATION

Common setting (A4, discrete time):
M = {a, b}
D_1 = {a}
R_global(t) = (x_t, y_t) ∈ {0,1}²
Θ imposes no additional restriction
R_1(t) = x_t
A_1(t) = A_1(R_1(t); Φ_1) = R_1(t)

The filtering chain:
R_global(t) →_F R_1(t) →_F A_1(t)
holds at every t in both models below.

Model K1 (with feedback):
x_{t+1} = 1 − A_1(t)
y_{t+1} = y_t

Therefore:
A_1(t) →_U R_global(t+1)
because the x-component of R_global(t+1) depends non-constantly on A_1(t).

This is:
P ∧ ¬(A_i ↛_U R_global)
the model for C2.

Model K0 (without feedback):
x_{t+1} = x_t
y_{t+1} = y_t

Then R_global(t+1) does not depend on A_1(t), so:
A_1 ↛_U R_global

This is:
P ∧ ¬(A_i →_U R_global)
the model for C1.

Consequences:
CE-V-06 ⊬ (A_i →_U R_global)
CE-V-06 ⊬ (A_i ↛_U R_global)

Hence the global-to-local result can be neither strengthened into a universal prohibition of local-to-global feedback nor into a universal existence of it.

K1 and K0 both satisfy the filtering chain, while the presence of reverse update-dependency differs between them.

VERIFICATION

V1. K1 and K0 satisfy A1–A4 and the filtering chain at every t (checked in 6.3).
V2. K1 and K0 differ only in the update rule of x_t; the filtering chain is identical in both.
V3. In K1, the x-component of R_global(t+1) depends non-constantly on A_1(t), satisfying D2.
V4. In K0, R_global(t+1) is independent of A_1(t), satisfying D3.

FAILURE TESTS

FAILURE TEST A:
Derive:
Global → Local ⊢ Local ↛ Global
Result: FAILED.
Reason: K1.

FAILURE TEST B:
Derive:
Global → Local ⊢ Local → Global
Result: FAILED.
Reason: K0.
In addition, absence of a prohibition does not establish existence of coupling.

FAILURE TEST C:
Treat temporal extension as a universal physical law.
Result: FAILED / NOT DERIVED.
Reason: Temporal applicability requires explicitly defined temporal structures and operations.

FAILURE TEST D:
Treat the selected global realization as uniquely derived.
Result: FAILED / NOT DERIVED.
Reason: Realization and selection remain distinct.

FAILURE TEST E:
Extend the filtering construction to S_global(t) when A4 is violated, i.e. when Restrict, Compatible or realization context is undefined at some t.
Result: NOT APPLICABLE.
Reason: The construction is undefined, not refuted.

FAILURE TEST F:
Read →_U (as in K1) as causation.
Result: FAILED / NOT DERIVED.
Reason: →_U is a model-level functional dependence. Filtering and update-dependency remain distinct from causation.

RESULT

The consolidated RL-022 establishes two compatible boundaries of CE-V-06.

I. DIRECTIONAL BOUNDARY

The validated global-to-local filtering relation entails neither the universal absence nor the universal existence of local-to-global influence.

The directional structure remains open with respect to possible feedback.

Established at the level of logical consistency (existence of models K1 and K0).

II. TEMPORAL BOUNDARY

S_global(t) →_F R_i(t) →_F A_i(t),
provided that the temporal structures, restriction operations, compatibility conditions and realization context are explicitly defined (A4).

No universal global dynamics is derived.

RELATION BETWEEN I AND II

The two boundaries have distinct conclusions (C1–C2 versus C3).

The countermodels of I are formulated in discrete time under A4, so I uses the A4 conditions of II.

FAILURE BOUNDARIES

This RL does NOT establish:
- local-to-global causation;
- absence of local-to-global feedback;
- universal bidirectional coupling;
- universal no-feedback;
- a universal law of global evolution;
- unique global realization;
- unique realization selection;
- physical realization of the temporal extension;
- cosmological necessity of any particular realization;
- automatic local organization from global realization;
- global realization from local organization;
- any feedback law, its stability or convergence.

LIMITATIONS

The result is conditional on the formal structures specified above.
The temporal extension is model-dependent.
The directional result is a logical boundary result, not an empirical claim about physical systems.
K0 and K1 use a trivial Compatible(Θ, ·); the result is an existence result and makes no claim for every Θ, in particular no claim that feedback is compatible with any given non-trivial Θ.
Global realization remains distinct from global selection.
Admissibility remains distinct from realization.
Filtering remains distinct from causation.
Update-dependency remains distinct from causation.
The result does not modify the Frozen Core.
