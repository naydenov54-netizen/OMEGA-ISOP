3. SPAN AND CANDIDATE COCONE
============================================================

The span is:

          f1
     C0 -------> C1
      |
      | f2
      v
     C2

Here f1 and f2 are the literal inclusion maps:

f1(o1)=o1, f1(o2)=o2.
f2(o1)=o1, f2(o2)=o2.

The candidate cocone is:

C1 --j1--> M
C2 --j2--> M

where j1 and j2 are literal inclusions:

j1(o1)=o1, j1(o2)=o2, j1(a)=a.
j2(o1)=o1, j2(o2)=o2, j2(b)=b.

The square commutes:

j1 o f1 = j2 o f2.

Both j1 and j2 are injective and preserve and reflect equivalence.

Verification on C1:
- o1 ~1 a, and j1(o1) ~M j1(a).
- o1 is not equivalent to o2, and j1(o1) is not equivalent to j1(o2).
- The equivalence class {o1,a} is exactly the restriction to C1 of {o1,a,b}.
Thus j1 reflects as well as preserves equivalence.

Verification on C2 is symmetric.

Therefore the displayed square is a valid commutative cocone in C_EQ-EMB.

============================================================
4. UNIVERSAL PROPERTY TEST
============================================================

Question:
Is M a pushout of the span in C_EQ-EMB?

Answer:
NO. The candidate M is not a pushout in C_EQ-EMB.

Construct a test cocone N:

N = ({p,q,r}, ~N)
Equivalence classes:
{p,r}, {q}.

Define:

g1:C1 -> N
g1(o1)=p
g1(o2)=q
g1(a)=r

g2:C2 -> N
g2(o1)=p
g2(o2)=q
g2(b)=r

Check g1:
- It is injective.
- o1 ~1 a iff p ~N r.
- o1 is not equivalent to o2 iff p is not equivalent to q.
- Therefore g1 preserves and reflects equivalence.

Check g2:
- It is injective.
- o1 ~2 b iff p ~N r.
- o1 is not equivalent to o2 iff p is not equivalent to q.
- Therefore g2 preserves and reflects equivalence.

Agreement on C0:
g1 o f1 = g2 o f2,
because both maps send o1 to p and o2 to q.

Thus (g1,g2) is a valid cocone in C_EQ-EMB.

If M were a pushout, there would have to be a unique morphism h:M -> N such that:

h o j1 = g1
h o j2 = g2.

The underlying function is forced:

h(o1)=p
h(o2)=q
h(a)=r
h(b)=r.

But a and b are distinct elements of M, while h(a)=h(b)=r.

Therefore h is not injective.

Hence h is not a morphism in C_EQ-EMB.

The required factorization does not exist in the target category. This contradicts the pushout universal property.

FORMAL CONCLUSION:
M is an amalgam of the span, but M is not its pushout in C_EQ-EMB.

This counterexample disproves the claim that the particular amalgam constructed by the carrier pushout in Set, followed by equivalence closure, is automatically a categorical pushout in the embedding category.

It does NOT prove that no pushout can ever exist for this span in every possible category or under every alternative definition.
