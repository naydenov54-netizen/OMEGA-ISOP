1. QUESTION
Can the available OMEGA-ISOP specification determine a concrete cosmological relational structure R_cosmo, its automorphism group Aut(R_cosmo), and a canonical invariant measure suitable for identifying a Lorentzian spacetime volume measure?

2. REPOSITORY-BOUNDARY FINDING
The available cosmological audit records do not provide a fully instantiated object R_cosmo from which Aut(R_cosmo) can be computed. The cosmological closure record identifies missing ingredients including a complete diagram D_CC_cosmo, its index category and functor, a verified colimit, a specified realization space Real(R_cosmo), a specified realization map Phi_cosmo, and a computable A(R_cosmo;Phi_cosmo).

Consequently, the notation Aut(R_cosmo) is currently a target for definition, not a group established by the current specification. No group action on a specified measurable space has been provided from which existence or uniqueness of an invariant measure could be proved.

3. WHAT MUST BE SPECIFIED BEFORE AUT(R_COSMO) IS WELL-DEFINED
At minimum, a test realization must declare:
A. Carrier: a set or category of events/regions/objects E, with its identity and equality conventions.
B. Signature: each relation and operation on E, including whether the intended causal primitive is a strict chronological relation ≪, a causal relation ≼, an apartness relation, or a different explicitly defined relation. These structures must not be silently identified.
C. Morphisms: the exact structure-preserving bijections or isomorphisms. Define Aut(R) as the group of bijections f:E→E preserving and reflecting every designated relation and operation.
D. Realization status: whether E and its relations are abstract relational data or already arise from a particular manifold and Lorentzian metric. If a metric is used to define the structure, the result must not be described as deriving that metric independently.
E. Measurable structure: a sigma-algebra Σ on E, or a justified route to a measurable spacetime realization (M,Σ). A group action on E alone does not automatically define a useful countably additive measure.
F. Candidate measure class: specify whether the target is a finite measure, probability measure, locally finite measure, Radon measure, or a spacetime volume measure with physical dimensions. State the domain and required regularity.
G. Invariance: require μ(f(A))=μ(A) for every admissible automorphism f and every measurable A for which the expression is defined.
H. Scale/calibration: state whether normalization, a reference region, physical units, or an independently fixed volume is available. Do not conflate dimensionless probability normalization with physical four-volume.
I. Compatibility claim: if μ is to equal dvol_g, specify the metric g, the identification map, and the theorem or independent axiom establishing μ=dvol_g.

4. AUTOMORPHISM-INVARIANCE DOES NOT BY ITSELF FIX ABSOLUTE SCALE
Let (E,Σ) be a measurable space, G act measurably on E, and μ be a G-invariant measure. For any constant c>0, the measure cμ is also G-invariant:
(cμ)(f^{-1}(A)) = c μ(f^{-1}(A)) = c μ(A) = (cμ)(A).
Thus invariance alone cannot select the overall positive normalization whenever a nonzero invariant measure exists and scalar multiples remain admissible. This is a direct mathematical fact, conditional only on the stated definitions.
A probability condition μ(E)=1 can fix the scalar when 0<μ(E)<∞, but it imposes a dimensionless normalization. It does not by itself establish that μ is a Lorentzian volume measure, that μ=dvol_g, or that a physical length scale has been determined.
Existence is also not automatic: some group actions and measure classes admit no nonzero invariant measure of the required kind. Uniqueness is not automatic either; multiple invariant measures may exist, including measures differing in relative weights across invariant components or orbits.

5. CONDITIONAL CAUSAL-GEOMETRY ROUTE, NOT AN OMEGA DERIVATION
A possible external test realization is a sufficiently regular distinguishing Lorentzian spacetime whose chronological relation is defined by x≪y exactly when a future-directed timelike curve connects x to y. Under appropriate hypotheses, causal-order information can determine conformal geometry, rather than the absolute conformal scale. The exact theorem and hypotheses must be stated before relying on this route.
Even if an automorphism group of the chronological structure is thereby represented by conformal automorphisms, that fact does not itself prove existence or uniqueness of a G-invariant measure, nor identify such a measure with dvol_g. A conformal rescaling g' = Ω²g in n dimensions gives dvol_g' = Ω^n dvol_g; in four dimensions the density scales by Ω⁴. A further independent scale-fixing or metric-volume compatibility condition is required.
This route would test a separately specified continuum realization. It would not establish that the OMEGA-ISOP Frozen Core constructs that realization, its measurable structure, its metric, or its measure.
