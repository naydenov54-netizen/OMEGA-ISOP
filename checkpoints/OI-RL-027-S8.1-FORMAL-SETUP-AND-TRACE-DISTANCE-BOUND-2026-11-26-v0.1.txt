==================================================
2. DEFINITIONS
==================================================

Finite-dimensional bipartite system:

G = H_A tensor H_B

rho_AB in D, where D is an explicitly specified admissible domain of bipartite density operators.

rho_A = Tr_B(rho_AB)
rho_B = Tr_A(rho_AB)

Q_A(rho_AB) = Tr(rho_A^2)

Trace distance:

D(rho,sigma) = (1/2)||rho - sigma||_1

where ||.||_1 denotes trace norm.

==================================================
3. FOUR S8 CASES: OPERATIONAL SEPARATION
==================================================

A. EXACT LOCAL EQUALITY

rho_B = rho'_B.

Question: does equality of reduced states imply equality of Q_A on the selected domain D?

Known RL-027 status:
- On the pure-global-state domain, Q_A = Tr(rho_B^2), hence exact identifiability.
- On the full mixed-global-state domain and d_A >= 2, exact identifiability fails.
- For fixed rho_B = sigma in the full mixed domain, the attainable Q_A interval is [1/d_A, 1].

These are existing RL-027 results and are not reproved here.

B. APPROXIMATE LOCAL EQUALITY

D(rho_B, sigma) <= eta, with sigma a fixed reference density operator.

Two distinct questions must be kept separate:
(i) pairwise variation between two admissible global states;
(ii) the full range of Q_A over all admissible global states whose B-marginal lies in the eta-ball around sigma.

On the full mixed domain, product states tau tensor sigma are admissible and have the exact B-marginal sigma while Q_A = Tr(tau^2) spans [1/d_A,1]. Therefore local uncertainty alone does not narrow this interval, even at eta = 0.

On the pure-global-state domain, Q_A = Tr(rho_B^2). Thus the uncertainty range reduces to purity extrema of rho_B over the trace-distance ball around sigma. S7 spectral extrema are relevant here, conditional on independent validation of the S7 derivation and implementation.

C. GLOBAL PERTURBATION

Operational meaning adopted for this subcase:

D(rho_AB, rho'_AB) <= epsilon.

This assumption is stronger and different from approximate equality of B-marginals. It yields a general bound on Q_A, derived in Section 4.

D. CORRELATED GLOBAL STATES

The word "correlated" alone does not define a mathematical domain or constraint. A future subcase must specify what is fixed or bounded, e.g. global purity, mutual information, a correlation operator, Schmidt data for pure states, or another explicit invariant/constraint.

No general theorem for this case is claimed in this checkpoint.

==================================================
4. ANALYTICAL RESULT: GLOBAL TRACE-DISTANCE LIPSCHITZ BOUND
==================================================

PROPOSITION:

For any finite-dimensional bipartite density operators rho_AB and rho'_AB,

|Q_A(rho_AB) - Q_A(rho'_AB)|
<= 2 D(rho_AB, rho'_AB).

PROOF:

Partial trace is a completely positive trace-preserving map, so trace-distance contractivity gives:

D(rho_A, rho'_A) <= D(rho_AB, rho'_AB).

For arbitrary density operators rho and sigma, write:

Tr(rho^2) - Tr(sigma^2)
= Tr[(rho - sigma)rho] + Tr[sigma(rho - sigma)].

Let X = rho - sigma. Then X is Hermitian and traceless. For every effect E satisfying 0 <= E <= I:

|Tr(XE)| <= (1/2)||X||_1 = D(rho,sigma).

Both rho and sigma are effects, since they are density operators and hence 0 <= rho,sigma <= I. Applying the inequality to E = rho and E = sigma, and using the triangle inequality:

|Tr(rho^2) - Tr(sigma^2)| <= 2D(rho,sigma).

Set rho = rho_A and sigma = rho'_A, then use contractivity:

|Q_A(rho_AB) - Q_A(rho'_AB)|
<= 2D(rho_A,rho'_A)
<= 2D(rho_AB,rho'_AB).

QED.

STATUS:
Analytical derivation established in this working checkpoint. The constant 2 is a valid general upper-bound constant. Optimality/tightness of the constant has NOT been established.

==================================================
5. DOMAIN-RELATIVE UNCERTAINTY WIDTH
==================================================

Define the set of admissible Q_A values for a reference B-marginal sigma and local uncertainty radius eta:

U_Q(D; sigma, eta)
= { Q_A(rho_AB) :
    rho_AB in D,
    D(rho_B, sigma) <= eta }.

Define its width:

W_Q(D; sigma, eta)
= sup U_Q(D; sigma, eta) - inf U_Q(D; sigma, eta).

Interpretation:
- W_Q(D; sigma, 0) = 0 is exact identifiability on the fibre over sigma, provided the fibre is nonempty.
- W_Q(D; sigma, eta) measures the total Q_A uncertainty allowed by local information with radius eta.
- lim_{eta -> 0+} W_Q(D; sigma, eta) = 0 expresses local stability at sigma.
- Uniform stability requires the corresponding convergence uniformly over the relevant admissible reference marginals.

This is a definition / formal diagnostic, not a claim that stability has been established for RL-027.

For the full mixed domain and d_A >= 2, product states tau tensor sigma show:

W_Q(D_all; sigma, 0) = 1 - 1/d_A.

Thus the width does not vanish as eta approaches zero on the full mixed domain.

For the pure-global-state domain, Q_A = Tr(rho_B^2), and the width is reduced to the spectral purity range over the allowed local trace-distance ball. Connection to S7 is conditional on S7 audit/validation.

==================================================
6. LOOP AUDIT: INITIAL CHECK
==================================================

L1 Definitions:
Trace distance, partial trace, subsystem purity, and global perturbation condition explicitly defined.

L2 Quantifiers:
The proposition applies to every pair of finite-dimensional bipartite density operators satisfying the stated global trace-distance condition.

L3 Dependency chain:
Global trace-distance bound -> contractivity under partial trace -> subsystem trace-distance bound -> purity Lipschitz inequality.

L4 Hidden assumptions:
Finite-dimensional density operators; normalized positive trace-one states; trace distance convention includes factor 1/2.

L5 Countermodel attempt:
No countermodel can violate the stated inequality under these assumptions because the proof uses standard trace-distance duality and contractivity. No tightness claim is made.

L6 Premise removal:
Without a bound on global trace distance, this proposition supplies no useful pairwise bound. A bound on rho_B alone does not imply a bound on Q_A over the full mixed domain.

L7 Non-implications:
The result does not establish identifiability from rho_B alone, does not establish S8-D correlated-state results, and does not prove the constant 2 optimal.

L8 Scope:
Finite-dimensional quantum density operators. No extension to infinite-dimensional systems or other metrics is claimed.

This is an initial LOOP AUDIT recorded with the derivation; a full audit of all S8 subcases remains open.
