3. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF S3.12
============================================================

Claim:
F embeds as an induced substructure of P^pi iff there exists an induced relational map h:F->Q such that
|h^(-1)({q})| <= |pi^(-1)({q})|
for every q in h(F).

NECESSITY CHECK:
Given an induced embedding e:F->P^pi, set h=pi o e.
The pullback equivalence transfers every positive and negative relation instance from e to h.
For each q, injectivity of e restricts to an injection from h^(-1)({q}) into pi^(-1)({q}).
Thus the capacity inequality follows.

SUFFICIENCY CHECK:
Given h and the capacity inequalities, choose an injection from each finite h-fiber into its corresponding pi-fiber.
Distinct pi-fibers are disjoint, so the chosen injections combine into an injective map e:F->P.
Since pi o e=h, the full relation equivalence transfers from h to e by the pullback definition.

AUDIT:
No proof gap found under the stated assumptions:
- F finite;
- purely relational signature;
- finite relation arities;
- induced equivalence checked on all tuples, including repeated coordinates;
- pullback defined exactly along pi.

The theorem is not thereby extended to signatures with functions, constants, or other additional structure.

4. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF S3.13
============================================================

For the uniform m-fold blow-up, every pi-fiber has exactly m elements.

An induced embedding e:F->Q^(m), projected through pi_m, yields an induced relational map h:F->Q with every h-fiber of size at most m.

Conversely, an induced relational map h with maximum collision number at most m can be lifted by assigning distinct copy indices in {1,...,m} within each h-fiber.

Therefore:
F embeds into Q^(m) iff kappa_F(Q)<=m.

AUDIT:
No counterexample or proof defect found for finite nonempty F under the stated relational assumptions.

5. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE W[1]-HARDNESS REDUCTION
============================================================

PROBLEM:
FIBER-THRESHOLD on finite simple undirected graphs.

PARAMETER:
n=|F|.

SOURCE:
k-CLIQUE, parameterized by k.

REDUCTION:
Given graph G and integer k>=2, set:
F=K_k
Q=G
m=1.

Then:
kappa_(K_k)(G)<=1
iff there is an induced relational map K_k->G with every fiber of size at most one
iff there is an injective induced relational map K_k->G
iff G contains a k-clique.

The final equivalence holds because K_k has every off-diagonal edge and no loops; an injective induced embedding therefore selects exactly k pairwise adjacent distinct vertices of G.

The construction is polynomial, and |F|=k.

CONCLUSION:
The problem is W[1]-hard parameterized by |F|.

No W[1]-membership proof is supplied. Therefore W[1]-completeness is NOT claimed.

The direct q^n enumeration is an XP upper bound, not an FPT upper bound. Unless FPT=W[1], an FPT algorithm parameterized by |F| does not exist for the unrestricted graph problem.

AUDIT:
Reduction is correct.

7. INDEPENDENT CHECK OF para-NP-HARDNESS BY m
============================================================

At m=1, FIBER-THRESHOLD is exactly induced-subgraph embedding:
kappa_F(Q)<=1 iff F embeds as an induced substructure of Q.

For finite simple undirected graphs, induced-subgraph embedding with both F and Q as input is NP-complete.

Thus the problem is NP-hard on the slice m=1.

If an algorithm of the form f(m)*poly(N) existed, fixing m=1 would give a polynomial-time algorithm for this NP-hard slice.

CONCLUSION:
The parameterization by m alone is para-NP-hard, unless P=NP.

AUDIT:
Correct. This does not imply hardness when another structural parameter is also bounded.

8. CHECK OF S3.14 COMPUTABILITY AND NP CLAIMS
============================================================

For finite explicit F and Q, enumerate all maps h:F->Q.
There are |Q|^|F| candidates.
For a fixed finite signature of bounded arity, each candidate can be checked in polynomial time by testing all relation tuples and computing its fiber sizes.

This gives a terminating exact algorithm and an XP upper bound.

For the decision problem kappa_F(Q)<=m, a map h is a polynomial-size certificate. The verifier checks induced relational equivalence and the fiber-size bound.

Under the stated fixed-signature, bounded-arity, explicit-encoding assumptions, membership in NP is valid.

At m=1 the graph problem is induced-subgraph embedding, giving NP-hardness and hence NP-completeness.

Fixed F, or a fixed finite forbidden family, yields polynomial time in Q because the exponent |F| is constant.

AUDIT:
No defect found under the stated encoding assumptions.
Succinct encodings, unbounded arity, and infinite structures are outside this conclusion.

9. LOGICAL SCOPE: PULLBACK VERSUS ARBITRARY AMALGAM
============================================================

S3.12-S3.14 characterize membership of one specific relational pullback P^pi in a forbidden class.

Failure:
P^pi not in K

does not imply:
there is no amalgam D in K.

The distinction is real. A free relational amalgam may omit mixed cross-relations and remain in K even when the quotient pullback introduces a forbidden induced structure.

Therefore any inference from failure of this pullback to nonexistence of every admissible amalgam requires an additional theorem and cannot be obtained from S3.12-S3.15 alone.

AUDIT:
The scope warning in S3.15 is correct and mathematically essential.
