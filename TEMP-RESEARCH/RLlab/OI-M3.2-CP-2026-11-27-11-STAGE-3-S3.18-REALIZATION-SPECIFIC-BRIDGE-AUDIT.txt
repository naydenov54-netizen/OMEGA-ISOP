2. PRIMARY OMEGA-ISOP SOURCE CONDITIONS
============================================================

The controlled Volume II mathematical foundations specify:

2.1 Primitive relational substrate

A = (U0,#)

with Boolean-valued apartness in the selected realization.

The standard apartness conditions are:

A1: not(x # x)
A2: x # y iff y # x
A3: x # y implies (x # z) or (z # y), for every z.

The document explicitly treats these as one admissible realization, not the only possible realization.

2.2 Indistinguishability

Under a complete Boolean apartness realization:

x ~ y iff not(x # y).

Under A1-A3 and classical logic, this complement relation is an equivalence relation.

The quotient:

DS = U0 / ~

is derived conditionally. It is not primitive.

2.3 Configuration embeddings

An embedding f:C1->C2 is injective and satisfies:

x #_1 y iff f(x) #_2 f(y).

Thus the OMEGA-ISOP configuration embedding condition is an induced embedding for the single binary relation #.

2.4 P2.3 quotient-based strong amalgam

For a span of apartness embeddings C->A and C->B:

- quotient each carrier by its apartness-indistinguishability relation;
- form the pushout Q of the quotient sets;
- form the pushout carrier S_P of the underlying sets over C;
- define pi_P:S_P->Q by the quotient-class maps;
- define:

x #_P y iff pi_P(x) != pi_P(y).

Under the explicit assumptions recorded in the HANDOFF, this constructs a strong amalgam for Boolean apartness structures with injective embeddings preserving and reflecting apartness.

The construction is not claimed to be a categorical pushout in the category of apartness structures.

2.5 P2.4 additional relations

P2.4.1 defines a free relational amalgam by taking the union of the A-side and B-side relation tuples on the P2.3 carrier. Mixed tuples not inherited from either side are false.

This is proved only for pure relational signatures with no extra axioms coupling the additional relations to apartness, subject to agreement on the common substructure.

P2.4.2 states that an n-ary relation descends to S/~ exactly when it is coordinatewise invariant:

(x1~y1 AND ... AND xn~yn)
IMPLIES
(R(x1,...,xn) IFF R(y1,...,yn)).

The HANDOFF marks the general quotient-level relational amalgamation as conditional and requiring further proof audit.

2.6 Richer structures

- Markov kernels;

These are not primitive elements of the relational substrate. They are realization-dependent extensions.

3. EXACT MATCH: S3.12 AND THE P2.3 APARTNESS REDUCT
============================================================

Let Q be the quotient-set pushout used in P2.3.

Equip Q with the discrete Boolean apartness relation:

q #_Q r iff q != r.

Let pi_P:S_P->Q be the P2.3 projection.

By the P2.3 definition:

x #_P y iff pi_P(x) != pi_P(y)
          iff pi_P(x) #_Q pi_P(y).

Therefore the apartness relation on P is exactly the relational pullback of the apartness relation on Q along pi_P.

For the single-symbol relational signature Sigma={#}, this is precisely the construction required by S3.12.

The embedding condition in Volume II:

x #_F y iff e(x) #_P e(y)

is exactly the induced-embedding condition used in S3.12, including both preservation and reflection.

CONCLUSION 3.1:

The S3.12 fiber-capacity theorem applies to the Boolean apartness reduct of the P2.3 construction whenever the forbidden pattern F is finite and the stated classical Boolean assumptions hold.

This is a genuine realization-specific bridge, not merely an analogy.

It does not yet extend to P2.4 additional relations or to richer OMEGA-ISOP structures.


============================================================
4. SPECIALIZATION OF THE FIBER CRITERION TO APARTNESS
============================================================

Let F be a finite Boolean apartness structure satisfying A1-A3.

Define:

x ~_F y iff not(x #_F y).

Then ~_F is an equivalence relation under the stated assumptions.

Because Q has discrete apartness:

q #_Q r iff q != r,

a map h:F->Q is an induced relational map exactly when:

x #_F y iff h(x) != h(y).

Equivalently:

h(x)=h(y) iff x~_F y.

Therefore every induced relational map h:F->Q:

1. is constant on each ~_F-equivalence class;
2. maps distinct ~_F-equivalence classes to distinct points of Q.

Thus h factors through an injective map:

F/~_F -> Q.

If Q has fewer points than F/~_F, no such h exists.

If Q has at least |F/~_F| points, such maps exist, and every nonempty h-fiber is exactly one ~_F-equivalence class.

Consequently, when Q has enough quotient points:

kappa_F(Q) = max { |[x]_(~_F)| : x in F }.

Otherwise:

kappa_F(Q) = infinity.

For nonuniform fibers of pi_P, S3.12 gives the sharper pointwise condition:

|[x]_(~_F)| <= |pi_P^(-1)({h(x)})|

for each quotient class assigned to h(x).

This is the exact specialization of S3.12 to the P2.3 apartness pullback.

INTERPRETATION:

The fiber-capacity condition measures how many distinct representatives are available in P for each indistinguishability class of F.

It does not by itself establish the existence or nonexistence of every possible amalgam. It characterizes induced copies inside this specified pullback carrier.

6. TRANSFER FAILURE: P2.4 FREE RELATIONAL AMALGAM IS NOT GENERALLY A PULLBACK
============================================================

S3.12 applies to a relation defined exactly by pullback:

R_P(x1,...,xn)
iff
R_Q(pi(x1),...,pi(xn)).

P2.4.1 instead defines the free relational amalgam by taking the union of the A-side and B-side tuples and leaving mixed tuples false.

These are different constructions in general.

A concrete counterexample shows that the free relation need not even descend to the P2.3 quotient.

COUNTEREXAMPLE:

Let the common structure C have carrier:

S_C={c}.

Let:

S_A={c,a}
S_B={c,b}.

Put the apartness relation equal to the empty relation on A and B.

The structures agree on C.

All three elements c,a,b belong to the same apartness-indistinguishability class in the P2.3 amalgam.

Add one unary relation symbol R.

Interpret it by:

R_A={c,a}
R_B={c}.

The two structures agree on C because R(c) is true on both sides.

The P2.4.1 free relational amalgam has carrier:

S_P={c,a,b}

and relation:

R_P={c,a}.

But:

a~b

while:

R_P(a)=true
R_P(b)=false.

Hence coordinatewise invariance fails:

a~b does not imply (R_P(a) iff R_P(b)).

Therefore R_P does not descend to a well-defined unary relation on the quotient S_P/~.

This free relational amalgam is valid as a free relational amalgam of the expanded structures, but it is not the pullback of a quotient-level unary relation along pi_P.

CONCLUSION 6.1:

P2.4.1 cannot be treated as an instance of S3.12 merely because its underlying carrier comes from P2.3.

The free relational expansion and the quotient pullback must remain distinct unless quotient invariance is independently proved.

7. WHAT P2.4.2 DOES AND DOES NOT PROVIDE
============================================================

For an n-ary relation R on S_P, coordinatewise invariance under ~ is necessary and sufficient for R to descend to a relation on S_P/~.

This is a valid quotient-descent criterion.

However, it does not by itself establish:

- that the relation inherited from A and B is coordinatewise invariant;
- that the descended relation agrees with both quotient-side relations;
- that the resulting quotient expansion satisfies additional axioms;
- that the pullback recovers the original free relational amalgam;
- that all constraints of OMEGA-ISOP are preserved;
- that a general amalgam exists or is unique.

Thus P2.4.2 is a necessary technical bridge for a quotient-level relational construction, not a general compatibility or amalgamation theorem.


============================================================
8. OPERATIONS AND DYNAMICS ARE OUTSIDE THE S3.12 SIGNATURE
============================================================

S3.12 is stated for purely relational signatures.

It does not include:

- function symbols;
- constants;
- algebraic operations;
- transition maps;
- Markov kernels;
- Koopman operators;
- realization maps;
- operational response maps;
- global or dynamical constraints.

A relational reduct may satisfy the S3.12 hypotheses while an additional operation fails to descend to the quotient.

For a unary operation f_P to induce an operation f_Q on Q through pi_P, at minimum the compatibility condition must hold:

pi_P(f_P(x)) = f_Q(pi_P(x))

for every x in P.

For an n-ary operation, the corresponding condition is:

pi_P(f_P(x1,...,xn))
=
f_Q(pi_P(x1),...,pi_P(xn)).

These conditions are not consequences of the relational pullback definition.

For dynamics, an analogous intertwining or compatibility condition must be stated for the selected realization and state-space maps.

Volume II explicitly treats realization and dynamics as additional, realization-dependent structures. Their transfer cannot be inferred from S3.12.


============================================================
9. AMALGAM EXISTENCE VERSUS ONE SPECIFIED PULLBACK
============================================================

S3.12 characterizes whether a finite induced structure F occurs in one specified pullback P^pi.

It does not characterize whether some other admissible amalgam D exists.

Therefore:

P^pi not in K

does not imply:

there is no admissible amalgam D in K.

This limitation is already explicit in S3.15 and remains fully active in the OMEGA-ISOP setting.

For P2.3, the existence of the stated strong apartness amalgam is established by its own quotient-based construction under its explicit assumptions.

For richer constraint classes, existence must be proved separately.

A failure of the P2.4 free expansion to satisfy a selected constraint does not rule out a different admissible amalgam unless a separate nonexistence argument is supplied.
