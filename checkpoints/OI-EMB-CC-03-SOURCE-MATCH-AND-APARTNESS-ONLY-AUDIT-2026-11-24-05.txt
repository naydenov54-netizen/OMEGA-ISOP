4. APARTNESS-ONLY SPAN
======================================================================

Use the nontrivial finite span:

S0={a,b}
S1={a,b,c}
S2={a,b,d}

On S0, S1, and S2, define apartness by inequality:
x#i y iff x!=y.

The inclusions:
i1:S0->S1
i2:S0->S2
are embeddings.

Each object is nontrivial:
|S0|=2, |S1|=3, |S2|=3,
and a#b in each.

For this finite test, an amalgam is a pair of embeddings:
j1:S1->A
j2:S2->A
with:
j1 o i1 = j2 o i2.

The selected apex apartness must satisfy irreflexivity, symmetry, and
cotransitivity. This is an explicit test realization, not a declaration
that these are the complete canonical admissibility conditions.

======================================================================
5. JOINTLY-GENERATED APARTNESS PROFILE
======================================================================

Restrict to apex carriers generated by the two source images:
A=j1(S1) union j2(S2).

The common base forces the images of a,b to agree.
Injectivity forces the images of c and d to be distinct from a,b.
There are two independent questions:
- whether j1(c) and j2(d) are equal;
- if distinct, whether they are apart.

CASE A1:
Four distinct elements a,b,c,d.
Apartness is full inequality:
x#y iff x!=y.
The source maps are embeddings.
This is a strong amalgam.

CASE A2:
Four distinct elements a,b,c,d.
Apartness is determined by the partition:
{{a},{b},{c,d}}.
Equivalently, all pairs in different blocks are apart and c,d are not
apart.
The source restrictions on S1 and S2 are still exactly inequality.
The source maps are embeddings.
This is also a strong amalgam because the two source images intersect
exactly in {a,b}.

A1 and A2 satisfy the three selected apartness axioms.
For partition apartness, irreflexivity and symmetry are immediate.
If x,y lie in different blocks, then for any z, z cannot lie in both
blocks; hence x#z or z#y. Thus cotransitivity holds.

CASE A3:
Identify j1(c)=j2(d).
The apex carrier has three elements a,b,c with full inequality.
Both source maps remain injective embeddings, but their images intersect
in more than the common base. This is a non-strong amalgam.

Thus, under the jointly-generated convention:
Profile_strong={A1,A2}
Profile_core={A1,A2,A3}
provided non-strong amalgams are admitted by the selected realization.

This finite enumeration does not count extensions with arbitrary
additional apex elements.

6. TWO INCOMPARABLE MINIMAL STRONG WITNESSES
======================================================================

Compare witnesses over the fixed span by factorization through an
embedding h commuting with both source legs.

A1 and A2 are not comparable.

A map A1->A2 commuting with the source maps must fix the four labelled
elements a,b,c,d. But A1 has c#d and A2 has not(c#d), so this map does
not preserve apartness.

A map A2->A1 also fixes all four labelled elements, but it fails to
reflect apartness on c,d.

Therefore neither witness factors into the other.

Minimality:
Suppose V is any witness with an embedding h:V->A1 commuting with the
source maps. Since the two source embeddings force four distinct
distinguished images a,b,c,d in V, its carrier has at least four
elements. Since A1 has exactly four, h is bijective. An embedding is
bijective and preserves/reflects apartness, so h is an isomorphism over
the span. Thus A1 is minimal.
The same argument applies to A2.

No least witness exists in this jointly-generated class:
A least witness would have to embed into both A1 and A2 over the span.
The source legs force its four distinguished elements to remain
distinct. Its carrier would therefore have exactly four elements, and
the commuting embedding would fix the labelled c,d pair. But c#d is
true in A1 and false in A2. No single apartness relation on that
four-point carrier can satisfy both embedding conditions.

RESULT:
Two incomparable minimal strong amalgams and absence of a least
amalgam are proved in the auxiliary category of finite nontrivial
apartness structures with the stated axioms and embedding morphisms.

This is not yet a canonical C_METACORE theorem.

======================================================================
7. FIXED-AMBIENT REALIZATION CAVEAT
======================================================================

Volume II III.4 states that configurations are subsets of a carrier U0
and their apartness is the restriction of one ambient relation #.

If a single fixed ambient pair (U0,#) and fixed source inclusions are
used, then the cross-pair relation between the already identified
ambient elements c and d is fixed. In that strict fixed-ambient setup,
A1 and A2 cannot both be alternative structures on the same four
labelled carrier with the same ambient relation.

The two-witness result in Section 6 instead treats A1 and A2 as distinct
admissible apex realizations in an abstract category of finite
apartness structures, with the same abstract source span.

The source architecture permits realization-dependent apartness, but
the exact intended scope of C_METACORE across realizations is not
specified in the inspected clauses.

Consequently:
- two minimal witnesses: proved in the abstract auxiliary category;
- two minimal witnesses inside one fixed ambient (U0,#): not proved and
  generally unavailable for this labelled carrier;
- canonical C_METACORE non-uniqueness: unresolved.

If the selected realization fixes standard inequality on every apex,
A1 is the only jointly-generated four-point apex profile of this form.
If it permits non-tight apartness satisfying the stated axioms, A2 is
also admissible.
