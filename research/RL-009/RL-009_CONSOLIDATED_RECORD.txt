STRUCTURAL INDEPENDENCE RL-LEVEL WORKING DEFINITION
Let P1 and P2 be boolean predicates on admissible model instances M in the realized dynamical family compatible with R.
P1 and P2 are structurally independent on this family if all four combinations:
(1,1)
(1,0)
(0,1)
(0,0)
are realizable under the same ceteris paribus discipline.

If a combination is provably unrealizable:
P1 and P2 are NOT independent
and the direction of the implication must be explicitly stated.

This definition is RL-level only.
It is not a Frozen-Core definition.

CLOSURE PROXY DEFINITIONAL DEPENDENCE
The Stage 1B operational proxy defines:
Closure(M) := Persistence(M,τ,ε) AND NOT Persistence(M^ablated,τ,ε)

where M^ablated is M with R_self(x) severed and fixed to a constant, while all external inputs are held identical.

Therefore, by construction:
Closure(M) = 1 → Persistence(M,τ,ε) = 1.

Thus:
Persistence = 0 AND Closure = 1
is DEFINITIONALLY UNREALIZABLE under this operationalization.

Consequently:
Structural Independence(Closure, Persistence)
NOT SATISFIED under the present Closure proxy.

This is a DESIGN-LEVEL FACT.
It is not an experimental finding.

The present Closure proxy therefore cannot establish structural independence from Persistence.

are non-degenerately realizable?
