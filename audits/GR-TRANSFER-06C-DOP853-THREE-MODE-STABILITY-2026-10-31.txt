M_A,q' = A_q M_A,q,   M_A,q(0)=I
M_B,q' = B_q M_B,q,   M_B,q(0)=I

For k=0,...,5, sensitivities satisfy:
S_q,k' = B_q S_q,k + C_q,k M_B,q
S_q,k(0)=0

where:
C_q,k(u) = [[0,0],[q*phi_k(u),0]]

Residual convention used in this run:
F_(q,i,j) = (M_B,q(T))_(i,j) - (M_A,q(T))_(i,j)

Jacobian convention:
J_(q,i,j),k = (S_q,k(T))_(i,j)

Ordering:
q = [+1,-1], and within each q:
(0,0), (0,1), (1,0).

NOTE:
The residual orientation and ordering above describe the executed
three-mode cell. This record does not independently establish that
this convention matches every historical implementation outside
the archived two-mode numerical reproduction.
