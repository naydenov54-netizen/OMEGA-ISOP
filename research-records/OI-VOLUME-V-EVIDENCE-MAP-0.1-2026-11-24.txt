VM-04 — OPERATIONAL RESPONSE DISTANCE
Source: Volume II v1.3, operational-distance section; Volume III v1.1, Sections 18-20; M3.2 v1.5, Section 8.
Claim:
D_L(M1,M2) = sup_{a in L_op} ||Resp_M1(a)-Resp_M2(a)||_Y.
Under nonempty L_op, normed Y and defined response maps, D_L is an extended pseudometric.
Dependencies: nonempty L_op; normed Y; common defined response domain. Finiteness requires additional boundedness. Separation of distinct models is not guaranteed.
Evidence status: formal construction and stated properties are present in controlled sources. The distinction between pseudometric and metric is explicit.
Physical bridge status: potentially compatible with operational comparison of models, but physical observables and measurement protocols must be supplied by a selected realization.
Audit disposition: HIGH-READINESS FORMAL INTERFACE; NO PHYSICAL CLAIM.

VM-05 — THEOREM 91, CONDITIONAL NON-EXPANSION
Source: M3.2 v1.5, Section 9; Volume II/III operational-response framework.
Assumptions:
H1: R exists, T is admissible, and R is preserved.
H2: Y is normed.
H3: there is tau:L_op -> L_op with Resp_(T(M))(a)=Resp_M(tau(a)).
H4: operational criterion and comparison class remain fixed.
Conclusion:
D_L(T(M1),T(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2).
Claim class: conditional theorem.
Evidence status: statement and assumptions are explicit in M3.2. The estimate depends on the common tau and the fixed comparison class; it is not a universal information-conservation law.
Physical bridge status: potentially testable in a specified model only after T, probes, response maps and tau are physically defined.
Audit disposition: KEEP CONDITIONAL; DO NOT REMOVE H1-H4.
