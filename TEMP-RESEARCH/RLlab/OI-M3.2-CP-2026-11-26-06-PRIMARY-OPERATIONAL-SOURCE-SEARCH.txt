4. AUTHORITATIVE LOOP_AUDIT_PROTOCOL L1-L8 RECOVERED
============================================================

The active M3.2 handoff on main contains the authoritative LOOP_AUDIT_PROTOCOL in its operational-instructions section. The exact eight stages are:

L1 - STATE THE TARGET:
Write the exact proposition C to be proved. Identify its logical form, domain, quantifiers, and conditions. Do not use an informal paraphrase as the target.

L2 - EXPOSE THE DEPENDENCY CHAIN:
List every definition, axiom, assumption, lemma, compatibility condition, operator property, and external result used by the proof. Trace the dependency chain of each item to its actual justification.

L3 - TEST FOR CONCLUSION SMUGGLING:
For every premise P and every defining clause, ask whether P is identical to C, logically implies C in the stated domain, or is equivalent to C after a change of notation or representation. Check whether an intermediate object was defined so that the desired conclusion is built into its definition.

L4 - CHECK INDEPENDENCE:
Any premise that carries the essential content of C must have an independent justification that does not rely on C or on an equivalent statement. If no such justification exists, the derivation is circular or incomplete. Renaming C, encoding it in an operator, or expressing it as covariance, invariance, equivariance, compatibility, or a response identity does not make it independent.

L5 - COUNTERMODEL TEST:
Attempt to construct a model in which all stated premises and definitions hold while C fails. If such a model exists, record the countermodel and the exact failed implication. If no countermodel is found, do not claim that none exists; record only that the attempted search did not find one.

L6 - PREMISE-REMOVAL TEST:
Remove or weaken each non-foundational premise in turn and determine whether the proof still works. This identifies which premise actually carries the conclusion. Do not remove a premise silently or claim a stronger theorem than the remaining assumptions support.

L7 - CLASSIFY THE RESULT:
Use one of these statuses:
NON-CIRCULAR DERIVATION;
CONDITIONAL RESULT;
CIRCULAR;
INCOMPLETE;
REFUTED.
A passed audit is not by itself proof of correctness.

L8 - REPORT NON-IMPLICATIONS:
State what the proof does not establish, including broader domains, uniqueness, necessity, universality, physical interpretation, or empirical validity where these do not follow.
