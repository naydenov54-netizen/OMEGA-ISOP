2. CLOSURE SCOPE
==================================================

N5 is closed only as the bounded-deterministic-noise recovery-uncertainty correction in THEOREM 91.6A v0.3-N5-CORRECTED, within the stated finite-dimensional linear observation framework and Euclidean norms.

The archived result establishes, under the stated assumptions and for a fixed observation y:

y = A(x) + eta
||eta|| <= epsilon

For any two compatible states x1 and x2:

||x1 - x2|| <= 2*epsilon/gamma_A

and therefore:

diam(C(y)) <= 2*epsilon/gamma_A.

The sharpness construction in the theorem attains this bound under the stated assumptions. The positive minimum-gain condition is explicit:

A injective iff gamma_A > 0

in the stated finite-dimensional Euclidean setting.

No unique recovery under nonzero bounded noise is claimed.
