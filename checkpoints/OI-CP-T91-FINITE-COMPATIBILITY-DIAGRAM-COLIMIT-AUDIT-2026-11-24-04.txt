============================================================
2. THE SPAN DIAGRAM
============================================================

Use three finite carriers:
S0={a}
S1={a,b}
S2={a,c}.

Equip each with the induced inequality apartness:
x #_i y iff x != y, for i=0,1,2.

Let:
e1:S0->S1, e1(a)=a;
e2:S0->S2, e2(a)=a.

Both maps are injective and preserve and reflect #. Thus they are embeddings of the selected finite apartness configurations.

The indexing category J is the span category with objects 0,1,2 and nonidentity arrows:
0->1;
0->2.

Define D:J->C as the above object and morphism assignment.

The diagram is a finite explicit compatibility span: the two configurations share the common one-point subconfiguration.

============================================================
3. PUSHOUT IN THE CATEGORY OF FINITE RELATIONAL STRUCTURES AND HOMOMORPHISMS
============================================================

Define category RelFin_hom:
- objects are finite sets equipped with a binary relation #;
- morphisms f:(S,#S)->(T,#T) are functions satisfying:
  x #S y => f(x) #T f(y).
They need not be injective and need not reflect the relation.

Define P={a,b,c} and relation #P generated by the images of the source relations:
#P={(a,b),(b,a),(a,c),(c,a)}.

There are canonical maps:
j1:S1->P, j1(a)=a, j1(b)=b;
j2:S2->P, j2(a)=a, j2(c)=c.

They are injective and are induced-substructure embeddings on their respective source pairs.

P is the pushout in RelFin_hom.

Proof of universal property:
Given any object (Q,#Q) and homomorphisms
f1:S1->Q, f2:S2->Q
such that f1∘e1=f2∘e2,
define the unique set map h:P->Q by:
h(a)=f1(a)=f2(a);
h(b)=f1(b);
h(c)=f2(c).

Every pair in #P is one of the four source relation pairs, and f1 or f2 preserves that pair. Hence h preserves #P. Uniqueness follows because a,b,c are the images of the diagram generators.

Therefore P with j1,j2 is the categorical pushout in RelFin_hom.

============================================================
4. WHY THIS IS NOT YET A PUSHOUT IN THE EMBEDDING CATEGORY
============================================================

Let RelFin_emb denote the category with the same objects but only injective relation-preserving-and-reflecting embeddings as morphisms.

The same span need not have a pushout in RelFin_emb.

Counterexample to the proposed P as a pushout:
Take Q=S1 with the identity embedding f1:S1->Q and the embedding f2:S2->Q defined by:
f2(a)=a;
f2(c)=b.

Both f1 and f2 are valid embeddings of their respective two-point inequality configurations into Q, and they agree on S0.

If a pushout map h:P->Q existed in RelFin_emb, it would have to satisfy:
h(a)=a;
h(b)=b;
h(c)=b.

Thus h(b)=h(c), so h is not injective. It cannot be a morphism in RelFin_emb.

This shows that the homomorphism-category pushout cannot simply be re-labelled as a pushout in the embedding-only category.

No general nonexistence theorem for every possible alternative candidate is claimed here. The explicit candidate P fails the required universal property in RelFin_emb, and the span has a genuine amalgamation/identification issue under embeddings.

============================================================
5. FULL COLIMIT RELATION AND INVOLUTION
============================================================

The pushout relation is exactly:
#P={(a,b),(b,a),(a,c),(c,a)}.

This is the undirected three-vertex star centered at a. It is not the complete inequality relation on P because:
(b,c) notin #P;
(c,b) notin #P.

Define:
g(a)=a;
g(b)=c;
g(c)=b.

Then:
g(g(x))=x.

Check every relation pair:
(a,b)->(a,c);
(b,a)->(c,a);
(a,c)->(a,b);
(c,a)->(c,a).

Thus g maps #P into #P. Since g is an involution, the reverse implication also holds:
x #P y iff g(x) #P g(y).

Therefore g is an automorphism of the entire colimit relational structure (P,#P), not merely of the two source configurations separately.

6. COMMON-NOISE STOCHASTIC DYNAMICS ON THE COLIMIT
============================================================

Fix p in [0,1] and define:
kappa(x,.)=(1-p)delta_x+p delta_g(x).

For every finite tuple:
Lambda_n(x1,...,xn;.)
=(1-p)delta_(x1,...,xn)
+p delta_(g(x1),...,g(xn)).

Because g is an automorphism of the entire relation #P, every tuple satisfying any selected coordinatewise #P constraints is mapped to another tuple satisfying those same constraints.

Marginals:
Marginal_i(Lambda_n)=kappa(x_i,.).

Projective consistency:
Coordinate projection of Lambda_n equals the corresponding lower-dimensional Lambda.

Permutation equivariance:
Coordinate permutations commute with Lambda_n.

Thus the stochastic coherent-lift construction passes for the entire finite colimit relation in RelFin_hom.
