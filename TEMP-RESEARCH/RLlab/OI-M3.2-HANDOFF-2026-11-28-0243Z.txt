4. A1-A3 AUDIT AND NON-IDENTIFICATION RESULT
============================================================

Core conditions as currently transcribed in the handoff:
A1: NOT(x # x)
A2: x # y IFF y # x
A3: x # y -> (x # z OR z # y), for every z.

Derived relation:
x ~ y IFF NOT(x # y)

Under classical Boolean logic, A1-A3 imply that ~ is an equivalence
relation. Consequently, # is inequality between distinct equivalence
classes in the quotient and is total on distinct quotient classes.

The constructed R is asymmetric in general:
R(a_0,b_1)=TRUE, while R(b_1,a_0)=FALSE.
Therefore R cannot be directly identified with #.

The proposed symmetrization
x #_s y IFF R(x,y) OR R(y,x)
also fails A3. Counterexample:
x=a_0, y=a_1, z=b_0.
Then x #_s y is TRUE, while x #_s z is FALSE and z #_s y is FALSE.

This disproves:
- direct identification R = #;
- the specific direct symmetrization R(x,y) OR R(y,x) as a realization
  of # satisfying A3.

It does NOT disprove a model in which R is a distinct derived relation
on a separately defined object, provided a valid bridge to the core
apartness relation is supplied and proved.
