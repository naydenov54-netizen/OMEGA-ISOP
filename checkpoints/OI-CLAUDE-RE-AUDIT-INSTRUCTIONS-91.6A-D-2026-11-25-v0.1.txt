5. REQUIRED AUDIT METHOD
==================================================

For every nontrivial claim:
1. State the exact proposition and quantifiers.
2. List all definitions and assumptions.
3. Re-derive the result independently.
4. Test edge cases and boundary values.
5. Attempt countermodels.
6. Remove or weaken assumptions and report what fails.
7. Separate theorem correctness from provenance and applicability.
8. Identify any defect introduced by the corrected versions.
9. Report whether the proof is complete, conditional, incomplete, circular, or refuted.

Numerical verification is supplementary. It does not replace proof inspection. Show calculations where constants are checked.
