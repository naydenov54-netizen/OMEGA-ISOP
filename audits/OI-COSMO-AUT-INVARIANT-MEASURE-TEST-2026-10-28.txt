2. FINITE TOY MODEL
Let X be a finite set with n >= 2 elements, equipped only with the complete apartness relation:
x # y iff x != y.
Every permutation of X preserves this relation. Therefore its automorphism group is the full symmetric group S_n, acting transitively on X.

Suppose, as an additional requirement, that a measure nu on the power set P(X) must be invariant under every automorphism:
nu(σ(A)) = nu(A) for all σ in S_n and all A subset X.

For any x,y in X, there exists σ with σ(x)=y. Hence:
nu({x}) = nu({y}).
Finite additivity then gives:
nu(A) = |A| w
where w is the common singleton weight and A is any subset of X.

Thus automorphism invariance determines equal relative weights in this finite transitive example. It does not determine w.

If one additionally requires probability normalization nu(X)=1, then:
n w = 1, so w = 1/n
and the unique invariant probability measure is:
nu(A) = |A|/n.

This uniqueness follows from two added conditions: invariance under all automorphisms and total probability one. Neither condition is a construction of physical spacetime volume.
