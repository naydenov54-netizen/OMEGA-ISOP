3. MATHEMATICAL AUDIT: THEOREM 91
============================================================

Audit the exact conditional non-expansion claim:

D_L(M1,M2) = sup over a in L_op of ||Resp_M1(a)-Resp_M2(a)||_Y

Claimed assumptions include:
H1: R exists, T is admissible, and R is preserved.
H2: Y is normed.
H3: There exists a map tau:L_op->L_op such that Resp_(T(M))(a)=Resp_M(tau(a)).
H4: The operational criterion and comparison class remain fixed.

Claim:
D_L(T(M1),T(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2).

Required checks:

1. Write the exact quantifiers and formal domain. Specify the comparison class C, probe set L_op, response maps, codomain, norm, and domain/codomain of T.
2. Determine whether H3 means ONE COMMON tau independent of M for every model in C. If the wording is ambiguous, report both readings.
3. Independently derive or refute the inequality under the common-tau reading.
4. Check whether tau(L_op) is necessarily a subset of L_op and whether that is sufficient for the supremum inequality.
5. Check whether surjectivity of tau is needed for non-expansion, and what stronger conclusion follows if tau is surjective.
6. Independently reproduce or improve the model-dependent-tau counterexample. Verify all numerical distances.
7. Determine the precise role of H1, H2, and H4. In particular, identify which assumptions are not used in the algebraic inequality.
8. Determine whether D_L is a metric or only an extended pseudometric under the stated conditions.
9. Test edge cases: empty probe set, infinite supremum, non-invariant comparison class, mismatched response spaces or norms, and undefined transformed models.
10. Apply the full LOOP audit. State clearly that a conditional theorem does not derive H3 from the Frozen Core or OMEGA-ISOP architecture.

Do not call Theorem 91 a universal information-conservation theorem.

5. MANDATORY LOOP AUDIT
============================================================

For every nontrivial theorem and every claimed bridge, apply these eight steps explicitly:

L1 TARGET: State the exact proposition, domain, quantifiers, and conditions.
L2 DEPENDENCIES: List definitions, axioms, assumptions, lemmas, compatibility conditions, and external results.
L3 CONCLUSION SMUGGLING: Test whether any premise or definition already contains the target or an equivalent consequence.
L4 INDEPENDENCE: Identify the independent justification for every premise carrying essential content.
L5 COUNTERMODEL: Attempt a model satisfying all premises while falsifying the target. Record the exact result. Do not claim no countermodel exists merely because none was found.
L6 PREMISE REMOVAL: Remove or weaken premises individually and state what fails.
L7 CLASSIFICATION: NON-CIRCULAR DERIVATION / CONDITIONAL RESULT / CIRCULAR / INCOMPLETE / REFUTED.
L8 NON-IMPLICATIONS: State what does not follow, including generality, uniqueness, necessity, physical interpretation, and empirical validity.

A valid conditional theorem must not be misreported as a derivation of its own essential compatibility assumption.
