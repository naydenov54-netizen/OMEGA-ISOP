No conclusion that J preserves this pushout can be drawn: the source category has no corresponding pushout object for this diagram.

A reverse identity-on-objects inclusion functor RelFin_hom -> APFin_nontriv_emb does not exist, because not every homomorphism is an embedding.

No equivalence of categories has been established.
