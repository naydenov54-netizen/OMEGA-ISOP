2. FORMALIZATION, TYPING, AND SCOPE CORRECTIONS

F-01 — COORDINATE / SIGNATURE EQUIVALENCE
Added an explicit lemma:
F(x)=F(y) iff for every i∈I, f_i(x)=f_i(y).
Under classical logic, this yields:
D(x,y)=1 iff there exists i∈I with f_i(x)≠f_i(y) iff F(x)≠F(y).

F-02 — D-COMPATIBLE RELATIONS
Defined P_D={(x,y)∈X×X:D(x,y)=1} and R D-compatible iff R⊆P_D.
Clarified the exact boundary:
- If P_D is nonempty, at least two distinct D-compatible relations exist.
- If P_D is empty (D≡0), the only D-compatible relation is the empty relation.
The finite example remains a valid non-uniqueness witness.

For the corestriction F_im:X→F(X), the equation is:
F_im = Phi ∘ q.
Audit Task 03 already factors its signature map into the full product codomain and requires no correction on this point.
