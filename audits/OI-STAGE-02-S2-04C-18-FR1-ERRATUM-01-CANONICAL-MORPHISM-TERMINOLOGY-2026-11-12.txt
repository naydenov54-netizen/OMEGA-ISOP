2. REQUIRED TEXTUAL REPLACEMENT

In Section B, FR-1, under “Доказателство”, replace:

„Нека P е копроизведение с инжекции i:B→P, j:C→P.“

with:

„Нека P е копроизведение с канонични морфизми i:B→P и j:C→P.“

Add immediately after that sentence:

„Каноничните морфизми i и j не се приемат за инективни в класове (i) и (ii). В клас (iii) те са инективни по дефиницията на класа морфизми. Доказателството по-долу използва само запазването на # от i, j, u1 и u2, а не инективността на i или j.“

3. MATHEMATICAL REASON

The three morphism classes have different requirements:

(i) all functions preserving #;
(ii) strong functions, preserving and reflecting #;
(iii) injective functions preserving #.

Thus injectivity is not part of the morphism definition in classes (i) and (ii). A coproduct's canonical morphisms must be described as canonical morphisms unless injectivity has separately been proved.

In class (iii), canonical coproduct morphisms, if the coproduct exists, are morphisms of that category and hence injective. This does not justify using “injections” as a uniform description across (i), (ii), and (iii).

4. EFFECT ON THE FR-1 PROOF

The proof requires:

(a) x#y in B;
(b) i preserves #, hence i(x)#i(y) in P;
(c) A3 in P, yielding i(x)#j(z) OR j(z)#i(y);
(d) the universal property supplies u2:P→W2 in the first branch and u1:P→W1 in the second branch;
(e) u1 and u2 preserve #;
(f) the specified missing cross-relations in W1 and W2.

No step requires i or j to be injective.

Therefore the correction changes the terminology and removes an unjustified uniform injectivity claim. It does not change the proof's logical conclusion, provided the stated premises and cocone morphisms hold in each category considered.

5. DISTINCTION THAT MUST BE PRESERVED

The maps B→Wk and C→Wk used to exhibit cocones are inclusion maps. Under the stated constructions they are injective, strong, and #-preserving.

These inclusion maps are not the same maps as the hypothetical canonical coproduct morphisms i:B→P and j:C→P.

Do not transfer the injectivity property of the inclusion maps to i and j.
