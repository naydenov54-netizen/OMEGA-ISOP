EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

FACT:
A statement directly supported by an identified source, derivation, or inspected implementation.

FORMAL RESULT:
A mathematically established result within its stated assumptions and domain.

CONDITIONAL RESULT:
A result established only under explicitly stated constructional, analytical, computational, or environmental conditions.

RECORDED COMPUTATIONAL RESULT:
An output preserved from a recorded execution. It is not automatically an independent reproduction or analytical proof.

NUMERICAL CROSS-CHECK:
A comparison against a numerical reference or approximation. It is not an interval proof.

AUDIT FINDING:
A conclusion about evidence, source code, arithmetic, provenance, or an unresolved proof obligation.

HYPOTHESIS / RESEARCH TARGET:
A proposition or construction not yet established as a formal result.

OPEN:
The required proof, source, or validation has not been completed.

BLOCKED:
The required evidence or dependency is unavailable or cannot currently be verified.

NOT ESTABLISHED:
The available evidence does not support the claim. This is not necessarily a proof that the claim is false.

For the stated exact first-order linear system

x'(t) = A(t) x(t),

det(M(t)) = det(M(t0)) * exp(integral(t0,t) trace(A(s)) ds).
