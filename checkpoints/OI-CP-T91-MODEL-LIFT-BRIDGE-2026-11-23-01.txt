OMEGA-ISOP
THEOREM 91 — MODEL-LEVEL LIFT BRIDGE
CHECKPOINT: OI-CP-T91-MODEL-LIFT-BRIDGE-2026-11-23-01
STATUS: CONDITIONAL FORMALIZATION / HOLD FOR CANONICAL ADOPTION
SCOPE: Auxiliary formal bridge only. No Frozen Core or canonical theorem modification.

1. PURPOSE

Theorem 91 compares operational responses of models through D_L and writes
Resp_(T(M))(a). Volume II defines state-level evolution T_X : X -> X, while
the operational distance is written for models M. A model-level lift is
therefore required to type the theorem without identifying a model with a
state.

2. MODEL CLASS AND UNDERLYING STATE SPACE

Fix a realization Phi and let M_Phi be a class of mathematical models
admissible under that realization.

For each M in M_Phi, let X_M be its state space and let

U : M_Phi -> Set
U(M) = X_M

denote the underlying-state-space assignment.

Let

Resp_M : L_op -> Y

be the operational response map for model M, on the fixed operational
domain L_op, with Y a normed space.

Let C be the fixed comparison class, C subseteq M_Phi.

3. STATE-LEVEL DYNAMICS

For each model M under consideration, let

T_X,M : X_M -> X_M

be a state-level evolution map. In the common-carrier case this may be one
fixed map T_X : X -> X, as in the Volume II notation.

State-level admissibility and relational preservation remain governed by
the selected realization Phi. In particular, nonemptiness

A(R;Phi) != empty

means that at least one compatible evolution exists. It does NOT imply
that an arbitrary selected T_X is admissible.

4. MODEL-LEVEL LIFT

A model-level lift is a map

T_hat : C -> M_Phi

such that T_hat(M) is the model obtained by transporting or transforming
the model structure of M consistently with T_X,M.

The lift condition must be witnessed by a structure-preserving model
morphism

j_M : M -> T_hat(M)

whose underlying state map is T_X,M:

U(j_M) = T_X,M.

The precise meaning of model morphism depends on the explicitly declared
signature and structure of M. It must preserve every structure that the
selected model category declares to be preserved. This checkpoint does
not assume that the signature is already specified in Volume II.

For T_hat to be an endomorphism of the comparison class, require

T_hat(C) subseteq C.

At minimum, all original and transformed models must lie in one common
domain on which their response maps and D_L are defined.

IMPORTANT:
The existence of T_X,M does not by itself prove the existence or
uniqueness of T_hat. A lift can fail to exist if the state map does not
transport the model's operations, relations, observables, or other
declared structure. A non-injective map may require additional quotient
or congruence conditions. These conditions must be established for the
chosen model category, not presumed.

5. OPERATIONAL COVARIANCE CONDITION

The model-level lift alone does not imply the response identity required
by Theorem 91.

Introduce the separate covariance condition:

There exists one common map

tau : L_op -> L_op

such that

for every M in C and every a in L_op:

Resp_(T_hat(M))(a) = Resp_M(tau(a)).

The same tau must work for all M in C. A model-dependent tau_M is
insufficient for the contraction proof.

Also require:
(a) L_op is nonempty;
(b) all response maps in the identity are defined;
(c) Y is a normed space;
(d) the operational criterion and comparison class are fixed;
(e) T_hat(C) subseteq C, or an equivalent common-domain condition.

6. MODEL-LEVEL OPERATIONAL DISTANCE

Define

D_L(M1,M2) =
sup_(a in L_op) ||Resp_M1(a) - Resp_M2(a)||_Y.

For M1,M2 in C, the lifted dynamics obeys

D_L(T_hat(M1),T_hat(M2))
=
sup_(a in L_op)
||Resp_(T_hat(M1))(a) - Resp_(T_hat(M2))(a)||_Y

=
sup_(a in L_op)
||Resp_M1(tau(a)) - Resp_M2(tau(a))||_Y

<=
sup_(b in L_op)
||Resp_M1(b) - Resp_M2(b)||_Y

=
D_L(M1,M2).

Therefore

D_L(T_hat(M1),T_hat(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2).

No surjectivity of tau is required. The inequality follows because
tau(L_op) is a subset of L_op.

7. BRIDGE DIAGRAM

For each M:

M  --j_M-->  T_hat(M)
|                 |
U                 U
|                 |
X_M --T_X,M-->   X_M

The diagram expresses that the underlying state map of the model
transformation is the chosen state evolution. It is meaningful only
after the model category, its objects, morphisms, and preserved
structure have been specified.

8. RELATION TO THEOREM 91 HYPOTHESES

H1 remains the structural applicability condition: R exists, T_X is
admissible, and R is preserved under the selected realization.

H2 supplies the normed-space structure of Y.

H3 is now typed at model level by using T_hat and one common tau.

H4 must ensure a fixed operational criterion and comparison domain.
For an explicit fully typed statement, class closure
T_hat(C) subseteq C should be stated, unless equivalent domain closure
is already guaranteed elsewhere.

H1 is not used directly in the norm inequality, but remains a required
structural precondition. It must not be removed merely because the
algebraic estimate uses H2-H4.

9. WHAT THIS BRIDGE DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

Established conditionally:
- the type distinction between state dynamics and model dynamics;
- the required shape of a model-level lift;
- the need for a structure-preserving witness j_M;
- the common-domain/closure requirement;
- the uniformity requirement on tau;
- the non-expansion inequality once the stated assumptions hold.

Not established by the current canonical source material:
- the explicit model signature;
- the concrete construction of T_hat from T_X;
- existence or uniqueness of the lift;
- whether the model morphism must preserve all relations, operations,
  observables, or a specified sub-signature;
- the operational covariance identity as a consequence of the lift.

Thus this bridge resolves the typing architecture conditionally, but it
does not yet prove that a suitable lift exists in the canonical OMEGA-ISOP
model category.

10. RECOMMENDED TYPED FORM OF THEOREM 91 CONCLUSION

Given H1-H4 together with an explicitly defined model-level lift
T_hat : C -> C and the uniform operational covariance condition,

D_L(T_hat(M1),T_hat(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2)

for all M1,M2 in C.

This is an auxiliary typed formulation, not a replacement of the
canonical Theorem 91 text.
