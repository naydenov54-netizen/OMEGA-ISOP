==================================================
1. PURPOSE AND CRITICAL CORRECTION
==================================================

This version preserves the explicit R² model and corrects the overbroad wording identified independently by Claude and Perplexity.

CORRECT STATEMENT:
For every fixed finite n, F_n is injective and has a Lipschitz inverse. However, the inverse Lipschitz constants are not uniformly bounded over n.

INCORRECT STATEMENT REMOVED:
"Exact identifiability is not equivalent to stable invertibility."

For each fixed finite n in this model, exact identifiability DOES imply Lipschitz-stable invertibility. The actual result is the failure of UNIFORM-IN-n stability.

==================================================
2. EXPLICIT MODEL
==================================================

Let X=R² with Euclidean norm and x=(u,v).

Define:

a_n=1/(n+1)
b_n=1/(n+1)²

for n>=1.

Define:

F_n(u,v)=(a_n*u,b_n*v)
       =(u/(n+1),v/(n+1)²).

This is a parameterized family of maps. It is NOT defined as a composition of the preceding maps.

==================================================
3. FINITE-STAGE INJECTIVITY AND CONTRACTION
==================================================

For fixed finite n, if F_n(u1,v1)=F_n(u2,v2), positivity of a_n and b_n gives u1=u2 and v1=v2. Thus F_n is injective.

For x1,x2:

||F_n(x1)-F_n(x2)||²
= (delta_u)²/(n+1)² + (delta_v)²/(n+1)^4.

The singular values are:

sigma_max(F_n)=1/(n+1)
sigma_min(F_n)=1/(n+1)².

Therefore:

Lip(F_n)=1/(n+1)<1.

Since R² is complete and F_n maps R² to R², each F_n is a Banach contraction.

This statement concerns the Lipschitz constant of this explicit map. It does not establish a connection to THEOREM 91's operational non-expansion distance.

==================================================
4. ASYMPTOTIC LIMIT
==================================================

For every fixed (u,v):

F_infinity(u,v)=lim(n->infinity)F_n(u,v)=(0,0).

If X contains more than one point, F_infinity is not injective.

Thus every finite-stage map is injective, while the pointwise limiting map is not.

The finite maps and their pointwise limit are distinct mathematical objects.

==================================================
5. EXACT INVERSE AND UNIFORM STABILITY
==================================================

For fixed finite n:

F_n^(-1)(y1,y2)=((n+1)*y1,(n+1)²*y2).

Hence:

Lip(F_n^(-1))=(n+1)².

The condition number is:

kappa(F_n)=sigma_max/sigma_min=n+1.

Consequently:

for every fixed finite n, F_n is bi-Lipschitz;

but:

sup(n>=1) Lip(F_n^(-1))=infinity.

Therefore the family has no uniform inverse-Lipschitz bound as n varies.

PRECISE CONCLUSION:
Finite-stage exact identifiability and finite-stage Lipschitz stability both hold. The family is not uniformly stable in n.

==================================================
6. BOUNDED NOISE
==================================================

Let y_n=F_n(x)+eta with ||eta||<=epsilon.

For two candidate states x1,x2, their closed observation-noise balls are disjoint exactly when:

||F_n(x1)-F_n(x2)||>2*epsilon.

Since:

||F_n(x1)-F_n(x2)||
>= ||x1-x2||/(n+1)²,

a sufficient condition for disjointness is:

||x1-x2||>2*epsilon*(n+1)².

If the observation-center distance is <=2*epsilon, the noise balls intersect or touch, so guaranteed distinction is unavailable under this noise model.

For fixed epsilon and fixed nonzero state separation, the sufficient separation requirement worsens with n. This is a practical noise-sensitivity statement, not loss of exact injectivity at finite n.

==================================================
7. INDIVIDUAL CHANNELS AND COMBINED OBSERVATION
==================================================

Define:

O1_n(u,v)=u/(n+1)
O2_n(u,v)=v/(n+1)².

Each scalar channel alone is non-injective on R². The combined map F_n=(O1_n,O2_n) is injective.

The result illustrates joint identifiability from individually insufficient scalar channels. It does not show that the channels become physically independent observables or that they preserve Shannon information.

==================================================
8. COMPLETE HISTORY: EXACT RESULT AND VACUITY LIMIT
==================================================

Define:

H(u,v)=(F_1(u,v),F_2(u,v),F_3(u,v),...).

The complete history is injective. In fact, F_1 alone is already injective. Therefore the history's injectivity is true but does not demonstrate that infinitely many stages are needed to recover the state.

Use the history metric:

d_H(H(x1),H(x2))=sup(n>=1)||F_n(x1)-F_n(x2)||.

For every n:

||F_n(x1)-F_n(x2)|| <= (1/2)||x1-x2||.

At n=1:

||F_1(x1)-F_1(x2)|| >= (1/4)||x1-x2||.

Thus:

(1/4)||x1-x2|| <= d_H(H(x1),H(x2)) <= (1/2)||x1-x2||.

So H is bi-Lipschitz under this specific history metric.

NON-VACUITY WARNING:
The full-history result is not a finite-witness or synergy example, because the first history element already identifies the state. Any claim that the infinite history is necessary is false for this construction.

A stronger nontrivial history example would require a separately defined family in which no single selected initial stage is injective and the complete family is needed. That is not added here.

==================================================
9. GENERAL POSITIVE-COORDINATE CRITERION
==================================================

Let F:X->R^m be injective, and define coordinate functions f_i:X->R.

For each finite n, let a_i(n)>0 and define:

F_n(x)=(a_1(n)f_1(x),...,a_m(n)f_m(x)).

Then F_n is injective for every finite n.

Proof: equality of F_n values and positivity of every a_i(n) imply equality of every f_i value, hence F(x1)=F(x2), and injectivity of F gives x1=x2.

If a_i(n)->a_i* and all f_i(x) are finite for each fixed x, then the pointwise limit is:

F_infinity(x)=(a_1* f_1(x),...,a_m* f_m(x)).

Let S={i:a_i* != 0}. Then F_infinity is injective iff the reduced map x->(f_i(x))_(i in S) is injective. This criterion is specific to the stated coordinate-scaled construction.

==================================================
10. LIMITATIONS AND NON-IMPLICATIONS
==================================================

This theorem does not establish:
- a universal result for arbitrary nonlinear observation families;
- a universal preservation of asymptotic identifiability;
- uniform inverse stability in n;
- robustness to arbitrary noise distributions;
- Shannon information preservation or loss;
- a formal connection to THEOREM 91, H3, or any Frozen Core axiom;
- physical realizability, empirical validation, or universal OMEGA-ISOP applicability.

"Operational contraction" in this document means only Lip(F_n)<1 in the stated metric. It must not be conflated with the non-expansion theorem labelled THEOREM 91 without a separately proved bridge.
