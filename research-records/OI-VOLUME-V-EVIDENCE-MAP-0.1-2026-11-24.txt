DEFINED UPDATE-DEPENDENCY TEST D2:
X ->_U Y iff Y(t+1) depends non-constantly on X(t), witnessed by admissible x1 != x2 for which the next Y values differ.

COMMON MODEL:
M={a,b}; D1={a}; R_global(t)=(x_t,y_t) in {0,1}^2; R1(t)=x_t; A1(t)=x_t.
The filtering chain R_global(t) ->_F R1(t) ->_F A1(t) is satisfied in both models below.

K1 AS SUPPLIED:
x_(t+1)=1-A1(t); y_(t+1)=y_t.
Since A1(t)=x_t, choosing x_t=0 and x_t=1 at fixed y_t gives different next global states. K1 VALIDLY witnesses A1 ->_U R_global.

K0 AS SUPPLIED:
x_(t+1)=x_t; y_(t+1)=y_t.
But A1(t)=x_t, so x_(t+1)=A1(t). At fixed y_t, choosing x_t=0 and x_t=1 gives different next global states. Under D2, K0 therefore ALSO has A1 ->_U R_global.
Finding: the supplied K0 does NOT witness A1 not->_U R_global. This is a formal countermodel defect in the archival supplement as written.

CORRECTED LABORATORY COUNTERMODEL K0*:
x_(t+1)=0; y_(t+1)=y_t.
For any two admissible current states with the same y_t and different A1(t)=x_t, the next global state is the same (0,y_t). Thus A1 not->_U R_global under D2.
K0* retains the same static filtering chain and time-defined structures. It differs from K1 only in the update rule for x.
