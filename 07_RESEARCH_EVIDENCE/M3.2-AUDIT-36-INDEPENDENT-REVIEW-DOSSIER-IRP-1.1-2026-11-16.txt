6. F1–F7 REQUIRED VERIFICATION

F1 — OBSERVATIONAL EQUIVALENCE
Verify:
h ~_e h' iff P_h^e = P_h'^e;
h ~_E h' iff P_h^e = P_h'^e for every e in E;
h ~_Π h' iff Law_h^π = Law_h'^π for every π in Π.
Check equivalence-relation properties, implications, repeated Bernoulli counterexample, marginal versus joint laws, and scope of declared experiment/protocol classes.

F2 — ADAPTIVE PROTOCOLS
Verify conditional-kernel construction, induction over finite histories, almost-sure equality, reachable-history conditions, protocol-dependent experiment selection, measurability, independent internal randomization, infinite-horizon construction (including Ionescu–Tulcea conditions), necessity versus sufficiency, and counterexamples when assumptions are removed.

F3 — POSTERIOR ODDS
Verify equal full-history laws, common dominating measure, Radon–Nikodym derivatives, likelihood ratio equal to one almost surely, posterior odds equal to prior odds, positive prior-mass conditions, and undefined likelihoods/zero-probability events.

F4 — PRIOR-MASS REDISTRIBUTION
Verify Proposition 1 and the identity:
-t L(a,h) + t L(a,h') = 0 when h ~_L h'.
Check expected action loss, action ranking and pre-data Bayes risk. Verify Proposition 1b and whether protocol equivalence, common policy feasibility, equal expected costs, loss assumptions and measurable policy representation are sufficient. Do not call sufficient assumptions necessary.
