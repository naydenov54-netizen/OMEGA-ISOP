3. EXACT SOURCE FINDINGS
============================================================

3.1 Configuration embeddings

Volume II III.5 defines an embedding f:C1->C2 as injective and preserving and reflecting the selected apartness structure:

x #1 y iff f(x) #2 f(y).

This establishes the embedding condition for configuration maps.
It does not, by itself, define the intersection condition for a strong amalgam.

3.2 Compatibility

Volume II IV.1 states:

CC(C1,C2) = 1 iff an admissible amalgamation of C1 and C2 exists.
Otherwise CC(C1,C2) = 0.

The section describes compatibility as structural admissibility and says the binary form is a minimal representation.
It does not explicitly add a strongness clause.

3.3 Diagram and R

Volume II IV.2 states that D_CC is the diagram generated by selected configurations and their admissible compatibility relations.
Where the required categorical construction exists:

R := colim(D_CC).

The colimit is unique up to the relevant categorical equivalence when it exists.
This is not a claim that a unique canonical structural representation exists in every formalism.

3.4 Existence boundary

Volume II IV.3 requires:
- a valid compatibility diagram;
- the relevant categorical structure;
- existence of the required colimit or an equivalent constraint-preserving construction.

If these conditions fail, R is not automatically defined by the construction.

3.5 D_CC remains open

Volume III Section 9 states that D_CC alone is not a complete categorical construction.
A complete realization requires:
J = indexing category;
D:J->C_METACORE;
the required morphisms and compatibility conditions;
the required universal property.

The formalization remains an open program unless these data are supplied.

3.6 Frozen Core wording

Frozen Core 1.7 defines compatibility by whether configurations can be combined without contradiction in their inherited difference structure under selected realization rules.
Its minimal CC realization again uses existence of an admissible amalgamation.
Frozen Core 1.8 permits an alternative constraint representation only if it preserves the compatibility information required by the selected realization.

No explicit strong-amalgamation requirement was found in these relevant clauses.

============================================================
4. LOGICAL CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

Finding A:
Strong amalgamation is NOT an explicit universal requirement in the inspected CC/R clauses.

Finding B:
The architecture does NOT explicitly declare that every homomorphism-level or every mathematically possible amalgamation is admissible.

Finding C:
Admissibility is realization-dependent and refers to selected structural conditions. The full conditions are not yet specified by the current D_CC formalization.

Finding D:
Volume II's embedding condition (injective plus apartness preservation and reflection) constrains configuration embeddings. It does not logically entail the strong-amalgamation intersection condition.

Finding E:
Volume III explicitly leaves the indexing category, functor, morphisms, compatibility conditions, and universal property open.

Therefore the architecture-level choice among:
(A) strong amalgams only;
(B) all source-admissible amalgams;
(C) a realization-selected admissibility class;
is UNDERDETERMINED by the inspected controlled wording.

The most faithful reading is (C) as an architectural interface, but the actual class remains unspecified. This is not an authorization to choose a new class.

============================================================
5. CONSEQUENCES FOR THE FINITE TEST PROFILE
============================================================

The finite span test previously analyzed has three jointly-generated core amalgam profiles:
A1: c and d are distinct and apart;
A2: c and d are distinct but not apart;
A3: c and d are identified.

Under the test's chosen apartness axioms and exact source embeddings:
- A1 and A2 are strong amalgams;
- A3 is a non-strong amalgam;
- all three satisfy the stated core test's source-admissibility conditions when strongness is not imposed.

The source audit does not select among A1, A2, or A3.
It also does not establish that the test's chosen auxiliary apartness axioms are the canonical universal OMEGA-ISOP admissibility rules.

Thus:
Profile_strong(D) = {A1,A2}
Profile_core(D) = {A1,A2,A3}
remain auxiliary results under the stated finite test convention only.

============================================================
6. CONSEQUENCES FOR R
============================================================

The current architecture supports the conditional form:

R := colim(D_CC)

when the selected category, diagram, and colimit exist.

It also permits an equivalent constraint-preserving construction if a canonical colimit is unavailable, provided the compatibility information required by the selected realization is preserved.

It does NOT currently determine whether R must encode:
- one selected admissible amalgam;
- the complete class/profile of admissible amalgams;
- an equivalence class of compatible diagrams;
- another constraint representation satisfying the selected realization's information-preservation requirements.

The finite test demonstrates why a relation-only representation may be insufficient:
apartness data alone may fail to record carrier identifications and strongness.
Any alternative representation must preserve whatever compatibility information the selected realization actually requires.
The source does not yet enumerate that information universally.

7. MINIMAL ARCHITECTURAL COMPLETION NEEDED
============================================================

A future D_CC realization must state, without circularity:

1. The object class of C_METACORE.
2. The exact arrow class.
3. The selected realization-specific admissibility predicate Adm(C1,C2;witness).
4. Whether strongness is required, optional, or merely recorded.
5. Whether CC is existential over all admissible witnesses or over a selected subclass.
6. The indexing category J and diagram functor D:J->C_METACORE.
7. The compatibility data and coherence conditions carried by D.
8. The required colimit universal property.
9. If no colimit exists, the exact information-preservation criterion for an alternative constraint representation.

These are formalization obligations, not proposed amendments to the Frozen Core.

============================================================
8. LOOP / CIRCULARITY CONTROL
============================================================

Do not define admissibility as "whatever admits the desired R" or "whatever preserves the desired theorem result."
That would build the target conclusion into the admissibility predicate.

Do not infer strongness from the embedding definition.
Injectivity of each source map does not imply that the two source images intersect only in the common base.

Do not infer that CC=1 selects a unique amalgam.
Existence is not selection, and a binary compatibility value does not encode the full witness space.

Do not infer that an arbitrary homomorphism cocone is source-admissible.
Categorical cocone status and source-level admissibility remain distinct until the arrow and witness conditions are fixed.
