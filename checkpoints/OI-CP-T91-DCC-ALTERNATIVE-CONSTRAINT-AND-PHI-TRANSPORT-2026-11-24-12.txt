4. ALTERNATIVE CONSTRAINT-PRESERVING REPRESENTATION
============================================================

Motivation:
The tested span has source-admissible amalgamations but no pushout in the auxiliary embedding category.
The homomorphism-category pushout exists but its universal property is evaluated against a weaker class of cocones.

Therefore test an alternative representation that records the admissible amalgamation space rather than forcing a single universal configuration.

Define the admissible amalgamation profile:

Profile(D) := AdmAmalg(D) / ~=_D

where ~=_D is isomorphism over the fixed diagram D.

Each profile element must retain:
1. the carrier identifications made among the source objects;
2. the induced apartness relation in the amalgam;
3. exact induced-substructure restrictions to each source object;
4. whether the amalgam is strong or non-strong;
5. the isomorphism class over the fixed diagram.

At minimum:
CC_source(D)=1 iff Profile(D) is nonempty.

The profile is intended as a constraint representation, not as a categorical pushout and not automatically as an object of C_METACORE.

A possible typed data representation is:
R_alt(D) = (Profile(D), Constraints(D), RestrictionData(D))

where:
- Profile(D) stores admissible amalgamation classes;
- Constraints(D) stores the defining admissibility predicates;
- RestrictionData(D) records the exact source embeddings/restrictions.

This construction preserves the existence predicate and, if fully specified, the distinctions among admissible outcomes. It may be used as an alternative representation of R only if it preserves all compatibility information required by the selected realization.

OPEN REQUIREMENTS:

5. MINIMAL PHI TRANSPORT SQUARE
============================================================

For a selected relational representation R and its target R', let:
Real(R) = realization space of R
Real(R') = realization space of R'

Let:
Phi_R: Real(R) -> M
Phi_R': Real(R') -> M'

A transport claim requires explicitly typed maps:
T_real: Real(R) -> Real(R')
T_M: M -> M'

The required compatibility equation is:

T_M o Phi_R = Phi_R' o T_real

This equation is a necessary realization-compatibility/naturality condition.
It is not sufficient by itself to prove admissible evolution transport.

If the profile construction is multi-valued, T_real must either:
- be a well-defined function on the selected realization classes; or
- be explicitly defined as a relation/functorial correspondence with stated coherence.
Do not silently treat a multi-valued correspondence as a function.

============================================================
6. EVOLUTION TRANSPORT AND A(R;Phi)
============================================================

Let:
Evol(X) = admissible candidate evolution laws on state space X
Evol(X') = admissible candidate evolution laws on state space X'

Specify:
T_E:Evol(X) -> Evol(X')

For one-way preservation, prove:

e in A(R;Phi_R) => T_E(e) in A(R';Phi_R')

For equivalence of admissibility, prove:

e in A(R;Phi_R) iff T_E(e) in A(R';Phi_R')

The second statement requires a suitable inverse, reflection theorem, or exact equivalence of the relevant evolution classes. It does not follow from the forward implication.

The definition of A(R;Phi) is realization-dependent. Consequently the proof must identify:
- the source and target state spaces;
- the source and target realization classes;
- the relation between Phi_R and Phi_R';
- the exact transformation of evolution laws;
- the preservation of every relational/dynamical constraint used in the definition of A.

A relational embedding alone supplies none of these maps automatically.

7. STOCHASTIC CASE
============================================================

If Evol contains stochastic kernels, T_E must be defined at the kernel level and must preserve the required structure.

Depending on the selected stochastic realization, prove:
- probability-kernel validity;

- correct coordinate marginals;

K_kappa(L_op) subseteq L_op.

A Markov kernel or relational endomorphism alone does not establish membership in A(R;Phi).
