2. MATHEMATICAL CHECK OF THE CORRECTION
The replacement of “injections” by “canonical coproduct morphisms” for i:B -> P and j:C -> P is correct and necessary for precision.
The proof as stated does not use injectivity of i or j. It uses:
(a) preservation of apartness by i and j;
(b) cotransitivity A3 in P;
(c) existence of the relevant universal morphisms u2:P -> W2 and u1:P -> W1;
(d) preservation of apartness by u1 and u2;
(e) the specified cocone equations;
(f) absence of x # z in W2 and absence of z # y in W1.

3. PROOF-SKELETON CHECK
From x # y in B and apartness preservation by i, infer i(x) # i(y) in P.
By A3 in P, infer i(x) # j(z) OR j(z) # i(y).
In the first branch, u2 maps the alleged apartness to x # z in W2, contradicting its stipulated absence.
In the second branch, u1 maps the alleged apartness to z # y in W1, contradicting its stipulated absence.
Thus the assumed coproduct P cannot exist, provided all premises above hold in the same category and the stated cocone maps are valid morphisms.
