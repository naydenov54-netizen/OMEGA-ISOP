5. MINIMAL RL PATH: RESULT TO REGISTRATION

Purpose: make a valid RL candidate registration-ready early, without waiting for unrelated audits or CE-V integration.

STEP 1 - SELECT
Select one specific result or tightly connected result set. Give it a working title and one-sentence claim. Do not wait for a full-project audit.

STEP 2 - CLASSIFY
Identify the evidence class and exact source/version. State whether the item is a theorem, conditional result, computational/numerical result, empirical result, conjecture, or other record type. Do not inflate its class.

STEP 3 - CHECK THE CLAIM
Check only what this claim needs: definitions and assumptions; domain/types; derivation or method; relevant counterexample/boundary conditions; provenance; Frozen Core compatibility; and reproducibility where applicable. Mark each item CHECKED, NOT CHECKED, NOT APPLICABLE, or UNRESOLVED. A missing optional check is not a blocker.

STEP 4 - PREPARE THE RL RECORD
Prepare the record immediately using the compact template in Section 6. Do not wait for optional review, universal T-tests, CE-V promotion, or canonical acceptance unless an authoritative rule for this item explicitly requires it.

STEP 5 - DECIDE
Choose SEND, REVISE, HOLD, or BLOCK for this specific item, with one concrete reason. SEND means ready for the next applicable submission step, not automatically registered. HOLD is permitted only for a named dependency that is genuinely necessary for that step. BLOCK requires a material defect in the stated claim or a mandatory requirement that is demonstrably unmet.

STEP 6 - SUBMIT / REGISTER
Identify the authoritative RL corpus, its actual entry schema, the authorized write interface, and any item-specific mandatory requirements from authoritative project/registry documentation. Submit through that interface when it is available and authorized. Never invent an RL-ID or registry requirement. If the registry assigns the ID, use the assigned ID; until then use RL-ID: PENDING REGISTRY ASSIGNMENT.

STEP 7 - READ BACK
After submission, retrieve the record from the authoritative registry. Verify the assigned identifier, title/claim, content, evidence class, source/traceability, and status against the submitted packet. Correct discrepancies and read back again.

STEP 8 - SET STATUS
Use RL_REGISTERED only after authoritative readback verifies both the registry identifier and the registered content. Before then use the precise applicable status, such as RL_PACKET_PREPARED, RL_SUBMISSION_PENDING, RL_REGISTRATION_REQUIRED, or RL_UNREGISTERED. RL_REGISTRATION_REQUIRED is not RL_REGISTERED.

Keep the prepared packet, complete any independent work, and report: (a) the exact blocked action; (b) the missing access, schema, instruction, or authorization; (c) why it is necessary; (d) what evidence was checked; and (e) the smallest action that would unblock registration.
