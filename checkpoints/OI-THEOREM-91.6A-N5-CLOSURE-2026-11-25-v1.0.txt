2. COMPLETED RESEARCH RESULT
==================================================

THEOREM 91.6A v0.3-N5-CORRECTED has been archived as a new research-development version.

The N5 correction explicitly states the required positive-gain premise:

A injective iff gamma_A > 0

for the stated finite-dimensional Euclidean setting.

For a fixed observation y, if x1 and x2 are both compatible with the deterministic bounded-noise model

y = A(x) + eta
||eta|| <= epsilon

then:

A(x1-x2) = eta2-eta1

and therefore:

||A(x1-x2)|| <= 2*epsilon.

Using the positive minimum gain:

gamma_A*||x1-x2|| <= ||A(x1-x2)||

gives:

||x1-x2|| <= 2*epsilon/gamma_A.

Consequently:

diam(C(y)) <= 2*epsilon/gamma_A.

The proof of sharpness constructs a minimizing unit vector v with:

||v||=1
||A(v)||=gamma_A

and selects:

x1=0
x2=(2*epsilon/gamma_A)*v
y=A(x2)/2.

Both states are compatible with the same observation using noise vectors of norm epsilon, and their distance is exactly 2*epsilon/gamma_A.

Therefore the universal diameter constant is sharp under the stated assumptions.

The closure keeps separate:
- disjointness of two candidate noise balls;
- the sufficient pairwise state-separation condition;
- the diameter of the compatible-state set for one observation.

No unique recovery under nonzero bounded noise is claimed.
