8. LOOP AUDIT B: THEOREM 91

TARGET:
D_L(T(M1),T(M2)) <= D_L(M1,M2).

PREMISES:
H1: R exists, T is admissible, and R is preserved.
H2: Y is normed.
H3: There is one common tau:L_op->L_op such that
Resp_(T(M))(a)=Resp_M(tau(a)).
H4: The operational criterion and comparison class remain fixed.
The operational probe set is nonempty as required by the operational-distance definition.

DEPENDENCY:
For each a in L_op:
||Resp_(T(M1))(a)-Resp_(T(M2))(a)||
=
||Resp_M1(tau(a))-Resp_M2(tau(a))||.
Since tau(a) belongs to L_op:
the right-hand quantity is bounded above by
sup over b in L_op of
||Resp_M1(b)-Resp_M2(b)||.
Taking the supremum over a gives the claimed inequality.

CONCLUSION:
Theorem 91 is a valid conditional non-expansion result under H1-H4 and the stated operational-domain conditions.

LOOP CHECK:
H3 is a substantive response-covariance premise. It is not derived by Theorem 91. H1 is also a substantive admissibility/preservation premise. The theorem does not prove H1 or H3 from the Frozen Core.

COUNTERMODEL / PREMISE REMOVAL:
If tau is not constrained to map L_op into L_op, the transformed response may sample probes outside the comparison supremum; the inequality need not follow.
If no common tau works for both models, the pointwise comparison cannot be placed under one common supremum in the stated way.
If the response identity is assumed only for one model or one probe, the universal comparison is incomplete.

CLASSIFICATION:
CONDITIONAL RESULT; NON-CIRCULAR when H1-H4 are independently stated assumptions.
INCOMPLETE if presented as deriving H1, H3, or stochastic relational preservation from the theorem itself.

NON-IMPLICATIONS:
No universal non-expansion law.
