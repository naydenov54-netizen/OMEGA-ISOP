============================================================
1. OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Continue S3.9 by testing whether preservation of a hereditary relational constraint class on the quotient pushout Q implies preservation after pulling the quotient structure back to the underlying set pushout S_P.

Use a finite relational signature and a class K defined by a single finite forbidden induced structure. The test must distinguish:

(A) preservation by the free relational amalgam on S_P;
(B) preservation by the quotient-level amalgam Q;
(C) preservation by the quotient-pullback structure on S_P.

============================================================
2. SIGNATURE AND FORBIDDEN STRUCTURE
============================================================

Use the finite relational signature:
Sigma={R},
where R is binary.

Let F be the three-element Sigma-structure:
S_F={u,v,w},
R_F=S_F x S_F.

Thus R_F contains all nine ordered pairs:
(u,u),(u,v),(u,w),
(v,u),(v,v),(v,w),
(w,u),(w,v),(w,w).

F is irreducible in the relational sense used in S3.4:
for every pair of distinct elements x,y in F, the tuple (x,y) belongs to R_F and therefore a relation tuple contains both x and y.

Define:
K=Forb(F),

where Forb(F) is the class of all Sigma-structures containing no induced copy of F.

K is hereditary under induced substructures.

Under the single-finite-forbidden-structure criterion established in S3.4-T1, irreducibility of F implies that K has the free amalgamation property for induced embeddings in the pure relational setting.

============================================================
3. INPUT SPAN
============================================================

Let:
S_C={c},
S_A={c,a},
S_B={c,b}.

Let all apartness relations be empty:
#_A=#_B=#_C=empty.

Then all elements on each side are equivalent under ~.

Define the binary relations:
R_A=S_A x S_A,
R_B=S_B x S_B,
R_C={(c,c)}.

The embeddings of C into A and B are induced embeddings for both apartness and R.

The side relations agree on C.

Coordinatewise invariance holds on each side because each side relation is universal on its carrier.

Membership:
A in K, because |S_A|=2 and F has three elements.
B in K, because |S_B|=2 and F has three elements.
C in K, because |S_C|=1.

============================================================
4. FREE RELATIONAL AMALGAM ON THE UNDERLYING SET PUSHOUT
============================================================

The underlying set pushout is:
S_P={c,a,b}.

The free relational amalgam is:
R_P^free=R_A union R_B.

Explicitly:
R_P^free =
{(c,c),(c,a),(a,c),(a,a),(c,b),(b,c),(b,b)}.

The two mixed tuples are absent:
(a,b) NOT IN R_P^free,
(b,a) NOT IN R_P^free.

The only possible three-element induced copy of F in S_P would use all of {c,a,b}. It is not a copy of F because R_P^free omits (a,b) and (b,a).

Therefore:
P_free in K.

This is the expected free-amalgamation preservation result for this span.

============================================================
5. QUOTIENT PUSHOUT
============================================================

Because all apartness relations are empty, each side quotient is a singleton:
Q_A={q_A},
Q_B={q_B},
Q_C={q_C}.

The quotient pushout is:
Q={q}.

The descended quotient relations are:
Rbar_A={(q_A,q_A)},
Rbar_B={(q_B,q_B)},
Rbar_C={(q_C,q_C)}.

The quotient free amalgam has:
Rbar_Q={(q,q)}.

Since Q has one element and F has three elements:
Q in K.

Thus the quotient-level relational amalgam belongs to K.

============================================================
6. QUOTIENT PULLBACK TO S_P
============================================================

The canonical map is:
pi_P:S_P->Q,

with:
pi_P(c)=pi_P(a)=pi_P(b)=q.

Pull back Rbar_Q:
R_P^pull(x,y) IFF Rbar_Q(pi_P(x),pi_P(y)).

Since Rbar_Q={(q,q)} and every element maps to q:
R_P^pull=S_P x S_P.

Therefore:
R_P^pull=R_P^free union {(a,b),(b,a)}.

In particular, the induced substructure on {c,a,b} is exactly F.

Hence:
P_pull NOT IN K.

============================================================
7. COUNTEREXAMPLE RESULT
============================================================

The same input span satisfies:
A in K,
B in K,
C in K.

The free relational amalgam satisfies:
P_free in K.

The quotient-level relational amalgam satisfies:
Q in K.

But the quotient-pullback structure does not satisfy:
P_pull NOT IN K.

Therefore the implication:

Q in K
IMPLIES
the quotient-pullback structure on S_P belongs to K

is FALSE in general, even when:
- Sigma is finite and purely relational;
- F is finite and irreducible;
- K=Forb(F) has the free amalgamation property;
- the side embeddings are induced;
- the side relations agree on C;
- each side relation is coordinatewise invariant under its apartness-equivalence relation;
- the quotient maps and set pushouts have the injectivity and exact-intersection properties of P2.3.

EVIDENCE_CLASS:
EXPLICIT FINITE COUNTEREXAMPLE WITH DIRECT VERIFICATION.

============================================================
8. RELATION TO S3.9 AND P2.3-P2.4
============================================================

This result sharpens S3.9.4-S3.9.6.

P2.3:
NOT REFUTED. The apartness strong amalgam remains valid in this example.

P2.4.1:
NOT REFUTED. The free relational amalgam on S_P exists and belongs to K.

P2.4.2:
Sidewise coordinatewise invariance is satisfied and each relation descends to its side quotient. Nevertheless, the quotient pullback adds mixed tuples.

S3.8-T1:
Its quotient-level conclusion Q in K is true in this example. That conclusion does not imply that the quotient-pullback structure on S_P is in K.

S3.9 FIBER-SATURATION CONDITION:
The free union relation is not saturated under pi_P-fibers:
pi_P(a)=pi_P(b),
R_P^free(a,a)=TRUE,
R_P^free(a,b)=FALSE.

This is exactly why the quotient pullback differs from the free union and leaves K.

============================================================
9. LOGICAL AUDIT
============================================================

TARGET:
For every hereditary class K of finite relational structures, if A and B belong to K and their quotient-level relational amalgam Q belongs to K, then the quotient-pullback structure on S_P also belongs to K.

STATUS:
REFUTED.

COUNTERMODEL:
The finite structure F and the span A,B,C defined in Sections 2-3 satisfy all stated side assumptions, while Q belongs to K and P_pull does not.

NO CIRCULARITY:
The failure is demonstrated by explicit carriers and relation tables. Membership and non-membership are checked directly by induced-substructure comparison. No desired conclusion is assumed in the construction.

============================================================
10. LIMITATIONS AND NON-IMPLICATIONS
============================================================

This counterexample does not show that every quotient-pullback amalgam fails.

It does not show that every hereditary relational class fails under quotient pullback.

It does not invalidate the free relational amalgam on S_P.

It does not invalidate P2.3 as an apartness strong amalgamation construction.

It does not invalidate the sidewise quotient-descent criterion of P2.4.2.

It shows that quotient-level preservation of K is insufficient, by itself, to establish preservation by the pullback structure on S_P.

The fiber-saturation condition from S3.9 is a sufficient-and-necessary condition for equality of the free union relation and the quotient pullback relation. Whether a weaker condition suffices for preservation of a particular K remains class-dependent and requires separate testing.
