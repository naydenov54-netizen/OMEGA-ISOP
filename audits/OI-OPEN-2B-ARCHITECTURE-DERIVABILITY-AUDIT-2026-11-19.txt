1. PURPOSE AND QUESTION
============================================================

Determine whether the existing OMEGA-ISOP source documents provide enough formal information to derive, without adding arbitrary assumptions or choosing a convenient test category, the architecture required to turn the OPEN-2 / Theorem 91 witness into a genuine architectural realization.

The required chain is:

C_METACORE
-> admissible configurations
-> compatibility CC
-> diagram D_CC
-> R = colim(D_CC)
-> Real(R)
-> Phi : Real(R) -> M
-> X_R
-> A(R;Phi)
-> admissible T and preservation of R
-> End(X,R), where applicable.

The audit does not attempt to construct missing objects by invention. It distinguishes source-defined concepts from incomplete specifications, uninstantiated constructions, and absent formal definitions.

No project-owner choice is inferred.

3. SOURCE-LEVEL FINDINGS
============================================================

3.1 CONFIGURATIONS AND C_METACORE

Volume II III.4 gives a local configuration representation:

C = (S_C,#_C)
S_C subseteq U0
#_C = # restricted to S_C x S_C.

It states |S_C| >= 2 for a minimal nontrivial configuration, but also says that exact admissibility conditions remain realization-dependent.

Volume II III.5 defines an embedding f:C1 -> C2 as injective and preserving and reflecting apartness:

x #1 y iff f(x) #2 f(y).

Volume II III.4 describes C_METACORE as a category whose objects are admissible configurations and whose morphisms preserve selected structural properties.

Finding:
- The local configuration representation is DEFINED.
- A local embedding template is DEFINED.
- The complete admissibility predicate Adm(C) is UNDER-SPECIFIED.
- The exhaustive hom-class Hom_C_METACORE is UNDER-SPECIFIED.
- The complete categorical signature and preservation policy are UNDER-SPECIFIED.

The source does not establish that every local apartness embedding is a C_METACORE morphism. It also does not establish that the only morphisms are literal inclusions or restrictions of global automorphisms.

Volume III Section 6 supplies a nontriviality test. It does not declare that test to be the complete, realization-independent admissibility predicate.

3.2 COMPATIBILITY CC

Volume II IV.1 defines:

CC(C1,C2) = 1 iff an admissible amalgamation of C1 and C2 exists;
otherwise CC(C1,C2) = 0.

The source calls the binary form a minimal representation and does not claim it is the unique possible representation.

Finding:
- The binary notation is DEFINED.
- The full meaning of admissible amalgamation is UNDER-SPECIFIED.
- The source does not settle whether the input is a pair with a common-target witness or a span with a designated common base and two maps.
- The target object's admissibility, the morphisms, and any agreement equation are not completely specified by the Boolean value.

A Boolean compatibility predicate is not, by itself, a categorical diagram.

3.3 DIAGRAM D_CC

Volume II IV.2 says that D_CC denotes the diagram generated by selected configurations and their admissible compatibility relations.

Volume III Section 9 explicitly states that the notation D_CC does not itself define a complete categorical construction. A complete realization requires:
- an indexing category J;
- a functor D:J -> C_METACORE;
- specified objects and morphisms;
- specified compatibility conditions;
- the required universal property.

Finding:
- D_CC is NAMED and conceptually DESCRIBED.
- The project-specific indexing category J is NOT SPECIFIED.
- The project-specific functor D is NOT SPECIFIED.
- The object and arrow assignments are NOT SPECIFIED.
- Functoriality cannot be checked without those assignments.

Classification:
D_CC is UNDER-SPECIFIED as a project-level categorical diagram.
The missing data are explicit formalization requirements, not details that may be inferred from a Boolean CC matrix.

3.4 RELATIONAL STRUCTURE R

Volume II IV.2 gives the conditional construction:

R := colim(D_CC).

Volume II IV.3 states that this requires a valid compatibility diagram, the relevant categorical structure, and existence of the required colimit or an equivalent constraint-preserving construction. It expressly states that R existence is conditional.

Volume III Sections 8-9 also require the explicit diagram and universal property.

Finding:
- The abstract colimit prescription is DEFINED CONDITIONALLY.
- A project-specific D_CC has not been instantiated by the inspected source.
- No project-level cocone and no universal-property proof are supplied in the inspected sources.
- No general existence theorem is asserted.

Classification:
R = colim(D_CC) is DEFINED AS A CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTION, BUT THE PROJECT-LEVEL INSTANCE IS NOT INSTANTIATED.
Its existence for the intended project diagram is NOT ESTABLISHED.
This is not proof that R cannot exist.

3.5 REALIZATION SPACE Real(R) AND MAP Phi

Volume II II and V.1 state:

Phi : Real(R) -> M

and describe Real(R) as admissible mathematical realizations of R. Phi represents a realization choice.

Finding:
- The abstract role and type of Phi are STATED.
- The construction of Real(R), its elements, admissibility predicate, and equivalence notion are not formally specified in the inspected source.
- No particular R has been constructed for the OPEN-2 witness.
- No particular realization object in Real(R) is supplied.
- No concrete Phi is supplied for the quantum witness.

Classification:
Real(R) is NAMED but UNDER-SPECIFIED as a constructible mathematical space.
Phi is ABSTRACTLY TYPED but NOT INSTANTIATED for the OPEN-2 witness.

3.6 REALIZED STATE SPACE X_R

Volume II V.2 states:

X_R = {x in X | x satisfies the constraints induced by R}.

Finding:
- The set-builder form is DEFINED.
- The phrase "satisfies the constraints induced by R" has no explicit constraint family, satisfaction relation, or evaluation rule in the inspected source.
- Without a constructed R and a realization Phi, X_R cannot be computed or identified.
- The OPEN-2 witness uses X as its state space but does not define X_R or prove X_R = X.

Classification:
X_R is ABSTRACTLY DEFINED but UNDER-SPECIFIED and NOT INSTANTIATED in the witness.

3.7 ADMISSIBLE DYNAMICS A(R;Phi)

Volume II II and V.2-V.3 state:

A(R;Phi) subseteq Evol(X).

A(R;Phi) is the class of evolution laws compatible with the realized relational structure. The source requires A(R;Phi) to be nonempty for a dynamically admissible realization. It then states that T in A(R;Phi) is compatible with the relational constraints in the selected realization.

Finding:
- The abstract type and intended role of A(R;Phi) are DEFINED.
- The exact compatibility predicate for membership is not given in a form that can be applied to the OPEN-2 quantum channel.
- The nonemptiness condition is explicit, but no proof for the OPEN-2 architecture follows without R and Phi.
- The witness does not prove T in A(R;Phi).

Classification:
A(R;Phi) is ABSTRACTLY DEFINED but UNDER-SPECIFIED for the proposed witness.
A(R;Phi) != empty and T in A(R;Phi) are NOT ESTABLISHED.

3.8 End(X,R) AND Aut(X,R)

Volume II V.3 says:

A reversible realization may be represented by T in Aut(X,R).

An irreversible compatibility-preserving evolution may be represented by an appropriate endomorphism class:

T in End(X,R).

Finding:
- The symbols Aut(X,R) and End(X,R) are NAMED.
- The phrase "appropriate endomorphism class" is not a formal definition.
- No objects, arrows, composition, identity, preservation condition, or relation to the colimit cocone are specified for End(X,R).
- No source passage equates preservation of an arbitrary equivalence relation on X with membership in End(X,R).

Classification:
End(X,R) is NOT FORMALLY DEFINED in the inspected sources.
The statement that T preserves the witness relation R_V does not establish T in End(X,R).

3.9 VOLUME IV AND VOLUME V CROSS-CHECK

The recovered Volume IV repeats the constraint-to-dynamics relation and preserves the open status of general construction and existence of R. Its recovered sections do not provide the missing categorical specification. The Volume IV source explicitly warns that it is a partial recovery.

Volume V preserves the canonical notation:
Phi : Real(R) -> M
A(R;Phi) subseteq Evol(X)
and the dependency chain from configurations through compatibility and R to dynamics and operational response.

Volume V also lists the general existence theorem for R as OPEN and preserves the distinction between admissibility and realization.

Neither Volume IV nor Volume V supplies the missing project-specific J, D, full C_METACORE hom-class, amalgam witness definition, colimit cocone, Real(R) construction, or End(X,R) definition.

============================================================
6. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE OPEN-2 WITNESS
============================================================

The quantum witness establishes an internally correct, model-specific construction involving:
- a qubit state space X;
- an externally selected equivalence relation R_V;
- an amplitude-damping channel T;
- preservation of R_V;
- an operational response map and its pullback;
- the response-distance non-expansion result.

The witness does not establish:
- that R_V is the architecture's R=colim(D_CC);
- that R_V is an element or realization of Real(R);
- a realization map Phi for the architectural R;
- the constraint-defined state space X_R;
- A(R;Phi) != empty;
- T in A(R;Phi);
- T in the formally specified End(X,R).

The independent OPEN-2 audit already establishes that the mathematical calculations M1-M9 pass. This source audit does not challenge those calculations. It identifies the missing architectural bridge between that valid quantum example and the OMEGA-ISOP relational construction.

The direction-of-dependence issue also remains:
the witness defines R_V through operational expectation values, whereas the OMEGA-ISOP dependency architecture places R before admissible dynamics and operational response. The current witness therefore cannot be treated as an independent derivation of the relational layer from the architecture.
