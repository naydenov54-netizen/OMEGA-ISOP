3. SCALE-FIXING LEMMA
Suppose two admissible Lorentzian metrics are conformally related:
g_2 = Omega^2 g_1,
where Omega: M -> (0,infinity) is smooth.

In dimension n, the metric volume densities obey:
dvol(g_2) = Omega^n dvol(g_1).

In four dimensions:
dvol(g_2) = Omega^4 dvol(g_1).

If both metrics are required to induce the same specified volume measure mu, then:
dvol(g_1) = mu = dvol(g_2)
implies Omega^4 = 1 pointwise, and positivity of Omega gives:
Omega = 1.
Therefore g_2 = g_1 under the fixed identification.
