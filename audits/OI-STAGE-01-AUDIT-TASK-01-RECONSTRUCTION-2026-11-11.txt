3. AUTHORITATIVE CORE DISTINCTION

The protected Volume II source states:

PD is not itself an entity, physical object, process, relation, or causal mechanism.

The same source separately introduces:

The source explicitly requires preservation of the distinction:
PD = meta-level admissibility of distinction.
# = formal realization of apartness on U0.

FINDING 01:
The candidate binary predicate D is not Primitive Difference PD itself. D is a mathematical relation and can serve as a particular formal realization of apartness when its domain and axioms are specified. Identifying D with PD would be a category error and would contradict the protected source distinction.

STATUS: SOURCE-COMPATIBILITY REQUIREMENT ESTABLISHED.

4. AXIOM COMPATIBILITY

The protected Volume II source gives a standard example of apartness satisfying:

A1 — IRREFLEXIVITY:
not(x # x).

A2 — SYMMETRY:
x # y iff y # x.

A3 — COTRANSITIVITY:
x # y implies (x # z) or (z # y), for all z.

The Stage 1 candidate defines, for a declared nonempty set X, an index set I, feature-value sets V_i, and maps f_i:X→V_i:

D(x,y)=1 iff there exists i∈I such that f_i(x)≠f_i(y).

Otherwise D(x,y)=0.

For this signature-based definition:
- irreflexivity follows because no feature value differs from itself;
- symmetry follows because inequality is symmetric;
- cotransitivity follows by selecting a feature witnessing f_i(x)≠f_i(y), then splitting on whether f_i(x) differs from f_i(z) or equals f_i(z).

The cotransitivity proof is valid in classical set theory. In a constructive foundation, the disjunction step requires the relevant decidable equality or an equivalent constructive apartness/witness structure. No unrestricted constructive claim is made here.

FINDING 02:
The candidate D satisfies the three standard apartness axioms in the declared classical set-theoretic setting. It is therefore compatible with one standard apartness realization.

This is compatibility of mathematical form, not proof that the Core uniquely selects D, its feature family, or its signature construction.

STATUS: PROVED UNDER STATED CLASSICAL SET-THEORETIC ASSUMPTIONS.

5. SIGNATURE DATA ARE ADDITIONAL MODEL INPUT

The candidate requires:
- a carrier X;
- an index set I;
- feature-value sets V_i;
- feature maps f_i:X→V_i.

These data specify which distinctions are represented by D. The protected Core's meta-level PD does not itself supply this feature family or these maps.

Different feature families can induce different D predicates. Different codomains or injective recodings can represent the same indistinguishability classes.

FINDING 03:
The signature-based construction is a selected realization/model input. It is not derivable from PD alone and is not established as the unique or canonical OMEGA-ISOP interpretation of Difference.

STATUS: CONDITIONAL COMPATIBILITY; UNIQUENESS NOT ESTABLISHED.

6. ZERO-KERNEL AND QUOTIENT STRUCTURE

For the candidate D, define:
x ~ y iff D(x,y)=0.

Because D(x,y)=0 exactly when all selected feature values agree, this is equality of signatures and is an equivalence relation. The quotient X/~ is therefore well-defined.

This result is consistent with Volume II, which states that under a complete apartness realization:
x ~ y iff not(x # y),
and that the quotient DS=U0/~ is derived when the selected apartness assumptions make ~ an equivalence relation.

FINDING 04:
The candidate quotient construction is mathematically compatible with the Core's conditional quotient construction, provided the candidate D is explicitly treated as the selected apartness realization on X. It does not establish that X=U0, that D=# in the canonical Core, or that the quotient is a primitive structure.

STATUS: CONDITIONAL COMPATIBILITY.

7. CARRIER AND NONTRIVIALITY BOUNDARY

The Stage 1 candidate specifies X as nonempty.

Nonemptiness alone does not imply that there are two distinct elements, nor that any pair is D-positive. In particular, a nonempty carrier with a constant signature gives D(x,y)=0 for all x,y.

Volume II separately states that a minimal nontrivial local configuration satisfies |S_C|≥2. That condition concerns a nontrivial configuration; it must not be silently transferred to every carrier X or treated as a consequence of PD.

FINDING 05:
The candidate's X≠∅ assumption is preserved. No nontriviality, positive-difference witness, physical realization, or |X|≥2 conclusion follows from it alone.

STATUS: SCOPE CLARIFICATION REQUIRED; NO CONTRADICTION.

8. DIFFERENCE / RELATION BOUNDARY

As a matter of standard mathematics, D's 1-valued extension is itself a binary relation on X. The accurate Stage 1 / Stage 2 distinction is therefore not “predicate versus relation” in the unrestricted mathematical sense.

Stage 1 studies a selected symmetric distinguishability/apartness predicate and its zero-kernel / quotient structure.

The protected Volume II source constructs its relational constraint structure through selected configurations, compatibility relations, a compatibility diagram D_CC, and a colimit R:=colim(D_CC), where the required categorical construction exists.

The candidate D, or a directed relation merely supported on D-positive pairs, is not that Core construction. The additional configuration, compatibility, diagram, and categorical data cannot be omitted.

FINDING 06:
The Stage 1 candidate does not derive the Core's relational constraint structure R. The Stage 2 boundary must preserve the Core's compatibility-colimit construction and must not replace it with an arbitrary directed relation.
