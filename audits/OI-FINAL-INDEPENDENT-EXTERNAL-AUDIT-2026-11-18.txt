3. MATHEMATICAL AND LOGICAL INTEGRATION

RL-021 — PASS
Basis reported: Razor §2 and controlled RL-021 copy.
Witnesses R1 = empty set and R2 = {(b,x)} reportedly produce the same observable I_B^0 and different target P_B.
The domain is correctly restricted to the finite unrestricted relational class.

RL-021.1 — PASS
Basis reported: controlled copy, lines 209-248, and Razor §3.
A numerical check at |X|=2 reportedly gives two counterexamples with the same boundary degrees (1,1) but different Q_B values (0 and 1).
The statistic T_B = sum_x o_B(x)*i_B(x) is sufficient for the binary target.
The claim that C_forward is necessary is correctly refuted by the G_backward witness.
The general H-characterization remains explicitly OPEN and is not represented as proven.

RL-027 — PASS WITHIN THE STATED DOMAIN
Basis reported: RESIDUAL-CONTROL §§3.1-3.5.
For pure states, Tr(rho_A^2) = Tr(rho_B^2). A numerical example with d_A=3 and d_B=5 reportedly gave 0.41628 on both sides.
For fixed rho_B, product states tau_A tensor sigma reportedly realize the range [1/d_A, 1]. The auditor states that the universal bounds and attainability establish the exact range.
The stability estimate
|Q_A(rho) - Q_A(sigma)| <= 2*||rho-sigma||_1
is reported valid, using
Tr(rho^2 - sigma^2) = Tr((rho-sigma)(rho+sigma)).
The auditor notes that stability does not contradict non-identifiability over the full domain because these are different claims.

THEOREM 91 — PASS CONDITIONALLY
Basis reported: Volume II §VIII.5 and RESIDUAL-CONTROL §4.1.
The proof is reported correct: one common tau is used for both models, followed by a supremum over a. Surjectivity is not required.
H1 does not enter the algebraic proof; it is an architectural condition rather than a proof step. Razor §5 is reported to state this correctly.
Full relational realization of H1 remains OPEN (see Section 5).

6. DOCUMENTATION ISSUES

DOC-1. Notation conflict A(Phi(R)).
Location: docs/OMEGA-ISOP-CONDITIONAL-STRUCTURAL-FRAMEWORK-IDENTITY, lines 41 and 45.
The document uses A(Phi(R)), while Volume V NOTATION N2 states that A(Phi(R)) is not used, Phi(R)=A(R) is deleted, and A(R;Phi) is adopted.
Recommended action: align the identity document notation with A(R;Phi).

DOC-2. Outdated next actions concerning primary RL sources.
Locations: Volume V v2.4-C-1.1, lines 224 and 651; FOUNDATION-PROGRAM, lines 420-434 and 522.
These passages say that primary RL sources should be recovered and recommend continuing the search. PROVENANCE-DISPOSITION §4 (2026-11-18) closes recovery as non-recoverable and discontinued.
Recommended action: add a control-index note that the older instructions have been superseded.

DOC-3. Three RL-027 statuses without a unified table.
Locations: Volume V (~lines 299-353), controlled copy, Razor §7, PROVENANCE §6.
The reported statuses are UNDER AUDIT, RL_REGISTERED, and the separated audit/registration/readback dispositions.
Recommended action: add one control-index row with four separate dimensions: mathematics / overall audit / registration / readback.

DOC-4. Terminology and numbering.
- RESIDUAL-CONTROL §3.6 uses "full mixed global domain" for D_all = S(H_A tensor H_B). Recommended term: "full state domain".
- Volume V §VII and CE-V archive line 491 use "CE-V-004", while other entries use CE-V-01..05. The numbering is inconsistent, although described as corrected numbering.
Recommended action: add a numbering note or standardize the format without changing the underlying record identity.

DOC-5. Normed space versus metric space.
Volume II H2 and Volume V N4 require a normed Y. Razor §5 shows that a metric space is sufficient.
The auditor does not classify this as an error because the normed-space assumption is stronger than necessary.
Recommended action: note explicitly that H2 is stronger than required.

DOC-6. Volume II Frozen Core label and documentation disclaimer.
Volume II line ~7 identifies it as a "FROZEN CORE COMPONENT"; its preface line ~1398 says it is a "documentation snapshot" that does not independently validate mathematical claims.
The auditor considers these statements compatible (frozen does not mean independently certified), but recommends an explicit cross-reference to the upper label.
