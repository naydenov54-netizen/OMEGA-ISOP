2. WHAT VOLUME II ACTUALLY DEFINES

III.2 — APARTNESS
A standard example of an apartness relation satisfies:
- irreflexivity: NOT(x # x)
- symmetry: x # y IFF y # x
- cotransitivity: x # y IMPLIES (x # z) OR (z # y), for all z.

The source explicitly says these axioms are ONE ADMISSIBLE REALIZATION, not the only possible realization.

III.3 — INDISTINGUISHABILITY
The source definition is:
x ~ y IFF no admissible apartness witness separates x and y.

Under a complete apartness realization, this MAY be represented as:
x ~ y IFF NOT(x # y).

Under the standard apartness assumptions, ~ can be shown to satisfy equivalence-relation properties. The quotient DS := U0 / ~ is derived only when ~ is an equivalence relation.

Consequences:
- Do not silently identify the witness-based definition with Boolean complement in every realization.
- A1-A3 plus classical Boolean negation support the equivalence result only in the stated standard complete Boolean realization.
- The Frozen Core and Volume II do not make A1-A3 a universal requirement across every possible realization.

III.4 — LOCAL CONFIGURATIONS
The source defines:
C = (S_C, #_C)
where S_C is a subset of U0 and #_C is the restriction of # to S_C x S_C.

A minimal nontrivial configuration satisfies:
|S_C| >= 2.

The exact admissibility conditions remain realization-dependent.

This is a definition of a local configuration, NOT a definition of relational decomposition or indecomposability.

III.5 — CONFIGURATION EMBEDDINGS
An embedding f:C1->C2 is injective and preserves AND reflects the selected apartness:
x #1 y IFF f(x) #2 f(y).

This does not specify a complete embedding notion for arbitrary relational expansions with additional relation symbols and coupling axioms.

IV.1 — COMPATIBILITY
CC(C1,C2)=1 iff an admissible amalgamation of C1 and C2 exists; otherwise CC(C1,C2)=0.

The source calls this a minimal representation of compatibility, not a unique possible representation.

IV.2 — COMPATIBILITY DIAGRAM
D_CC denotes the diagram generated by selected configurations and their admissible compatibility relations.

Where the required categorical construction exists:
R := colim(D_CC).

The resulting R is a relational constraint structure generated from the compatibility structure.

IV.3 — EXISTENCE BOUNDARY
R requires:
- a valid compatibility diagram;
- the relevant categorical structure;
- existence of the required colimit or an equivalent constraint-preserving construction.

R is not automatically defined if these conditions fail.
