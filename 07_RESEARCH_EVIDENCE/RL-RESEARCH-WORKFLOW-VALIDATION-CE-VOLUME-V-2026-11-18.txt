PURPOSE
This document records the research workflow agreed for OMEGA-ISOP. It is a process guide, not a new theorem, scientific claim, or change to the Frozen Core.

1. RL — RESEARCH LABORATORY
RL is the exploratory research environment. Investigations may examine mathematical structures, definitions, theorems, proofs, constructions, dependencies, counterexamples, boundary cases, and potential applications.

An idea or exploratory observation is not automatically a result. Research must distinguish conjecture, preliminary reasoning, demonstrated result, and unresolved question.

2. RL RESEARCH RECORD — RECORDING GROUNDED RESULTS
When an investigation produces a sufficiently grounded result, record it with its actual status and traceability.

A record should include, as appropriate:
- research question and key;
- exact definitions and assumptions;
- domain and scope;
- formal construction;
- proof, derivation, witness, or counterexample;
- verification and failure tests;
- corrections to earlier formulations;
- established results;
- open questions;
- limitations and non-claims;
- status and traceability.

Negative results and non-identifiability results are valid research outcomes. Counterexamples and failed conjectures should be retained where they clarify the scope.

3. VALIDATION
A result is evaluated for:
- logical and mathematical correctness;
- consistency of definitions and assumptions;
- boundary and degenerate cases;
- counterexamples and failure tests;
- reproducibility or independent checking where feasible;
- compatibility with established results;
- precise scope and limitations.

Validation must not silently broaden a result beyond its assumptions. Any unresolved issue must remain explicit.

4. CE — CONFIRMED / ESTABLISHED
Only results that pass the applicable validation criteria may be promoted to CE status.

CE is not awarded because an idea is interesting, useful-looking, or elegantly formulated. Promotion requires evidence appropriate to the claim and a documented status decision.

CE status does not by itself imply physical interpretation, empirical confirmation, universality, or eligibility for Volume V.

5. VOLUME V — SELECTIVE INTEGRATION
A CE result is not automatically included in Volume V.

Volume V integration is selective and requires a separate assessment of:
- mathematical and structural significance to OMEGA-ISOP;
- relevance to the architecture or other established results;
- non-duplication;
- compatibility and traceability;
- suitable placement and documentation;
- preservation of its actual evidential status.

Only results that merit integration are entered into Volume V. Research records may remain in RL/CE without Volume V promotion.

6. PHYSICAL INTERPRETATION AND APPLICATION
Where research is intended to support physics or cosmology, the workflow is:
mathematical result → explicit physical bridge and assumptions → model → derivation → testable consequences.

A mathematical construction is not automatically a physical law. Physical, causal, dynamical, empirical, or cosmological interpretations require their own explicit justification and validation.

8. STATUS AND GOVERNANCE
- This workflow is a research procedure.
- It does not modify Frozen Core.
- It does not automatically alter existing RL, CE, or Volume V records.
- It does not change the evidential status of any existing result.
- Promotion decisions must be traceable and explicitly recorded.
- Open problems remain open until supported resolution.
- No step may be skipped merely to accelerate integration.
