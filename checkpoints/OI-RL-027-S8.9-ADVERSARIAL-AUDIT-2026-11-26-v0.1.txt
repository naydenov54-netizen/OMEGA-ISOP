2. MIN: INDEPENDENT EPIGRAPH-QP COMPARISON
==================================================

The nonsmooth constraint:
sum_i(p_i-q_i)_+ <= epsilon
was independently reformulated with auxiliary variables s_i:

s_i>=0,
q_i+s_i>=p_i,
sum_i s_i<=epsilon.

The objective remains:
minimize sum_i q_i^2,
with q_i>=0 and sum_i q_i=1.

This is a convex quadratic objective with linear constraints.

Executed comparison:
- 2,000 random cases;
- d=2..8;
- r=1..d;
- random Dirichlet spectra;
- eta sampled in the feasible interval;
- SLSQP applied to the epigraph formulation.

Result:
- 2,000/2,000 solver runs successful;
- maximum absolute purity discrepancy between S8.5 formula and epigraph-QP solution:
  2.22e-15.

A preliminary direct SLSQP formulation using the nonsmooth hinge constraint showed small objective discrepancies in 9/3,000 runs, with a maximum discrepancy around 9.31e-6. The linear epigraph formulation removed this discrepancy in the 2,000-case comparison. This is recorded as solver sensitivity, not as a counterexample to S8.5.

3. MAX: EXACT RATIONAL VERTEX ENUMERATION
==================================================

For fixed r, the constraint:
sum_i(p_i-q_i)_+<=epsilon
is equivalent to the finite family:
sum_{i in S}(p_i-q_i)<=epsilon
for every subset S of {1,...,r}.

Together with:
q_i>=0,
sum_i q_i=1,
this gives an explicit rational polytope whenever p and eta are rational.

All candidate vertices were enumerated using exact Fraction arithmetic in dimensions d=2..5.

Executed comparison:
- 140 rational test cases;
- r=1..d;
- 50,029 feasible vertices checked;
- the S8.6 greedy candidate's exact rational purity equalled the maximum vertex purity in every case.

Result: ZERO mismatches.

This is finite-dimensional computational evidence. The general proof remains the majorization argument in S8.6.

5. SPECTRAL REDUCTION AND PURE-STATE REALIZATION
==================================================

Let sigma_B and beta_B be density operators with descending eigenvalue lists p and q.

For Hermitian matrices, the trace-norm eigenvalue inequality gives:
sum_i |p_i-q_i| <= ||sigma_B-beta_B||_1.

Therefore:
D_spectral(p,q) <= D(sigma_B,beta_B).

This proves that every operator-feasible beta_B produces a spectral vector feasible for the scalar trace-distance constraint.

Conversely, for any probability vector q supported on at most r eigenvalue slots, choose beta_B diagonal in an eigenbasis of sigma_B with diagonal q. Then:
D(sigma_B,beta_B)=1/2 sum_i|p_i-q_i|.

Thus the scalar spectral feasible set has commuting operator representatives and gives the exact extrema, not merely an outer bound.

Finally, every density operator beta_B with rank(beta_B)<=r<=d_A admits a purification on A tensor B by Schmidt decomposition. Hence both commuting extremizers are realizable as B-marginals of pure global states.

This closes the logical bridge from the scalar rank-constrained optimization to the pure-global-state domain, under finite-dimensional assumptions.
