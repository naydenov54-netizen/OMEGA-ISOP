2. FROZEN APARTNESS FRAMEWORK RECOVERED

The declared Boolean apartness structure is A=(U0,#), with # : U0 x U0 -> {true,false}.
The standard apartness realization used in the source satisfies:
A1 IRREFLEXIVITY: NOT(x # x).
A2 SYMMETRY: x # y IFF y # x.
A3 COTRANSITIVITY: x # y IMPLIES (x # z) OR (z # y), for every z.

The indistinguishability relation is defined by:
x ~ y IFF NOT(x # y),
under the complete Boolean apartness realization. The source states that A1-A3 establish that ~ is an equivalence relation. Its quotient is DS=U0/~.

LOGIC BOUNDARY: The handoff explicitly uses classical logic and Boolean negation. Do not silently generalize the derivation to intuitionistic or non-Boolean apartness.

3. CORRECTION TO CHECKPOINT 001

Checkpoint 001 correctly warned that irreflexivity and symmetry of # alone do not imply transitivity of ~. However, the actual OMEGA-ISOP apartness framework recovered here includes A3 cotransitivity. Therefore, under the frozen A1-A3 assumptions and classical Boolean logic, the triple:

4. RECOVERED EMBEDDING AND AMALGAM FRAMEWORK

A configuration embedding is injective and preserves AND reflects apartness:
x #1 y IFF f(x) #2 f(y).
Preservation alone is insufficient.

Handoff P2.3 constructs a quotient-based strong amalgam:
Q_A=S_A/~_A, Q_B=S_B/~_B, Q_C=S_C/~_C.
Take the pushout Q of Q_A and Q_B over Q_C.
Take the pushout S_P of the underlying sets S_A and S_B over S_C.
Define pi_P on the two component images using the quotient maps into Q.
Then define:
x #_P y IFF pi_P(x) != pi_P(y).

The handoff records that P satisfies A1-A3 and that the component embeddings preserve and reflect apartness, with intersection exactly the common image of C.

LIMITATIONS: This is a strong amalgam, not a proof of categorical pushout in the category of apartness structures. It does not establish amalgamation for arbitrary added relations, constraints, operations, or dynamics.

5. RELATIONAL EXPANSIONS AND THE STAGE-3 GAP

P2.4.1: For pure relational signatures, the free relational amalgam interprets each relation as the union of the A-side and B-side tuples, leaving mixed tuples false. Source status: proved only when there are NO extra axioms coupling those relations to apartness, and when the components agree on the common substructure.

P2.4.2: For an n-ary relation R, coordinatewise ~-invariance is:
(x1~y1 AND ... AND xn~yn) IMPLIES (R(x1,...,xn) IFF R(y1,...,yn)).
This is necessary and sufficient for R to descend to a well-defined relation on S/~. It is NOT by itself a compatibility criterion for arbitrary constraint theories. The quotient-level relational amalgamation remains conditional and requires proof audit.
