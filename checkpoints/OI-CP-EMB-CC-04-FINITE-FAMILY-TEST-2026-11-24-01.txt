4. CATEGORY-THEORETIC TEST MODEL
============================================================

Define the infinite dihedral group:

D_infinity = <a,b | a^2=e, b^2=e>

Let C be the one-object category whose endomorphism monoid is D_infinity.
Composition is group multiplication.

Define two finite subcategories on the same object X:

D_1: Hom_D1(X,X) = {e,a}
D_2: Hom_D2(X,X) = {e,b}

Each is a finite category because a^2=e and b^2=e.

Each diagram has an individual finite witness:
W_1 = D_1
W_2 = D_2

Any subcategory W of C containing both D_1 and D_2 must contain a and b and be closed under composition. Hence it contains (ab)^n for every integer n >= 0.

In D_infinity, ab has infinite order. Therefore W has infinitely many morphisms.

Conclusion within this explicitly specified category/subcategory witness convention:
Two finite diagrams can each have a finite individual witness while every common subcategory witness containing both is infinite.

This is a valid counterexample to the unrestricted inference:
“finite family + finite individual subcategory witnesses => finite common subcategory witness.”

It is NOT yet a CC-specific counterexample because the exact EMB_CC-03 CC-sufficiency predicate and its witness object have not been recovered.

============================================================
6. CONDITIONAL FINITENESS LEMMA
============================================================

A positive finite-family theorem can be obtained under an additional finite-closure condition.

Suppose:
(1) The family D_1,...,D_n is finite.
(2) Each D_i has a finite witness W_i.
(3) The union of the W_i is contained in a common admissible witness construction.
(4) The closure of the finite union under all required witness operations is finite.

Then the resulting common witness is finite.

Condition (4) is substantive and is not implied merely by the finiteness of the W_i.
Condition (3) is also substantive: individually admissible witnesses need not be jointly compatible.

This is a conditional lemma schema, not a theorem of canonical OMEGA-ISOP until the witness and closure notions are fixed.
