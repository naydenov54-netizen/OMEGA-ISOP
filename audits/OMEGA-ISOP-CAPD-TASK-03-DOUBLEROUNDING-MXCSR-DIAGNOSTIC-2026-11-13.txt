The initial MXCSR before each individual rounding function was:
0x00001F80 (DEC 8064), RC=0.

The observed results were identical under O0 and O2:

Function          MXCSR after       Decimal   RC
ROUND_NEAREST     0x00001FBF        8127      0
ROUND_DOWN        0x00003FBF        16319     1

DoubleRounding::test() returned enum values:
ROUND_NEAREST = 0
ROUND_DOWN    = 1
ROUND_UP      = 2
ROUND_CUT      = 3

For all these observations, x87 Control Word remained 0x037F.

The full MXCSR values, not only RC bits, are retained above and in the raw
output below. In these values, MXCSR status bits 0–5 are set and the
exception-mask bits 7–12 are set. This observation does not establish that
six floating-point exceptions were newly raised by arithmetic; the CAPD
rounding functions write the observed MXCSR state. The status flags and
exception masks must be interpreted separately.
