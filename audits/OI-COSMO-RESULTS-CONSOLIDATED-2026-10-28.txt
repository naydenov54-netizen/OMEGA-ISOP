RESULT 1 — CONFORMAL VOLUME SCALING
For an n-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian metric g and a positive conformal factor Ω, let g' = Ω²g. The metric volume density obeys dvol_g' = Ω^n dvol_g. In four dimensions, dvol_g' = Ω⁴ dvol_g. This follows from the determinant scaling det(g') = Ω^(2n) det(g), hence sqrt(|det(g')|) = Ω^n sqrt(|det(g)|).
Status: established mathematical identity, conditional on the stated smooth metric/conformal setup.

RESULT 2 — INDEPENDENT COMPATIBLE MEASURE CAN FIX A CONFORMAL FACTOR
If g₂ = Ω²g₁ on the same identified n-dimensional manifold, Ω>0, and both metrics induce the same independently fixed positive volume measure μ, then Ω^n=1 almost everywhere in the measure-density sense, and for continuous Ω this gives Ω=1 everywhere. In four dimensions the density ratio is Ω⁴.
Important qualification: μ must be independently fixed and genuinely compatible with both metric volume densities. Defining μ := dvol_g after choosing g does not independently determine g or its scale.
Status: conditional lemma. It does not construct μ from OMEGA-ISOP.

RESULT 3 — FINITE TRANSITIVE SYMMETRY TOY MODEL
Let X be a finite set of n elements with full symmetric group S_n acting on it, and let ν be a measure on P(X) invariant under every permutation. All singleton weights are equal, so ν(A)=|A|w. If additionally ν(X)=1, then w=1/n and ν(A)=|A|/n is the unique invariant probability measure. Without normalization, positive scalar multiples remain invariant.
Status: proved finite result. It establishes relative weights under transitivity and invariance; it does not establish a physical spacetime volume or units of length^4.

RESULT 4 — CONDITIONAL MINKOWSKI SYMMETRY NO-GO
On R^4 with Borel sets, suppose μ is a locally finite Borel measure invariant under all translations. By uniqueness of Haar measure, μ=cλ⁴ for c≥0. If μ is also invariant under all positive uniform dilations x↦sx, then μ(sA)=s⁴μ(A) and invariance forces c=0. Therefore no nonzero locally finite Borel measure is invariant under both all translations and all positive uniform dilations.
Status: proved conditional result for this explicit external model and measure class. Minkowski volume itself is translation-invariant and Lorentz-invariant, but not invariant under uniform dilations. This does not establish that OMEGA-ISOP requires the tested group.

RESULT 5 — SYMMETRY DOES NOT FIX ABSOLUTE NORMALIZATION
For any group action preserving μ, every positive scalar multiple cμ is also invariant whenever the same measure class permits it. Therefore invariance alone cannot fix absolute normalization. Probability normalization may fix a dimensionless scalar under appropriate finite-measure assumptions, but does not identify μ with Lorentzian spacetime volume or calibrate physical units.
Status: general direct consequence of the invariance definition.
