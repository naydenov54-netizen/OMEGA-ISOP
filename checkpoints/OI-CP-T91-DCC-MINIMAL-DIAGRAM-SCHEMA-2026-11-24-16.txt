ESTABLISHED SOURCE FACTS:

A. Objects
Frozen Core 1.6 and Volume II III.4 define local configurations as admissible relational substructures:
C = (S_C,#_C)
where S_C is a subset of the selected carrier and #_C is the restricted apartness structure.
The admissible configurations form, or may be represented by, C_METACORE.

B. Morphisms
Volume II III.5 defines a configuration embedding f:C1->C2 as injective and preserving and reflecting apartness:
x #1 y iff f(x) #2 f(y).

Volume II III.4 and Frozen Core 2.1 describe C_METACORE morphisms more generally as maps preserving the selected structural properties. The sources do not explicitly state that every morphism of every D_CC realization must be an embedding of the exact III.5 type. The selected morphism class must therefore be declared by the realization.

C. Binary compatibility
Frozen Core 1.7 and Volume II IV.1 state:
CC(C1,C2)=1 iff an admissible amalgamation of C1 and C2 exists.
Otherwise CC(C1,C2)=0.

This is a binary minimal representation of compatibility. It does not define all higher-order compatibility data.

D. Relational specification
Frozen Core 1.8 and Volume II IV.2 state:
R := colim(D_CC)
when the required categorical construction exists.

The construction requires a valid compatibility diagram, a relevant categorical structure, and a colimit or an equivalent constraint-preserving construction. Existence is conditional.

E. Formalization boundary
Volume III Section 9 explicitly states that D_CC is not a complete categorical construction by notation alone. A complete realization requires:
J = indexing category
D:J->C_METACORE
the required morphisms and compatibility conditions
the required universal property.

No particular J or universal property beyond this requirement is fixed in that section.

============================================================
MINIMAL PARAMETERIZED SCHEMA
============================================================

The narrowest faithful schema is not one fully specified diagram. It is the following parameterized interface:

(1) Select a category C_METACORE whose objects are admissible configurations.

(2) Select a category J and a functor:
D:J->C_METACORE.

(3) Declare the allowed object and morphism data of J and D. The arrows must be morphisms of the selected C_METACORE realization. When they are configuration embeddings in the sense of Volume II III.5, they must be injective and preserve and reflect apartness.

(4) Declare the compatibility predicate Adm for the selected realization. For a binary pair, its minimal source-level encoding is CC(C1,C2)=1 iff an admissible amalgamation exists.

(5) Supply all compatibility/coherence conditions needed for the complete diagram, not merely the pairwise CC values.

(6) Define the required cocone and universal property if R is a categorical colimit:
For every compatible cocone from D to an object X, there must be a unique factorization through the colimit object R, in the selected category.
This is the ordinary categorical meaning of colimit, not a recovered OMEGA-ISOP-specific strengthening.

(7) If an alternative constraint-preserving representation is used instead of a colimit, specify and verify exactly which compatibility information it preserves. The sources require preservation of the compatibility information required by the selected realization, but do not give a complete formal predicate for that preservation.

(8) Require only the existence conditions stated by the selected construction. No finiteness condition on R or on every admissible target appears in the inspected source clauses.

Important qualification:
The schema above is a formalization interface reconstructed from source requirements. It is not a new canonical definition of D_CC, Adm, or C_METACORE. It does not select a particular J or expand the allowed diagram class.

============================================================
DIAGRAM-SHAPE AND COHERENCE FINDINGS
============================================================

1. The sources do not restrict J to binary spans.

2. The sources do not explicitly authorize every arbitrary finite diagram or every cyclic diagram.

3. The phrase "diagram generated by selected configurations and their admissible compatibility relations" does not specify whether the indexing category is:
- a span-only category;
- a finite graph/category allowing cycles;
- a larger category with additional higher-order morphisms or coherence data.

4. Pairwise compatibility is not enough, by itself, to establish a compatible cocone for a complete multi-object diagram. A higher-order compatibility/coherence condition must be supplied whenever the selected J contains joint constraints not reducible to its pairwise edges.

5. The exact morphism class in a selected D_CC realization must be stated. The existence of configuration embeddings in Volume II III.5 does not prove that every group monomorphism in an external category is a permitted C_METACORE morphism.

6. The ordinary colimit universal property is required if the construction is claimed to be a colimit. The alternative constraint-preserving construction remains possible under Frozen Core 1.8 / Volume II IV.3, but its preservation criterion must be specified.

============================================================
BROWN TRIANGLE CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

Brown's six-object triangle consists of three finite vertex groups and three finite edge groups, with six injective edge-to-vertex maps. In the group category it has an infinite simple direct limit and no finite common target. Each proper two-vertex edge span has a finite completion.

Classification against the recovered OMEGA-ISOP schema:

CANONICAL D_CC STATUS: UNDECIDABLE.

Reasons:
A. J's permitted diagram shapes are not fixed. Therefore the source does not establish whether the cyclic six-object indexing category is admitted or excluded.

B. C_METACORE's realization-specific object and morphism class is not fixed sufficiently to establish that Brown's finite groups and all six group monomorphisms are admissible configurations and configuration embeddings.

C. The realization-specific Adm predicate is not fully specified. Pairwise group-theoretic amalgamation data do not establish that the complete triangle satisfies OMEGA-ISOP higher-order compatibility.

D. The inspected sources do not require R or every common target to be finite. Brown's infinite direct limit therefore does not contradict the general existence of R.

Conditional classification:
- If a selected D_CC realization restricts J to binary spans only, Brown's full triangle is EXCLUDED by diagram shape. This is a conditional statement about that selected realization, not a source-established canonical restriction.
- If a selected realization admits the six-object cyclic diagram, admits the group objects and six monomorphisms as its configurations and morphisms, and its Adm/coherence conditions accept the Brown diagram, then Brown is ADMISSIBLE in that realization and excludes a finite common target for that diagram.
- The inspected canonical source set does not decide between these cases.
