BASIS FOR CLOSURE
1. The domain is explicitly specified as a nonempty topological space (Theta,tau).
2. Definition 91.6D-3 specifies local stability using a neighborhood U of theta_0 in (Theta,tau) and a finite constant K_U >= 0.
3. The pairwise inequality is mathematically well-defined under the stated metric assumptions.
4. The induced map g_U:F(U)->Q is well-defined and unique because the inequality forces q to be constant on each fiber of F restricted to U, and d_Q is a metric.
5. The equivalence between the pairwise inequality and the Lipschitz property of g_U on F(U), with the subspace metric inherited from Y, is valid.
6. The example F(x)=x^2, q(x)=x correctly demonstrates local stability around x_0=1 on U=(1/2,3/2), while global exact identifiability fails.
7. At x_0=0, the stated local Lipschitz estimate fails.
8. The distinction between local stability and global exact identifiability is correctly stated.
9. D-1 and D-2 were independently compared with v0.3 and found mathematically unchanged.
