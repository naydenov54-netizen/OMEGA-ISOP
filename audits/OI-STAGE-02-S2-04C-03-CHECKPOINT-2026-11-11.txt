2. SOURCE FACTS

Volume II defines a local configuration as:
C = (S_C,#_C)
S_C subset U0
#_C = # restricted to S_C x S_C
|S_C| >= 2 for a minimal nontrivial configuration.

It introduces C_METACORE as a category whose objects are admissible configurations and whose morphisms preserve the selected structural properties.

For configurations C1=(S1,#1) and C2=(S2,#2), an embedding
f:C1 -> C2
is injective and satisfies:
x #1 y iff f(x) #2 f(y).

The biconditional is essential: preservation alone does not establish reflection.

Volume II defines:
CC(C1,C2)=1 iff an admissible amalgamation exists.

It further states that D_CC is the diagram generated by selected configurations and admissible compatibility relations, and that R := colim(D_CC) is conditional on a valid compatibility diagram, the relevant categorical structure, and existence of the required colimit or an equivalent constraint-preserving construction.

3. SOURCE-LEVEL FINDINGS

FINDING 01:

FINDING 02:

FINDING 03:

FINDING 04:
The source does not impose a global automorphism extension condition on embeddings or morphisms.

FINDING 05:
The phrase "morphisms preserve the selected structural properties" does not fully specify the morphism class or the selected property set.

FINDING 06:

FINDING 07:
The categorical completeness of D_CC, including its objects, arrows, composition, functoriality, and candidate universal property, remains unresolved at project level.

4. STATUS OF TEST CATEGORIES

C_U0-INC:
Literal-inclusion category on admissible induced substructures.
Amalgamation was established for the stated union construction, subject to object closure and admissibility conditions.

C_U0-AUT:
Category whose morphisms are restrictions of global automorphisms of (U0,~).
Amalgamation was established using M=U0, provided U0 is itself an admissible object of the category.

C_U0-EMB:
Category with all local injective embeddings preserving and reflecting the equivalence/apartness structure.
General fixed-carrier amalgamation is refuted by finite counterexample FC-01.

None of these test categories is established by the source as the intended C_METACORE.

5. DEPENDENCY AND LIMITATIONS

The choice of morphism class is mathematically consequential. It affects the category, the admissible diagrams, amalgamation, and the possible colimit construction for R.

The positive amalgamation results in C_U0-INC and C_U0-AUT cannot be transferred to C_METACORE without a source-grounded identification of their morphism classes and object classes.

The negative result for C_U0-EMB cannot be treated as a contradiction in OMEGA-ISOP because the source has not established that all local embeddings are precisely the intended C_METACORE morphisms.

No category may be selected merely because it yields a desired positive theorem.
