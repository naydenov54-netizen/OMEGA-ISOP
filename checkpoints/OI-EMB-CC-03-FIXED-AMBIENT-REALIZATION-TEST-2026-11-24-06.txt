0. DECISION
======================================================================

For each individual realization/test of C_METACORE, use one fixed
ambient apartness structure (U0,#).

This is the most directly source-supported working interpretation:

C=(S_C,#_C), S_C subset U0, and #_C the restriction of the ambient #.

This does NOT assert that OMEGA-ISOP permits only one realization
globally. Different realizations may use different ambient pairs.
They must not be mixed within one fixed-realization test.

This is a working interpretation for the present EMB_CC-03 test,
not a new canonical axiom or a permanent architecture amendment.

1. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE TWO-WITNESS TEST
======================================================================

The earlier A1/A2 pair varied the apartness relation on the apex pair
(c,d). It therefore compared two different ambient apartness
realizations, not two apex objects inside one fixed (U0,#).

Accordingly, the earlier result:
- remains valid in the abstract category ranging over apartness
  structures;
- does not establish two incomparable minimal witnesses inside one
  fixed ambient realization;
- must not be used as a canonical C_METACORE non-uniqueness theorem.

For a fixed ambient pair (U0,#), the value of c#d is already fixed.
A1 and A2 cannot both be realized on the same labelled four-point
subset with the same restricted ambient relation.

======================================================================
2. FIXED-AMBIENT SPAN TEST
======================================================================

Let (U0,#) be fixed.

Let C0,C1,C2 be admissible local configurations:
Ci=(Si,# restricted to Si),
with Si subset U0 and |Si|>=2.

Let the span maps be embeddings:
i1:C0->C1
i2:C0->C2.

The embedding contract is:
x#0y iff i_k(x)#_k i_k(y).

For a compatible aligned span in the subconfiguration/inclusion case,
S0 subset S1 and S0 subset S2, define:
S_A := S1 union S2
#_A := # restricted to S_A x S_A
A := (S_A,#_A).

Then the inclusion maps:
m1:C1->A
m2:C2->A
are embeddings, and:
m1 o i1 = m2 o i2.

This is a valid fixed-ambient amalgam IF AND ONLY IF A is an admissible
object of C_METACORE under the selected realization's admissibility
conditions.

The source text explicitly leaves exact admissibility
realization-dependent. Therefore closure under finite unions is NOT
assumed and is the central remaining existence obligation.

======================================================================
3. MINIMALITY / LEASTNESS BOUNDARY
======================================================================

If admissible configurations are closed under the relevant unions,
and morphisms in the tested subcategory are literal inclusions, then
A=(S1 union S2,# restricted) is the least amalgam under inclusion:
for any admissible B containing both S1 and S2,
S1 union S2 subset B.

This inclusion-minimality statement is conditional on:
1. the union being admissible;
2. the span being aligned by literal inclusions;
3. the tested order being inclusion, not arbitrary embedding
   factorization.

For arbitrary embedding spans, the union construction requires an
explicit alignment of the two images in U0.
For arbitrary morphism classes, leastness under factorization does
not follow merely from subset minimality.

No general least-amalgam theorem is claimed.
