2. FORMAL CLAIMS
======================================================================

C1. The filtering dependency
    R_global ->_F R_i ->_F A_i
    does not entail the existence of
    A_i ->_U R_global.

C2. The same filtering dependency does not entail the absence of
    A_i ->_U R_global.

C3. The filtering construction may be conditionally extended to
    S_global(t), provided A4 is satisfied.

======================================================================
3. DEFINITIONS
======================================================================

S_global = (M, Gamma, Theta)
D_i subset M
R_i in Restrict(R_global,D_i) intersect Compatible(Theta,D_i)

D1. X ->_F Y (filtering dependency): Y is constrained by X through
    Restrict intersect Compatible, or through the dependency
    A_i(R_i; Phi_i).

D2. X ->_U Y (update dependency): in the specified discrete-time model,
    Y(t+1) depends non-constantly on X(t). That is, there exist admissible
    x1 != x2 such that
    Y(t+1 | X(t)=x1) != Y(t+1 | X(t)=x2).
    Dependence may concern the whole state Y or an explicitly defined
    component of Y.

D3. X not-> _U Y := not (X ->_U Y), in the same model.

D4. P does_not_entail Q (P |-not Q): Q is not derivable from P, demonstrated
    by a model satisfying P and not-Q.

6. DIRECTIONAL RESULT: COUNTERMODELS K1 AND K0
======================================================================

Common setting (A4, discrete time):
    M = {a,b}
    D_1 = {a}
    R_global(t) = (x_t,y_t) in {0,1}^2
    Theta imposes no additional restriction
    R_1(t) = x_t
    A_1(t) = A_1(R_1(t); Phi_1) = R_1(t)

The filtering chain
    R_global(t) ->_F R_1(t) ->_F A_1(t)

holds at every t in both models.

K1 (with feedback):
    x_(t+1) = 1 - A_1(t)
    y_(t+1) = y_t

Therefore A_1(t) ->_U R_global(t+1), because the x-component depends
non-constantly on A_1(t). K1 is the countermodel for the claim that filtering
entails absence of reverse update-dependency.

K0 (without feedback):
    x_(t+1) = x_t
    y_(t+1) = y_t

Then R_global(t+1) does not depend on A_1(t), so
    A_1 not-> _U R_global.
K0 is the countermodel for the claim that filtering entails reverse
update-dependency.

Consequently:
    CE-V-06 does_not_entail (A_i ->_U R_global)
    CE-V-06 does_not_entail (A_i not-> _U R_global)

K1 and K0 share the same filtering chain but differ in reverse
update-dependency.

8. RESULT CLASSIFICATION
======================================================================

I. Directional boundary:
Global-to-local filtering entails neither universal absence nor universal
presence of local-to-global influence. Reverse feedback remains open. This is
a logical-consistency result established by K1/K0.

II. Temporal boundary:
Under A4, the filtering chain extends conditionally as
    S_global(t) ->_F R_i(t) ->_F A_i(t).
No universal global evolution law follows.

The directional and temporal claims have separate conclusions. The K0/K1
countermodels for the directional result are discrete-time models under A4.

11. RELATION TO THE OPEN CE-V-06 LOCAL-NONEMPTY-FIBER INVESTIGATION
======================================================================

This record supplies directional and temporal boundaries. It does not prove
local nonemptiness.

For the current local-fiber investigation, keep the working distinctions:
    X_i := Restrict(R_global,D_i)
    C_i := Compatible(Theta,D_i)
    F_i := X_i intersect C_i

The target condition is:
    F_i != empty

Equivalently, under the class-valued interpretation of Restrict and
Compatible:
    there exists R_i such that
        R_i in Restrict(R_global,D_i)
        AND
        R_i in Compatible(Theta,D_i)

A4 establishes temporal well-definedness of the relevant structures and
operations. A4 alone does not establish pointwise nonemptiness of F_i(t).
The supplied RL-022 record contains no explicit transition operator T_i
mapping local candidates from t to t+1 and no preservation theorem showing
that compatibility or admissibility is maintained through such a transition.

Therefore, neither pointwise nonemptiness nor temporal propagation of
nonemptiness is established by RL-022/RL-023. Any future propagation lemma
would require separately defined transition dynamics and a proved
successor-preservation condition. Such a lemma must not be attributed to
RL-022 unless separately established and recorded.
