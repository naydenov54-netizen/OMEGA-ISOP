SECTION 9. DYNAMIC LAYER

9.1 Relational Constraint
The relational specification
R
constrains the admissible dynamical space:
A(R;Φ) ⊆ Evol(X).
For fixed realization map Φ:
A(R) := A(R;Φ).
The relation
R → A(R) → realized dynamics
does not establish a unique dynamics.

9.2 Realized Dynamics
A realized dynamics is selected from the admissible class:
T ∈ A(R;Φ).
The selection may depend on additional structure, boundary conditions, parameters, environmental coupling or an independent selection principle.
Therefore:
R ⟹ unique T
is NOT DERIVED.

9.3 Feedback and Adaptation
The extended research architecture permits:
R
→ A(R)
→ T
→ feedback
→ adaptation
→ persistent relational changes
→ R'.
The transition
R → R'
is not derived from R alone.
A valid derivation requires an explicitly defined update mechanism or selection rule.

9.4 Dynamic Closure
A dynamic closure claim requires specification of:
state space,
transition rule,
admissibility conditions,
update mechanism,
relevant observables,
persistence criterion.
Without these, “dynamic closure” remains an open formalization problem.

SECTION 15. OPERATIONAL INFORMATION INTERPRETATION

15.1 Difference Is Not Information
Primitive difference is not automatically information.
Information requires additional structure connecting distinction to operational access.
A candidate information structure therefore requires, at minimum:
distinction
+ stability
+ operational access.

15.2 Information Is Operational
A distinction becomes operationally informative when it can be:
represented,
detected or accessed,
distinguished under a specified observation/intervention language,
related to a relevant response.
Thus:
Difference ≠ Information.
Representation ≠ Mechanism.
Observation ≠ Consciousness.

15.3 Information and Organization
Operational information may contribute to organization when distinctions participate in persistent and functionally relevant relational structures.
This does not establish a universal information-to-organization law.

SECTION 17. SELF-REFERENCE

17.1 Candidate Self-Referential Form
A candidate self-referential dynamics may be represented schematically by
D(x) = F(x,R_self(x)).
Here:
x = system state,
R_self(x) = a self-referential relational specification,
F = update or transformation rule.

17.2 Formal Status
The expression
D(x) = F(x,R_self(x))
is an incomplete formal definition unless the following are specified:
domain,
codomain,
construction of R_self,
admissibility conditions,
existence conditions,
update semantics.
Therefore the self-reference construction remains:
STATUS: CANDIDATE / INCOMPLETE FORMALIZATION.
It does not establish consciousness, subjectivity or agency.

SECTION 18. OBSERVERHOOD

18.1 Operational Observer
Observerhood is defined operationally.
A system may be treated as an observer when its structure permits specified observation, measurement, intervention or response relations.
Observerhood therefore concerns an operational relation:
system ↔ observable environment.

18.2 Non-Implication
Observerhood does not imply:
consciousness,
subjective experience,
intentionality,
free will,
human-like cognition.
These remain separate questions requiring independent criteria.

SECTION 52. AI AND COMPUTATIONAL ORGANIZATION

Artificial or computational systems may be evaluated through measurable operational criteria including:
