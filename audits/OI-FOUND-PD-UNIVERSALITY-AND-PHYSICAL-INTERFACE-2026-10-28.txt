1. PURPOSE

Test whether the current weak reading of Primitive Difference (PD-A), as a meta-level admissibility condition for formal distinction, entails a nontrivial carrier and apartness relation. Then state what must be supplied before a formal construction can count as a physical model.

2. CONSEQUENCE CRITERION

For a theory T_A representing PD-A, a claim Q is a logical consequence only if every model satisfying T_A also satisfies Q. Thus derivation of a nontrivial carrier requires T_A |= |U0| >= 2, and derivation of an apartness witness requires T_A |= exists x,y in U0 such that x#y.

This criterion is relative to the formalization of T_A. It does not settle stronger possible interpretations of PD that have not been specified.

3. COUNTERMODEL TO NONTRIVIALITY

Consider U0 = {u} and # = empty relation. This model has no x,y such that x#y. It remains compatible with PD-A insofar as PD-A only permits formal distinction and does not assert an object-level witness.

Therefore, under the weak formalization:
  T_A does not entail |U0| >= 2.
  T_A does not entail existence of x,y in U0 with x#y.
  T_A does not entail existence or the axioms of # unless these are supplied elsewhere.

The conclusion is NOT DERIVED, not impossible and not a contradiction. The argument is a countermodel relative to the stated weak semantics.

4. DEPENDENCE ON FORMALIZATION

If Frozen Core contains stronger formal content that entails an object-level distinction, that content must be quoted/formalized and the consequence test rerun. Neither silently weakening PD nor silently strengthening it is acceptable. This audit does not amend Frozen Core.

5. MINIMUM PHYSICAL-REALIZATION INTERFACE

A formal carrier and an abstract relation are not yet a physical theory. A physical realization must specify at least:

P1. Physical state space X and the interpretation of its elements.
P2. A realization map Phi from formal structures/states into physical states or configurations, with its domain and codomain explicit.
P3. Operationally defined observables O_i, including measurement procedures and units where applicable.
P4. A dynamics or transition law T_t (or another precisely defined evolution rule), including its domain, assumptions, and well-posedness conditions.
P5. A mapping from formal predictions to measurable quantities, including uncertainty/tolerance and comparison rules.
P6. At least one quantitative prediction that differs from a relevant alternative model.
P7. A failure condition: an observation or measurement range that would count against the model.
P8. A statement of which ingredients are derived from the previous level and which are independent physical inputs.

These are interface requirements, not claims that such ingredients can already be derived from PD-A. They are not sufficient by themselves to guarantee a successful physical theory; they are minimum bookkeeping needed to distinguish formal construction from empirical physics.
