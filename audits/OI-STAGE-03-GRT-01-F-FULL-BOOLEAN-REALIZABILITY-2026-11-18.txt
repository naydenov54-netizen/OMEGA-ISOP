2. FIVE ADDITIONAL MODELS

2.1 MODEL F-000: A1 FALSE, A2 FALSE, A3 FALSE

Define:

A1 fails:
f_i(-x)=-i*x+1, while -f_i(x)=-i*x-1.

A2 fails:
Starting from x=1, f_i(1)=i+1. Already at n=1:

Therefore the required uniform convergence to A={0} is impossible.

A3 fails:
Choose x_i=0 and y_i=1/i.
Then |x_i-y_i|=1/i->0, but:

Thus no common modulus of continuity exists.

Truth pattern:
A1=FALSE; A2=FALSE; A3=FALSE.

2.2 MODEL F-001: A1 FALSE, A2 FALSE, A3 TRUE

Uniform attraction to zero fails.

A3 holds:
For all i,x,y:

The family has common Lipschitz constant 1.

Truth pattern:
A1=FALSE; A2=FALSE; A3=TRUE.

2.3 MODEL F-010: A1 FALSE, A2 TRUE, A3 FALSE

Define the common base function:
h(x)=0.1*x^2/(1+x^2).
g(x)=0.5*x+h(x).

For each i define:
p_i(x)=max(1-2i*|x-10|,0).
f_i(x)=g(x)+p_i(x)-p_i(-x).

A1 fails:
The bump term p_i(x)-p_i(-x) is odd.
The function 0.5*x is odd.
The nonzero function h is even.
For x!=0:
f_i(-x)+f_i(x)=2h(x)>0.
Therefore f_i(-x) is not equal to -f_i(x).

A2 holds:
The derivative of h is:
h'(x)=0.2*x/(1+x^2)^2.
Its absolute value is bounded above by 0.065.
Thus g is globally Lipschitz with constant L=0.565.

Also g(0)=0 and g is increasing. On [0,1]:
0<=g(x)<=g(1)=0.55.

The supports of p_i(x) and p_i(-x) are localized near +10 and -10. Both vanish on [0,1]. Hence:
f_i(x)=g(x) for x in [0,1].

Starting from B={1}, every orbit remains in [0,1], and:
|f_i^n(1)|=|g^n(1)|<=0.565^n.
The estimate is independent of i and tends to zero. Therefore A2 holds.

A3 fails:
Take:
x_i=10.
y_i=10+1/(2i).

Then:
|x_i-y_i|=1/(2i)->0.
At these points, p_i(10)=1 and p_i(y_i)=0; the reflected bump terms vanish.
Therefore:
f_i(10)-f_i(y_i)=1 + g(10)-g(y_i).

Since g is globally Lipschitz with constant 0.565:
|g(10)-g(y_i)|<=0.565/(2i).
Consequently:
|f_i(10)-f_i(y_i)|>=1-0.565/(2i)>=0.7175
for every i>=1.

The input distance tends to zero while the output difference remains greater than 0.7. Equicontinuity fails.

Truth pattern:
A1=FALSE; A2=TRUE; A3=FALSE.
