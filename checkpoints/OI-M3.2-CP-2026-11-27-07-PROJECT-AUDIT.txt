==================================================

Theorem 91 states conditional non-expansion of operational response distance under H1-H4.
The algebraic proof is valid if H3 means ONE COMMON MAP tau:L_op->L_op for every model in the fixed comparison class and every operational probe.

1. The model-independence of tau should be explicit in the quantified statement.

The H3 response identity is the substantive compatibility premise. Theorem 91 does not derive H3 from the Frozen Core or from H1 alone.
