4. BROWN TRIANGLE: EXACT TRANSFER LIMIT
============================================================

Brown's obstruction is a theorem about a diagram of finite groups and injective group homomorphisms. Its no-finite-target proof uses the group multiplication structure, the universal direct limit, and the fact that the direct limit is an infinite simple group.

The OMEGA-ISOP local configuration source signature is:
C=(S_C,#_C).

The explicit apartness signature alone does not contain a group multiplication operation.

Consequently, there are two different possible translations, which must not be conflated:

A. FORGETFUL UNDERLYING-CARRIER TRANSLATION

If each Brown group is replaced merely by its underlying finite carrier with inequality apartness, and the group operation is forgotten, then the Brown group-theoretic obstruction is lost. As finite sets with injective maps, the diagram has ordinary finite common set completions. The infinite-simple-group argument no longer applies.

B. STRUCTURE-RETENTIVE GROUP TRANSLATION

To retain Brown's obstruction, the selected OMEGA-ISOP realization must encode the group multiplication (or an equivalent group structure) as part of the admissible configuration structure, and its morphisms must preserve that structure as well as the relevant embeddings. The selected category and Adm must admit the resulting six-object triangle and its six edge maps.

The inspected Frozen Core / Volume II / Volume III clauses do not specify such a group-enriched configuration signature or authorize its use as the canonical C_METACORE realization.

Therefore Brown cannot be transferred to canonical D_CC merely because its groups have finite underlying sets or because its maps are injective.

This strengthens, but does not change, the CP-15/CP-16 classification.
