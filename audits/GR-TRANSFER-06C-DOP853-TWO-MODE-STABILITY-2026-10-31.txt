4. NUMERICAL ASSESSMENT

- The Jacobian is empirically highly stable across the two integration modes: the maximum absolute entrywise difference is approximately 1.94e-13.
- The Jacobian condition number is effectively unchanged, approximately 8052.14.
- The residual vector is more sensitive to integration settings than the Jacobian.
- The infinity norm decreases from approximately 5.49e-12 to 3.81e-12 under the tighter settings.
- The maximum componentwise difference between residual vectors is approximately 1.68e-12. This is not negligible relative to the residual magnitudes themselves.

5. LIMITATIONS AND STATUS

This comparison supports numerical stability of the computed Jacobian across these two DOP853 settings. It does not establish:
- a rigorous upper bound on integration or rounding error;
- existence of an exact root;
- uniqueness of a root;
- interval-certified satisfaction of the transfer constraints;
- physical validity of OMEGA-ISOP.

No Newton correction was applied as part of this two-mode comparison. The original coefficient vector was not modified.

Recommended next step:
Run one third, moderately distinct DOP853 setting and compare all six residual components and the Jacobian entrywise. Do not alter the original coefficients and do not apply a Newton correction as part of that diagnostic comparison.
