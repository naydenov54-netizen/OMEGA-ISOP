============================================================
1. OBJECTIVE AND RELATION TO S3.21
============================================================

S3.21 established preservation for relationally guarded
universal Horn theories over function-free relational signatures.

The present task tests whether that result remains valid when
constraints couple several relation symbols in one clause, and
whether the guard requirement can be weakened from one atom
covering every variable to several atoms whose variables
collectively cover the clause.

The investigation has two distinct outcomes:

1. MULTI-RELATIONAL POSITIVE RESULT:
   S3.21 applies without change to clauses whose bodies contain
   several different relation symbols, provided one relational
   atom contains every variable of the clause.

2. COLLECTIVE-GUARDING NEGATIVE RESULT:
   It is not sufficient that the union of the variables occurring
   in several body atoms covers all variables. Those atoms may
   be supported on opposite sides of an amalgam and jointly
   activate a mixed trigger.

The positive result is an application of S3.21, not a new
independent general theorem. The negative example identifies
a precise boundary against a tempting weakening of its guard
condition.


============================================================
2. FORMAL SETUP
============================================================

Let Sigma be a finite function-free relational signature.
Every relation symbol has a fixed finite arity. Equality means
actual identity of elements.

Let A and B be Sigma-structures with a common induced
substructure C. Replace the embeddings by isomorphic copies so
that:

  A intersect B = C.

The free relational amalgam D has universe:

  |D| = |A| union |B|.

For each n-ary relation symbol R in Sigma:

  R_D = R_A union R_B.

No mixed tuple is added unless it already belongs to one side.

A positive universal Horn clause has the form:

  forall x1,...,xn:
    (A1 AND ... AND Ak) IMPLIES H

where each body atom Ai is either:
  - a relational atom; or
  - a positive equality.

The head H is either:
  - a relational atom;
  - a positive equality; or
  - BOTTOM.

Terms are variables only. There are no function symbols,
disequality premises, or negated relational premises.

A theory is finite in this checkpoint when it has finitely many
clauses over the finite signature Sigma. The preservation proof
below is clause-local and does not depend on finiteness.


============================================================
3. MULTI-RELATIONAL GUARDED FRAGMENT
============================================================

DEFINITION 3.1 - SINGLE-ATOM GLOBAL GUARD

A clause is globally relationally guarded if its body contains
one relational atom:

  G(t1,...,tm)

such that every variable occurring anywhere in the clause,
including its head, occurs among t1,...,tm.

The other body atoms may use any relation symbols in Sigma.
They may express joins, cross-relational dependencies, type
conditions, or additional positive equality conditions.

The guard relation G need not be one of the relation symbols
appearing in the head.

DEFINITION 3.2 - FINITE MULTI-RELATIONAL GUARDED HORN THEORY

A finite multi-relational guarded Horn theory is a finite set
of globally relationally guarded universal Horn clauses over
a finite function-free relational signature.

No assumption is made that the guard relation is total.
Consequently, a guard may make a clause vacuous on assignments
where the guard atom is false.

EXAMPLE:

  forall x,y,z,w:
    (G(x,y,z,w)
     AND R(x,y)
     AND S(y,z)
     AND P(z,w))
    IMPLIES T(x,w).

The clause couples four relation symbols in its body and a
fifth relation symbol in its head. G is a global guard because
it contains every variable x,y,z,w.


============================================================
4. MULTI-RELATIONAL PRESERVATION THEOREM
============================================================

THEOREM 4.1

Let A and B be models of a multi-relational guarded universal
Horn theory T, and let D be their free relational amalgam over
the common induced substructure C.

Then D satisfies T.

The conclusion permits relational, equality, and BOTTOM heads.

PROOF

Take an arbitrary clause of T and an arbitrary assignment s
of its variables into D.

If the body is false under s, the implication is satisfied.

Suppose the body is true. In particular, its global guard atom
is true:

  G_D(s(t1),...,s(tm)).

By the definition of free relational amalgamation:

  G_D = G_A union G_B.

Therefore the guard tuple belongs to G_A or G_B.

Assume it belongs to G_A. Since the guard contains every
variable of the clause, every value assigned by s belongs to A.
Thus s is an assignment entirely into A.

The induced-substructure condition implies that every
relational body atom true in D under s is true in A under s.
Every positive equality true in D is also true in A.

Hence the complete body is true in A. Since A satisfies the
clause, its head is true in A, or the body cannot be true if
the head is BOTTOM.

If the head is relational, its tuple remains in D because
R_A is a subset of R_D. If the head is equality, actual identity
is unchanged in D. The BOTTOM case is impossible because A
satisfies the clause and its body is true.

The argument is identical if the guard tuple belongs to B.

Thus the clause is satisfied under every assignment into D.
Since the clause was arbitrary, D satisfies T.

QED.

COROLLARY 4.2

The class of models of a finite multi-relational guarded
universal Horn theory has the free relational amalgamation
property for induced embeddings over induced common
substructures.

The amalgam is strong because its underlying set pushout
identifies only the common substructure.


============================================================
5. CROSS-RELATIONAL JOIN TEST
============================================================

Consider the clause:

  forall x,y,z:
    (R(x,y) AND S(y,z)) IMPLIES T(x,z).

This is a cross-relational dependency. No single body atom
contains all three variables.

Take:

  C = {c}

  A = {a,c}
  R_A = {(a,c)}
  S_A = empty
  T_A = empty

  B = {c,b}
  R_B = empty
  S_B = {(c,b)}
  T_B = empty

The induced structures agree on C.

A satisfies the clause because S_A is empty.
B satisfies the clause because R_B is empty.

Their free relational amalgam D contains:

  R_D(a,c)
  S_D(c,b)

but does not contain:

  T_D(a,b).

Therefore D violates the clause under:

  x=a, y=c, z=b.

RESULT:

The free relational amalgam of two models of this
cross-relational Horn clause need not be a model of the clause.

This is a mixed-trigger failure. The first premise is supplied
by A and the second by B.


============================================================
6. COLLECTIVE VARIABLE COVERAGE IS NOT A GUARD
============================================================

The body of the clause in Section 5 collectively contains all
variables:

  Var(R(x,y)) union Var(S(y,z)) = {x,y,z}.

Its body-variable incidence pattern is also connected through
the shared variable y.

Nevertheless, the clause fails under free amalgamation.

Therefore neither of the following conditions is sufficient:

  A. The union of variables occurring in body atoms contains
     every variable of the clause.

  B. The body-variable incidence pattern is connected.

The proof of Theorem 4.1 requires one true relational atom
whose tuple contains every variable. Several atoms can be
supported on different sides.

This counterexample does not prove that single-atom global
guarding is necessary for every theory or every span. It proves
only that collective variable coverage and connectedness,
without an additional side-coherence condition, are insufficient
as general preservation criteria.


============================================================
7. RESTORING PRESERVATION WITH A GLOBAL GUARD
============================================================

Extend the Section 5 signature by a ternary relation G and
consider:

  forall x,y,z:
    (G(x,y,z)
     AND R(x,y)
     AND S(y,z))
    IMPLIES T(x,z).

For any assignment that activates the body in the free
amalgam, G(x,y,z) must belong to G_A or G_B.

Because G contains all three variables, the assignment lies
entirely in that same side. The other body atoms are therefore
also interpreted within that side, and the side model satisfies
the clause.

If the assignment is genuinely mixed, no guard tuple can be
present in the free union, so the body is false.

Thus the guarded clause is preserved.

The guard is a sufficient syntactic device for excluding
mixed triggers. It is not a necessary condition for
preservation: a particular unguarded theory may still be
preserved for other reasons.


============================================================
8. PULLBACK TEST FOR MULTIPLE RELATION SYMBOLS
============================================================

Let pi:X -> Q be surjective.

For every n-ary relation symbol R, define its inverse-image
interpretation on X by:

  R_X(x1,...,xn) IFF
  R_Q(pi(x1),...,pi(xn)).

THEOREM 8.1

If Q satisfies a positive universal Horn theory whose clauses
have either a relational head or BOTTOM, then X satisfies the
inverse-image theory.

This remains valid when a clause contains arbitrarily many
different relation symbols in its body and positive equality
premises.

PROOF

Take a clause and an assignment s into X whose body is true.

Every relational body atom is true in Q under pi composed
with s, by the inverse-image definition.

Every positive equality premise remains true after applying
pi, because equal elements have equal images.

Thus the projected assignment satisfies the complete body
in Q.

If the head is BOTTOM, this contradicts Q satisfying the
clause. If the head is relational, it is true in Q and therefore
its inverse image is true in X.

Hence the clause is satisfied in X.

QED.

EQUALITY-HEAD LIMIT

If the head is an equality u=v, quotient satisfaction gives:

  pi(s(u)) = pi(s(v)).

It does not in general give:

  s(u) = s(v).

For pullback preservation of this clause, the exact
clause-specific fiber requirement is:

For every assignment s into X satisfying the pulled-back body,
if pi(s(u)) = pi(s(v)), then s(u) = s(v).

This condition is necessary and sufficient for the equality
head to hold for every body-satisfying assignment in X, given
that Q satisfies the clause.

Global injectivity of pi is sufficient but stronger than
necessary. The relevant requirement concerns only pairs
generated by assignments satisfying the particular clause body.

The multi-relational form of the body does not remove this
fiber obstruction.

10. LOOP AUDIT
============================================================

TARGET C1:

For every finite multi-relational globally guarded universal
Horn theory T, if A and B satisfy T, then their free relational
amalgam D satisfies T.

PREMISES AND DEFINITIONS:

1. D interprets each relation as the union of its side
   interpretations.
2. A and B satisfy T.
3. Each clause contains one relational guard atom.
4. Every variable of the complete clause occurs in that guard.
5. The common substructure is induced.
6. Equality is actual identity.

DEPENDENCY CHAIN:

True guard atom in D
-> guard tuple belongs to one side
-> all clause variables are assigned into that side
-> all body atoms are true in that side
-> side-model satisfaction gives the head
-> the head remains true in D.

POSSIBLE CONCLUSION SMUGGLING:

The conclusion is not part of the definition of a guard.
The proof derives side-locality from the free-union semantics
and the variable coverage condition.

COUNTERMODEL ATTEMPT:

The mixed R/S example in Section 5 is a counterexample when
the global guard assumption is removed. It does not satisfy
the hypotheses of C1 and therefore does not refute C1.

CLASSIFICATION:

NON-CIRCULAR DERIVATION within the stated fragment.

TARGET C2:

Inverse-image pullback preserves positive universal Horn
clauses with relational heads or BOTTOM.

INDEPENDENT PREMISES:

1. Every relation on X is defined as the inverse image of the
   corresponding relation on Q.
2. Positive equality is preserved by every map.
3. Q satisfies the clause.

COUNTERMODEL:

For equality heads, a non-injective map can identify distinct
elements. The functionality example in S3.21 remains a valid
counterexample to unrestricted equality-head pullback.

CLASSIFICATION:

NON-CIRCULAR DERIVATION for relational heads and BOTTOM.
EQUALITY-HEAD CASE CONDITIONAL on the exact fiber requirement.

NON-IMPLICATIONS:

1. The result does not classify all first-order theories.
2. Single-atom global guarding is not proved necessary.
3. Collective variable coverage is not sufficient.
4. Connectedness of the body-variable incidence pattern is
   not sufficient.
5. Free-union failure does not imply failure of every strong
   amalgam.
6. No operation symbols, function symbols, dynamics, Markov
   kernels, response maps, or physical realization structures
   are covered.
7. No physical law or cosmological consequence follows.

11. APPLICATION TO OMEGA-ISOP
============================================================

S3.21 already provides the general guarded-Horn preservation
argument over relational signatures.

S3.22 tests its multi-relational scope and identifies a
specific failed weakening of its guard condition.

For a proposed finite relational constraint layer over the
P2.3 quotient construction:

1. Specify the finite relational signature.
2. Write each constraint as an explicit universal Horn clause.
3. Identify whether one body atom contains every variable.
4. If it does, free-union preservation follows under the
   S3.21/S3.22 assumptions.
5. If it does not, test mixed assignments explicitly.
6. After quotient amalgamation, separately audit pullback.
7. For equality-generating heads, state the exact fiber
   requirement.
8. Do not infer preservation of operations, dynamics,
   realization, response, or persistence from this relational
   result.

This is a candidate finite syntax for a restricted relational
layer. It is not established that the actual OMEGA-ISOP
realization or dynamical structures satisfy these hypotheses.

No change to P2.3, P2.4, the Frozen Core, or Volume V is
proposed or enacted.

12. FINAL STATUS
============================================================

S3.22 ESTABLISHES:

1. Multi-relational guarded Horn preservation is a direct
   application of S3.21 and remains valid when body clauses
   couple multiple relation symbols.
2. The guard may be a relation symbol distinct from all
   relation symbols in the other body atoms and the head.
3. Collective variable coverage across multiple body atoms
   does not prevent mixed-trigger failures.
4. Connectedness of the body-variable incidence pattern does
   not prevent mixed-trigger failures.
5. The cross-relational join example gives an explicit
   countermodel to that proposed weakening.
6. Multi-relational inverse-image pullback is preserved for
   relational heads and BOTTOM.
7. Equality-head pullback retains the clause-specific fiber
   obstruction.
