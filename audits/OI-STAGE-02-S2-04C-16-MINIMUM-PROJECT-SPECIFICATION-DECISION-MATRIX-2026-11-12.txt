DECISION 1 — OBJECT ADMISSIBILITY

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- Configurations have the stated induced-apartness representation.
- A minimal nontriviality test is stated.
- Exact admissibility remains realization-dependent.

COHERENT CANDIDATE DEFINITIONS:
A1. Adm_min(C): the stated cardinality and apartness-witness conditions only.
A2. Adm_real(C): Adm_min(C) together with explicitly enumerated realization-dependent constraints.
A3. A separately defined admissibility predicate not reducible to A1 or A2, provided every condition is stated.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Specify one exact predicate Adm(C), including the domain of quantification and every additional condition. State whether the nontriviality test is necessary, sufficient, or only a test condition.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- Any category object class is undefined until Adm(C) is fixed.
- Closure under a proposed morphism or amalgam construction must be proved for this exact Adm.
- S2-04C-15 proves invariance of the minimal nontriviality test under apartness embeddings, not invariance of arbitrary Adm_real.

DECISION 2 — MORPHISM CLASS

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- The local apartness-embedding template is injective and preserves and reflects apartness.
- The source does not establish that every such embedding exhausts Hom_C_METACORE.
- Literal inclusion-only morphisms and global-automorphism restrictions are not source-established requirements.

COHERENT CANDIDATES:
M1. All local apartness embeddings between declared objects.
M2. Literal inclusions only.
M3. A restricted subclass:
    Hom_P(C,D)={f in Emb_#(C,D): P(f)}
    for an explicitly defined predicate P.
M4. Restrictions of global automorphisms of the selected ambient structure.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Define Hom_C_METACORE(C,D) exactly. If M3 is selected, state P(f) as a typed, testable predicate.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- S2-04C-15 proves identity and composition closure for all apartness embeddings, once an object class is fixed.
- For M3, prove P(id_C) for every object and prove P(f) and P(g) imply P(g o f) for every composable pair.
- For M2 and M4, category laws must be checked against the exact carrier and ambient conventions.
- Colimit conclusions from one candidate hom-class cannot be transferred to another.

DECISION 3 — ADDITIONAL STRUCTURE

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- Apartness is explicitly selected in the embedding formula.
- The category-level phrase "selected structural properties" is broader, but the complete selection is not enumerated in the inspected passages.

COHERENT CANDIDATE DEFINITIONS:
For each additional symbol Q in the structure signature, specify its type and whether morphisms must:
(a) preserve Q;
(b) reflect Q;
(c) preserve and reflect Q;
(d) preserve Q only under stated conditions; or
(e) leave Q outside the categorical structure.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Publish the complete categorical signature, including any additional relations, operations, constants, distinguished subsets, boundary data, or ambient data. Do not add any item merely because it simplifies a proof.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- The hom-set must contain only maps satisfying every selected preservation/reflection clause.
- Identity and composition closure must be rechecked for the full signature.
- Apartness-only invariance does not establish invariance of predicates depending on omitted structure.

DECISION 4 — CARRIER CONVENTION

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- Local configurations are represented as subsets of a selected U0 with induced structure.
- A local embedding is a map between local carriers and need not be literal inclusion.
- The inspected source does not establish that every amalgam carrier must be a subset of the same U0.

COHERENT CANDIDATES:
K1. Fixed-carrier: every object and amalgam is an induced subconfiguration of the same U0.
K2. Abstract-carrier: amalgams may use newly constructed carriers.
K3. Realization-mediated: abstract objects are allowed only when an explicit realization/embedding condition into an ambient structure is met.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
State which carriers are allowed for objects and amalgams, and whether the same convention applies to all stages of D_CC.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- An abstract pushout does not automatically yield a subset of U0.
- A fixed-carrier construction requires a separate realization/closure proof.
- Fixed-carrier representation does not by itself force morphisms to be inclusions.

DECISION 5 — COMPATIBILITY INPUT

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- The inspected source uses the binary notation CC(C1,C2).
- The source records an admissible-amalgamation existence condition but does not fully specify a common base or span maps.

COHERENT CANDIDATES:
CC_pair(C1,C2): there exists an admissible common target M with morphisms C1 -> M and C2 -> M. This is a joint-embedding/common-target condition.
CC_span(C0;C1,C2;f1,f2): for a specified span C0 --f1--> C1 and C0 --f2--> C2, there exists an admissible M and maps j1,j2 such that j1 o f1 = j2 o f2.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Choose pair-based or span-based input. If pair-based, specify how its witnesses generate the diagram. If span-based, make C0,f1,f2 part of the input or define an unambiguous construction of them.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- Pair-based common embeddability does not, by itself, identify an overlap or impose an agreement equation on a common base.
- Span-based amalgamation carries an explicit agreement equation.
- The two predicates are not interchangeable without an additional theorem relating their inputs and witnesses.

DECISION 6 — AMALGAM ADMISSIBILITY

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- The source requires an admissible amalgamation.
- The complete predicate for admissibility of the amalgam is not supplied in the inspected passages.

COHERENT CANDIDATE DEFINITION:
For a specified pair or span, an amalgam consists of:
(i) an object M satisfying the exact Adm(M);
(ii) morphisms j1:C1 -> M and j2:C2 -> M;
(iii) in the span case, j1 o f1 = j2 o f2;
(iv) compliance with the selected carrier convention and all selected structure.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Specify the exact target admissibility predicate and whether the amalgam maps must be embeddings, monomorphisms, or another precisely defined morphism class. Do not use these terms interchangeably without proof in the selected category.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- A common target is not automatically an admissible amalgam.
- An admissible amalgam is not automatically a pushout.
- A union or quotient construction requires proof that its result satisfies Adm and that its structure/maps meet the chosen morphism definition.

DECISION 7 — INDEXING CATEGORY AND DIAGRAM

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- A diagram must be a functor D:J -> C_METACORE.
- J and D must specify objects, arrows, identities, composition, and compatible object/arrow assignments.

COHERENT CANDIDATE:
For a single span, J_span may be the free category on objects j0,j1,j2 and generating arrows a:j0 -> j1 and b:j0 -> j2, with only identity arrows and those generators. D must map this data to the chosen span and preserve identities and composition.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Specify the actual J and D_CC required by the project. State whether D_CC is a single span, a family of spans, a compatibility-generated category, or another diagram. Define how compatibility witnesses and their maps become arrows.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- The single-span J_span is only a candidate test diagram.
- A binary compatibility predicate does not define a general indexing category.
- Functoriality requires D(id_j)=id_D(j) and D(beta o alpha)=D(beta) o D(alpha).

DECISION 8 — COLIMIT AND UNIVERSAL PROPERTY

SOURCE-ESTABLISHED:
- R := colim(D_CC) is conditional on existence.
- The source requires the relevant universal property.
- A common amalgam or a binary pushout example is not automatically the required project-level colimit.

COHERENT CANDIDATE DEFINITION:
Specify a cocone eta_j:D(j) -> R satisfying:
eta_k o D(alpha)=eta_j
for every arrow alpha:j -> k in J.

Then prove:
For every object X in C_METACORE and every compatible cocone gamma_j:D(j) -> X, there exists exactly one morphism u:R -> X such that:
u o eta_j = gamma_j
for every j in J.

PROJECT CHOICE REQUIRING APPROVAL:
Identify the intended diagram and candidate cocone, then prove the universal property in the exact selected category, quantifying over all objects and compatible cocones of that category.

CONDITIONAL CONSEQUENCES:
- The universal property depends on the exact hom-sets.
- A result in P_min, E_F, C_EQ-EMB, or C_U0-AUT is scoped to that test category.
- A category must not be selected solely because it makes the desired colimit exist.
