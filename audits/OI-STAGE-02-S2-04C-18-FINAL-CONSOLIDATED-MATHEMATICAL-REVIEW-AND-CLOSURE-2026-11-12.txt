F-01 — MINOR — ACCEPTED; SCOPE QUALIFICATION REQUIRED

Issue:
The FR-1 and FR-2 examples use objects without a nontriviality condition:
- FR-1 uses C={z}, with empty apartness relation.
- FR-2 uses A={u,v} and C={c}, both with empty apartness relation.

If D1 makes nontriviality a condition for being an object, these examples may not be objects of the selected category. Their conclusions cannot then be transferred automatically to that restricted category.

Required clarification:
(a) Add D1 to the dependency list for FR-1 and FR-2.
(b) State that these counterexamples are proved in the AP-object scope without a nontriviality requirement.
(c) If a future category selected under D1 excludes these objects, re-check FR-1 and FR-2 in that category. Do not claim that the existing examples settle the restricted case.

Effect:
No change to the results in their declared scope. Project-level applicability remains conditional.

F-02 — MINOR — ACCEPTED; INTERPRETATION QUALIFICATION REQUIRED

Issue:
The FR-4 corollary uses the terms “compatibility” and “amalgamation”, whose project-level meanings remain unresolved under D5/D6.

Required clarification:
Qualify the interpretation as follows:

“Under the interpretation that compatibility means agreement of the induced apartness relation on the overlap, and that amalgamation means existence of the corresponding union object in the fixed-carrier inclusion category, the compatibility condition is automatic and the amalgamation construction is given by union. This interpretation remains conditional on D5 and D6.”

Alternatively, retain the formal union-colimit theorem in FR-4 and move the compatibility/amalgamation statement to an explicitly labelled observation.

Effect:
No change to the formal union-colimit result. Only the interpretation is conditional.

F-03 — MINOR — ACCEPTED; EXPLICIT LEMMA REQUIRED

Issue:
FR-5 relies on upward closure of Adm. The property is used in the reasoning but should be stated as a named lemma rather than left implicit.

Required insertion:

LEMMA UP — UPWARD CLOSURE OF Adm

Let U0 be a fixed carrier. Let Adm be defined by:
(i) |C| >= 2;
(ii) there exist x,y in C such that x#y, where # is the induced relation.

If:
C in Adm and C subseteq C' subseteq U0,

then:
C' in Adm.

Proof:
Since C in Adm, |C| >= 2 and there exist x,y in C with x#y.
Since C subseteq C', we have |C'| >= |C| >= 2.
Also x,y belong to C'. The induced relation on C' retains the pair x#y.
Therefore C' satisfies both defining conditions of Adm, hence C' in Adm.

Use:
Cite Lemma UP explicitly in FR-4 and FR-5 wherever upward closure is invoked.

Effect:
No change to the conclusion. This makes an existing dependency explicit and closes the exposition gap.

4. FR-BY-FR MATHEMATICAL DISPOSITION
==================================================

FR-1:
The contradiction argument using W1 and W2 is valid in the declared scope, provided the specified structures satisfy A1-A3 and the cocone maps are morphisms in the category under consideration.

The argument uses preservation of # by i, u1, and u2, cotransitivity A3 in P, the universal property, and the stipulated missing cross-relations. It does not require injectivity of the canonical maps i and j.

The existing FR-1 erratum correctly replaces “injections i,j” with “canonical morphisms i,j”. The inclusion maps into W1 and W2 must not be confused with the hypothetical canonical maps into P.

FR-2:
The no-cocone argument follows from preservation of # and irreflexivity A1. Its transfer to other morphism classes is correctly restricted: the span is not a span in the strong-map class when f is not strong, and is not a span in the injective-map class when g is not injective.

FR-3:
In a thin category, cocones correspond to upper bounds and universal cocones to least upper bounds. A colimit is therefore a supremum when it exists. Uniqueness is up to equivalence in a preorder and equality in a partial order. The empty-diagram case corresponds to a least element.

FR-4:
For a fixed carrier U0, induced relation, and the stated Adm, the union of a nonempty small diagram of admissible subobjects is admissible and is its least upper bound in the inclusion category. The no-initial-object example is valid. The compatibility/amalgamation interpretation is separately conditional on D5/D6.

FR-5:
The span-existence criterion is correct in the fixed-carrier inclusion category:
a span exists exactly when C1 intersect C2 is admissible.
The forward implication uses upward closure of Adm; the reverse implication uses the intersection itself as the apex. State Lemma UP explicitly.

FR-6:
The two Set pushouts have different cardinalities and therefore are not isomorphic. This is a Set result and is not asserted to be a pushout result in the category of inclusions.

FR-6b:
The examples correctly illustrate Set pushouts with injective legs. They do not establish that the Set pushout is a pushout in a category whose morphisms are inclusions.

FR-7:
Under a fully faithful right adjoint i with left adjoint L, and existence of colim(iD) in C+, the object L(colim(iD)) is a colimit of D in C. The hom-set argument uses the adjunction, the universal property of the colimit, and full faithfulness. The conclusion is valid under the stated assumptions.
