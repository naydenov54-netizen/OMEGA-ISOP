1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This audit executes a separate external test realization to check whether invariance under symmetries of a causal spacetime structure can select a nonzero locally finite volume measure. It is not a derivation from the OMEGA-ISOP Frozen Core and does not fill in R_cosmo by fiat.

2. TEST REALIZATION
Let M=R^4 with standard Minkowski metric η and its chronological relation x≪y. Consider Borel measures on R^4. The test group is not assumed to be the entire automorphism group. It suffices to require invariance under the subgroup generated by:
(a) all spacetime translations T_a(x)=x+a; and
(b) all positive uniform dilations D_s(x)=s x for s>0.
Both translations and positive uniform dilations preserve the Minkowski chronological relation. The test therefore asks whether a measure can be invariant under this explicitly specified subgroup. No claim is made here that this subgroup exhausts every automorphism of the chronological structure.

3. THEOREM
There is no nonzero, locally finite Borel measure μ on R^4 that is invariant under every translation and every positive uniform dilation.

4. PROOF
Assume μ is a locally finite Borel measure invariant under all translations. Translation invariance means μ(A+a)=μ(A) for every Borel set A and every a∈R^4. By uniqueness of Haar measure on the locally compact group (R^4,+), every translation-invariant locally finite Borel measure is μ=c·λ^4 for a constant c≥0, where λ^4 is four-dimensional Lebesgue measure.
Now also assume invariance under every positive dilation D_s. For a Borel set A with finite, strictly positive Lebesgue measure, dilation invariance requires:
μ(D_s(A))=μ(A).
But μ=c·λ^4 and λ^4(D_s(A))=s^4 λ^4(A), so:
c s^4 λ^4(A)=c λ^4(A).
Choose s≠1. Since λ^4(A)>0, this implies c=0. Hence μ is the zero measure. Therefore no nonzero measure in the stated class is invariant under both all translations and all positive dilations. QED.

5. INTERPRETATION
The result is a genuine no-go theorem for this explicitly stated external model and measure class. It shows that full invariance under a symmetry subgroup containing both translations and scale dilations is incompatible with a nonzero locally finite Borel volume measure on Minkowski R^4.
This does not mean that Minkowski spacetime lacks its standard volume measure. Lebesgue/Lorentzian volume is translation-invariant and invariant under Lorentz transformations, but it scales by s^4 under a uniform dilation rather than remaining invariant. One must therefore either:
(a) restrict the invariance group so that scale dilations are not required;
(b) introduce extra scale-fixing or calibration data and accept that the full dilation symmetry is not measure-preserving; or
(c) change the target measure class or invariance requirement, with a separate proof of the resulting properties.
Any such choice is an additional modeling decision, not a consequence of causal relation alone.

6. LIMITS
The theorem depends on the explicit test realization M=R^4, the Borel sigma-algebra, local finiteness, translation invariance, and invariance under every positive uniform dilation. It is not a universal theorem about every relational structure, every measurable space, or every possible measure class.
It does not prove that every invariant measure fails to exist for a smaller automorphism group. For example, if the group is restricted to translations and Lorentz transformations, Lebesgue measure is an invariant locally finite Borel measure, unique up to a positive scalar among translation-invariant locally finite Borel measures. That remaining scalar is not fixed by symmetry alone.
It does not prove that OMEGA-ISOP entails the Minkowski realization or its automorphism subgroup.
