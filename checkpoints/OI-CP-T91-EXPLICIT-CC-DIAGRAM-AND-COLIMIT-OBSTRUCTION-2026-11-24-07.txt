6. PUSHOUT OBSTRUCTION IN THE EMBEDDING CATEGORY
============================================================

The span D has no categorical pushout in APFin_nontriv_emb.

Proof by two cocones:

COCONE Q_SEP:
Take Q_SEP=A_STRONG with four distinct elements and inequality apartness.
The source inclusions form an embedding cocone.
If L were a pushout with structure maps j1:S1->L and j2:S2->L, the universal mediating embedding h_sep:L->Q_SEP would imply:
h_sep(j1(c))=c
and
h_sep(j2(d))=d.
Since h_sep is injective and c!=d, it follows:
j1(c)!=j2(d).

COCONE Q_IDENT:
Take Q_IDENT={a,b,c} with inequality apartness.
Define f1:S1->Q_IDENT as identity.
Define f2:S2->Q_IDENT by:
f2(a)=a;
f2(b)=b;
f2(d)=c.
Both are embeddings and agree on S0.

The pushout universal property would require an embedding h_ident:L->Q_IDENT satisfying:
h_ident(j1(c))=c
and
h_ident(j2(d))=c.
Hence:
h_ident(j1(c))=h_ident(j2(d)).
But the previous cocone forced j1(c)!=j2(d), so h_ident is not injective.
Contradiction.

Therefore no pushout exists for this span in APFin_nontriv_emb.

This proof rules out every possible pushout object in this category, not merely the previously proposed candidate.
