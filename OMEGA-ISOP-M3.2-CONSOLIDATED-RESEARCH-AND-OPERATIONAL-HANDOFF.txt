LOOP_AUDIT_PROTOCOL:
PURPOSE: Detect circular reasoning, hidden premise-conclusion equivalence, and conclusion-smuggling
  in definitions, assumptions, compatibility laws, operators, or intermediate constructions. L1
- STATE THE TARGET: Write the exact proposition C to be proved. Identify its logical form, domain,
  quantifiers, and conditions. Do not use an informal paraphrase as the target. L2
- EXPOSE THE DEPENDENCY CHAIN: List every definition, axiom, assumption, lemma, compatibility
  condition, operator property, and external result used by the proof. Trace the dependency chain of
  each item to its actual justification. L3
- TEST FOR CONCLUSION SMUGGLING: For every premise P and every defining clause, ask whether P is
  identical to C, logically implies C in the stated domain, or is equivalent to C after a change of
  notation or representation. Check whether an intermediate object was defined so that the desired
  conclusion is built into its definition. L4
- CHECK INDEPENDENCE: Any premise that carries the essential content of C must have an independent
  justification that does not rely on C or on an equivalent statement. If no such justification
  exists, the derivation is circular or incomplete. Renaming C, encoding it in an operator, or
  expressing it as covariance, invariance, equivariance, compatibility, or a response identity does
  not make it independent. L5
- COUNTERMODEL TEST: Attempt to construct a model in which all stated premises and definitions hold
  while C fails. If such a model exists, the premises do not prove C. Record the countermodel and
  the exact failed implication. If no countermodel is found, do not claim that none exists; record
  only that the attempted search did not find one. L6
- PREMISE-REMOVAL TEST: Remove or weaken each non-foundational premise in turn and determine whether
  the proof still works. This identifies which premise actually carries the conclusion. Do not
  remove a premise silently or claim a stronger theorem than the remaining assumptions support. L7
- CLASSIFY THE
RESULT: Use one of these statuses: NON-CIRCULAR DERIVATION (dependency audit passed within stated
  scope); CONDITIONAL RESULT (valid only under explicit assumptions, including any unproved bridge);
  CIRCULAR (the target or an equivalent is assumed or definitionally embedded); INCOMPLETE (a
  required premise or inference is unjustified); REFUTED (a countermodel or contradiction defeats
  the stated claim). A passed audit is not by itself proof of correctness. L8
- REPORT NON-IMPLICATIONS: State what the proof does not establish, including broader domains,
  uniqueness, necessity, universality, physical interpretation, or empirical validity where these do
  not follow.
STRICT_RULES:
- A definition may introduce notation or specify an object, but it cannot count as evidence that a
  substantive property follows unless that property is independently derived from the definition and
  other premises.
- A compatibility condition that directly encodes the target may support a conditional theorem, but
  cannot be presented as a derivation of that compatibility condition from the underlying
  architecture.
- If C is proved from an independently established premise P that already entails C, the conditional
  inference may be formally valid, but it does not establish C from a weaker foundational basis.
  State this limitation explicitly.
- A theorem with an explicit unproved assumption is not automatically circular; classify it as
  CONDITIONAL unless the assumption itself is circularly justified or the conclusion is otherwise
  smuggled in.
- Do not confuse a valid conditional theorem with proof that its assumptions hold in OMEGA-ISOP.
- Do not treat a change of symbols, operator notation, dual formulation, or equivalent response
  equation as an independent derivation.
- When a LOOP is found, identify the exact premise/definition and target relation, reject the
  claimed derivation as circular, and preserve only any valid conditional statement that remains
  after transparent restatement of assumptions.
- Do not alter Frozen Core definitions or proofs while conducting this audit. Any proposed protected
  change requires the existing change-control procedure.
